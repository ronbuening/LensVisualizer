# MTF Accuracy Plan

Open work to make simulated MTF agree with trusted software and with manufacturer charts, and the evidence behind
it. Closed stages are deleted from this file; when every stage is closed the file is deleted and its standing "do
not" items live in `agent_docs/decisions.md`. How the engine works today is in
`agent_docs/architecture/optics-engine.md` § Simulated MTF.

## Executive Summary

A reader asked how the MTF implementation was validated. An audit of 44 products (52 configurations, 19 with
digitized Sigma and Nikon chart values) found our curves systematically lower than published charts: mean signed
difference −0.063, mean absolute 0.073, with four drastic cases. A review against optiland (MIT) and the patents
found that **the MTF mathematics is sound; the drift comes from what the engine is fed and what it is compared
with.** The one approximation in the engine itself, the diffraction product, has been replaced.

1. **The core is correct.** Ray trace, geometric OTF, units, signs and sagittal/tangential naming agree with an
   independent d-line tracer and with optiland's conventions; the polychromatic combination is pinned by analytic
   tests only, since neither reference is polychromatic.
2. **Two of the four drastic cases were lens-data errors.** Two patents print an aspheric coefficient with the wrong
   sign (Nikon Z MC 105, Zeiss Batis 18); both are corrected. Nikkor Z 85mm f/1.8 S is an unresolved source
   contradiction (`agent_docs/sd-audit-queue.md` Section G), and Sigma 23mm f/1.4 DC DN (−0.23 mean; its first-order
   values match the patent) is undiagnosed. A screen flags 77 of 846 lenses (9 %) for the same class of
   self-inconsistency. *Stage 4.*
3. **The traced aperture still differs from the label on some lenses.** Queued in
   `agent_docs/sd-audit-queue.md`: Section H (stops still sized from a marketed f-number) and Section I (125 lenses
   that trace more than 3 % from their label on axis).
4. **Charts follow different conventions.** Nikon, Sony and Tamron charts exceed the diffraction limit, so they are
   geometric; Sigma, Zeiss and recent Canon include diffraction. *Stage 8.*
5. **A field slope remains**: slightly high on axis, low by about 0.02 / 0.06 at 10 / 30 lp/mm by 70 % field. It
   tracks clear apertures inferred from drawings and the single axial focus plane, which cannot be verified and are
   to be shown as uncertainty, not tuned. *Stage 8.*
6. **No real lens has been compared with another tool.** That check is an optional diagnostic (last section); no
   stage depends on it.

Where the comparison stands (19 digitized configurations, mean signed / mean absolute, from
`agent_docs/generated/mtf-chart-regression.generated.md`): −0.037 / 0.052 with diffraction for every maker and
−0.026 / 0.050 when each maker is compared in its own chart convention. About 0.03 remains unexplained after the
factors we tested: patent example versus production lens, unknown maker focus and spectrum, and a heuristic ±0.035
digitization allowance.

That report was generated on 2026-10-08, before the stop-lattice sampling, so these figures describe the earlier
engine until it is regenerated (candidate C12).

## Evidence

Frozen-engine reruns, an independent d-line tracer, the patent PDFs, re-digitized charts and exact Hopkins
integrals. Current numbers are regenerated by `reports/mtfChartRegression.report.ts`; the figures below are from the
investigation and are replaced by regenerated ones as stages land.

### Cannot be known — surface, never fit

Maker focus-plane policy and spectral weights (no maker documents them), and whether a patent example is the
production lens. A uniform 0.90 clear-aperture scale zeroes the mean outer-field bias but damages both lenses with
patent-listed apertures and is degenerate with method choice; it stays a diagnostic.

### optiland as reference

Only `ScalarFFTMTF` is externally validated (two OpticStudio points). optiland is monochromatic, has no focus
search, its stop does not clip unless given an aperture, its default aiming is paraxial (it misses the real pupil
centre by up to one pupil radius on wide angles), and default FFT sampling steps 19 lp/mm at f/1.4. It can validate
rays, spots, optical path and monochromatic diffraction MTF. It cannot validate dispersion, white-light weighting,
focus choice or stop sizing; those need analytic controls.

## Decisions

- **Wide open is the patent design f-number, set in data.** The readout shows f/1.46 with the marketed value as a
  secondary label.
- **Source errata are corrected with evidence**, under the standard in `agent_docs/lens-patent-audit.md`.
- **Zoom iris radii are derived per station by default in the runtime**, with an explicit fixed-iris opt-out.
- **optiland is an optional diagnostic, not a gate.** No stage's acceptance depends on it. If it is built, it runs
  on demand in a Python virtualenv outside `npm test`, and only its reference numbers are committed.
- **Digitized chart values are committed as numbers** with source URL and hash, never chart images.
- **A public validation article** closes the plan and quotes only regenerated numbers.

## Guard Rails

- Chart agreement is reported, never a test threshold. `npm test` asserts physics only.
- Nothing is tuned per lens. Conventions are per maker with a basis tag (documented, inferred, unknown).
- The 33 undigitized audit configurations stay an untouched holdout.
- Wording: "about 0.03 remains unexplained after the factors we tested"; "19 configurations from two makers,
  digitized with a heuristic ±0.035 allowance"; "the kernels are checked against exact Hopkins integrals; no real
  lens has been compared with another tool". Only if the optional cross-check has been run, the last becomes "rays
  and monochromatic diffraction MTF agree with optiland within stated tolerances given identical indices and
  apertures; dispersion, white-light weighting and focus choice are not covered".

## Open Stages

Order: 4, then 8, then 9. Each stage is one PR and quotes the chart report before and after.

### Stage 4 — Prescription self-consistency screen

- **Why.** A: a catalog screen trips a hard flag on 77 lenses and two on 20. The two corrected errata would have
  been caught by it; the existing image-plane census is paraxial and cannot see an asphere error.
- **Changes.** `sourceSummary` (source f, F-number, half-field, image height, back focus, total length, example
  locator, `scale`, per zoom station; transcribed, never computed). A pure
  `src/optics/analysis/prescriptionConsistency.ts`: EFL against the source; image plane against paraxial focus;
  spherical-aberration rim blow-up; stopped-down axial collapse; high-order distortion; field focus departing from
  h²; aspheres with every coefficient zero. `scripts/audit-prescription.mjs` with a report-only `--probe` that tries
  single-coefficient sign and exponent changes; a generated report; a cheap corpus sweep in
  `__tests__/src/lens-data/` as a shrink-only ratchet; rows in Section G of `agent_docs/sd-audit-queue.md`.
- **First suspects.** Sigma 23mm f/1.4 DC DN (the largest remaining chart residual), Fujifilm GF 80/1.7, Leica
  APO-Summicron-M 35, Pentax 645 35/3.5, Fujinon XF 23/1.4 R, Laowa 15/2, Tamron A03 28-200, Sony FE 20/1.8 G, and
  Schneider Super-Symmar XL 110 and Ricoh GR IIIx (aspheres whose coefficients are all zero).
- **Acceptance.** Reproduces 77 flagged / 20 multi-flag; `--probe` isolates each corrected erratum uniquely.

### Stage 8 — Surface what cannot be resolved

- **Why.** G, H, I: about 0.03 mean absolute survives every combination of the changes above.
- **Changes.** The maker's chart convention and its basis shown in the tab, with a button that sets the method and
  no automatic switch (the conventions table moves from `reports/data/` into `src/utils/catalog/`). A through-focus
  band whose width is fixed a priori from the traced f-number, re-projected from bundles already traced, as a
  display toggle. A lens-level `sdSource` (`source-table`, `figure-measured`, `heuristic`) with a non-blocking
  `estimated-apertures` note.
- **Not done.** No field-weighted default focus (four lenses supply 90 % of its gain); no clear-aperture scaling or
  band derived from chart fits; no per-maker default method; no spectrum change (band and node count are ruled in
  `agent_docs/decisions.md`, 2026-11-13).

### Stage 9 — Validation statement and close-out

- **Changes.** A site article quoting only regenerated report numbers with the wording above;
  `agent_docs/architecture/optics-engine.md` § Simulated MTF rewritten in place; this file deleted.

## Unscheduled Candidates

From a code and probe audit of the MTF path (six surveys, then an independent re-derivation of the numerical
claims). None is scheduled. Each needs the owner's go-ahead, and a change to engine results also needs the evidence
and approval `agent_docs/decisions.md` requires (2026-10-20). Delete a row when it ships; a rejected row moves to
`agent_docs/decisions.md` with its reason; an accepted feature row moves to `FEATURE_ADDITION_PLAN.md`. Effort:
S under a day, M one to three days, L longer.

### Accuracy

- **C8 — Stop-uniform weights are not the incident flux.** Since the stop lattice, a lit cell weighs 1 × bulk
  transmission; the row above that calls equal flux per cell exact describes the launch lattice. Weighting the
  same rays by projected launch area moves a Sigma 35mm f/1.4 Art corner by 0.008 (sagittal, 10 lp/mm, steady from
  128 to 512 cells) and three other lenses by 0.004 to 0.009; a direction-cosine measure moves it 0.019 but is
  rim-sensitive. Candidate: the stop-to-launch Jacobian as the cell weight, with the matching amplitude in the
  sheared estimator, after an independent irradiance control. It changes the Pupil Convention, which follows
  OpticStudio and CODE V. M to L.
- **C9 — Two aliased grids can agree.** Convergence compares successive grids, so a blur far coarser than the
  lattice can read the same wrong value on both: a 1.6 mm landing disc reads 1.0 at 10/30/50 lp/mm on 16 and 32
  cells. The default 0–100 lp/mm sweep catches that case, and it takes a blur of millimetres, so the exposure is
  soft-focus designs and far-off design planes (Pentax F 85mm Soft: a 20 lp/mm focus peak moved 9 mm between 64
  and 256 cells). Candidate: keep a field unconverged while adjacent landings differ by more than half a cycle at
  a judged frequency. Needed before any single-frequency focus option. M.
- **C10 — Estimator controls beyond sixth order.** An eighth-order control (3r⁸ waves, half cutoff) reads 0.0023
  low at every grid (five-point Boole; nine points 0.00004). A bowed stop-to-direction map (bow 0.2) reads 0.007
  high at half cutoff at every grid; real corners bow 0.008 (Sony FE 20mm f/1.8 G) to 0.049 (Sony FE 14mm f/1.8
  GM), where the same control gives 0.0005 or less. Add both as analytic tests with these bounds; no kernel change
  on this evidence. S.
- **C11 — Rim cells are all-or-nothing.** A cell counts when its centre lies in the stop. Area weights with traced
  boundary cells cut an analytic disc's error at 32 cells from 0.0044 to 0.0003; weights on the kept cells alone
  do nothing, and the sheared estimator needs its own treatment. The gain is speed (tolerance at a coarser grid),
  not accuracy at the 256 cap. L.

### Validation

- **C12 — Regenerate the chart report and stamp it.** `mtf-chart-regression.generated.md` predates the stop
  lattice, the unresolved-beam and dark-line rules and the 256 cap. Regenerate it, and print the engine commit and
  hashes of anchors, conventions and prescriptions so a stale copy says so. Required before Stage 9. S plus the
  run.
- **C16 — Midpoint comparison hides astigmatism errors.** The chart report compares pair midpoints; add the
  assignment-free `|S − T|` against `pair_max − pair_min` beside it, and record S, T and the chart aperture in any
  new digitization. M.
- **C18 — Stage 4 wording.** Its counts (77 of 846, 20 multi-flag) cannot be reproduced on today's 1,102 visible
  lenses, several first suspects already carry errata (Pentax 645 35mm, Fujifilm GF 80mm), and errata live at
  `source.errata`. Restate the acceptance against a fresh census. S.

### Data

- **C19 — Spectral eligibility.** 999 of 1,102 visible lenses get the photopic chart (280 with estimated
  dispersion) and 70 fall back to the reference line. 38 of those are blocked by a high-νd glass with no `dPgF`:
  59 surfaces, 0.46 % of the catalog's. 129 warned lenses estimate a single element. Extend the census to list
  every blocker and what each upgrade unlocks. L for the source work.
- **C20 — Finite conjugates.** 62 of 767 lenses with focus travel (8 %) have an eligible finite station; 504 of
  604 published finite stations lack `focus.conjugates`. Backfill from sources only, never from a close-focus
  label. L.
- **C21 — Gaps that reach a chart unnamed.** A spherical stand-in for unpublished aspheres (Schneider Super-Symmar
  XL 110mm) and reconstructed clear apertures (Sigma 23mm f/1.4) are stated only in file headers. Fold a declared
  missing-asphere warning into Stage 8's `sdSource` work. M, plus backfill.

### Features

- **C22 — Show the diffraction limit.** `MtfFieldResult.diffractionLimit` is computed and unused. Draw it as an
  opt-in S/T pair ("same traced pupil, no aberration"), add a field selector to the frequency view (it shows
  centre, 50 % and the outermost only) and pixel-pitch Nyquist markers. Never multiply a geometric curve by it. M.
- **C23 — A shareable MTF link.** Method, spectrum, focus, grid cap, field step, frequencies and the f/8 overlay
  live in localStorage, so a shared link can show different curves. "Copy MTF link" with versioned settings that
  do not overwrite the recipient's defaults. M.
- **C24 — Exports with context.** The CSV has no lens, aperture, station, method, spectrum or plane, and exports
  the selected frequencies even from the frequency view. Add current-view and all-values exports, the overlay's
  rows, and SVG then PNG with a caption. M.
- **C25 — Per-field diagnostics.** Grid, `maxDelta`, stable-through frequency, unresolved flux and notes exist per
  field; the table shows values, ray count and status, and notes lose their field. Attach them to the field, and
  show the focus scores. S.
- **C26 — Through-focus view and a focus offset.** `mtfFocus.ts` already re-lands traced rays on shifted flat and
  curved surfaces, off axis too. A through-focus plot per field and frequency, a user offset, and S/T peak
  positions as a field-curvature readout share that code with Stage 8's band. Depends on C9. L.
- **C27 — Comparisons beyond f/8.** Any aperture pair, MTF against aperture, and MTF against focal length over a
  zoom's published stations (369 zooms, 1,092 published stations), each stating its plane. M each.
- **C28 — Spectrum choices.** `MtfSpectralLine` already carries arbitrary lines. A chosen wavelength (S); R, G and
  B responses on one plane, a g-to-C band, an illustrative sensor-weighted daylight (M each); eligibility judged
  on the wavelengths requested.
- **C29 — Finite-station readouts.** Working f-number and traced magnification beside a finite chart, navigation
  to eligible stations, and an object-side lp/mm axis for macro lenses. M.
- **C30 — Refusals that say why.** One message covers 18 fisheyes, 7 mirror lenses (the same 7 are annular) and 7
  diffractive designs. Name the missing prerequisite (S). Unlock in stages, each behind its own control: an
  on-axis geometric fisheye path; geometric tracing of a diffractive order; mirror lenses last, which need optical
  path in the generalized tracer (the estimator reads an aberrated annulus 0.031 low, the optical-path kernel
  within 0.00003). L each.
- **C31 — Worker responsiveness.** The 30 ms budget is checked only between generator steps, and each step is a
  whole field and grid across every line; the f/8 overlay doubles the workers (four in comparison mode), each
  with its own 64 MiB cache. Phase-aware progress, yields inside long steps, and one page-wide budget before any
  pool. M.
- **C32 — RMS wavefront and Strehl per field.** Needs optical-path capture on the production bundles and the
  pairing fix of C13; a ratio of MTF to limit at one frequency is not Strehl. M to L.
- **C33 — Frequency-view legibility.** Fields differ by colour only there, and a line bridges fields still being
  computed. Add markers or end labels and a distinct style for the bridged span. S.

## Optional: External Cross-Check Tool (optiland)

A standalone diagnostic, outside the stage order. Build it when an outside reference is wanted, for example before
publishing a validation claim that names third-party software.

- **What it adds.** L: the analytic controls prove the kernels and two in-engine routes cross-check each other, but
  no real multi-element lens has been traced by other code in this repo. Rung-by-rung agreement with optiland would
  separate engine error from data error with an outside witness.
- **What it does not add.** Accuracy: every measured error in this plan was found and can be fixed without it. It is
  monochromatic, so it cannot check dispersion, the white-light sum, focus choice or stop sizing.
- **Changes.** `scripts/mtf-crossval-export.mjs` exports the prepared state (resolved gaps, rear plates, runtime
  stop, clear radii), per-wavelength fixed indices, our launch rays and an input hash. A Python script under
  `scripts/optiland/` builds each case with `IdealMaterial`, `RadialAperture` on every surface and the stop,
  `float_by_stop_size`, robust aiming, angle fields on +Y (mirrored), and never `image_solve` or the `.zmx` reader
  (it drops A18/A20). It injects our rays and runs `ScalarFFTMTF` with explicit sampling (512 rays, 1024 grid).
  Committed `reports/data/mtfOptilandReference.json`; a parity report that marks a case STALE on a hash mismatch.
  Only optiland's `DoubleGauss` sample enters `npm test`, as an inline synthetic prescription.
- **Acceptance.** Paraxial 1e-9; single rays 1e-8 mm; geometric MTF on identical rays 1e-7; optical path 2e-5
  waves; FFT MTF differences recorded against the current estimator. Ray rungs run at all five spectral lines.
- **Benchmark set.** canon-ef-135-f2l-usm, fujifilm-fujinon-gf-63mm-f28-r-wr, sigma-35mm-f14-dg-hsm-a, nikkor-z50f12,
  sony-fe-20mm-f18-g, sony-fe-400mm-f28-gm-oss, sigma-105mm-f28-dg-dn-macro-art, nikon-z-24-70f4s (both ends),
  nikon-z-mc-105f28, nikon-z-135f18-plena, sigma-45mm-f28-dg-dn-contemporary.
- **If built.** Add `agent_docs/gotchas.md` entries for optiland's defaults (paraxial aiming, non-clipping stop,
  inconsistent T/S labels in `ThroughFocusMTF` and `MTFvsField`).
