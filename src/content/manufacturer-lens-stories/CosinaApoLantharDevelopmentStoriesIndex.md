# Cosina: The Makers of the APO-LANTHAR 50mm F2

Cosina's 2019 [APO-LANTHAR development series](https://www.cosina.co.jp/category/apolanthar/) follows the same lens through optical design, mechanical design, product planning, and production. The introduction puts the project in the context of Cosina's revival of the Voigtländer brand in 1999. All five pages are in Japanese.
