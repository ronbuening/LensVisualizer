# Lens Stories from the Manufacturers

Nikon’s _NIKKOR — The Thousand and One Nights_ follows the development of lenses from early rangefinder designs through autofocus zooms. Other manufacturers tell their lens stories through developer interviews, factory reports, historical timelines, and optical design papers.

This guide gathers those first-party articles in one place. Each company page links to the original manufacturer article and, when the exact lens is available in the Surface & Stop catalog, to its interactive cross-section.

## What Qualifies

The index is intentionally narrower than a general photography reading list. Collections must be hosted by the manufacturer and concentrate on lens design, manufacture, or product history. Related standalone features appear on the corresponding company page. Press releases, specifications, reviews, ambassador galleries, and ordinary marketing pages are excluded.

The match standard for Surface & Stop links is equally strict. Each lens link identifies the generation discussed in the source. Where an article also explains a predecessor or comparison lens, its link is labeled accordingly. The ZEISS naming papers link to catalog examples of each design family.

This is a maintained research index, not an archive of the manufacturers' text. Links and catalog matches are periodically rechecked.
