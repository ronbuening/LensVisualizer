# Leica: Centennial Lens Stories

Leica's _Time Travel Through a Century_ was published for the company's 2025 centennial. The larger timeline places Leica products alongside a century of photography and world events; four of its chapters focus specifically on landmark lens families and the optical advances behind them.

The table names the first lens associated with each milestone where Leica's chapter discusses a family name rather than printing a complete model designation. Surface & Stop links are limited to the same historical generation, not a later lens carrying the same name.

The [complete Leica centennial timeline](https://timeline.leica-camera.com/en-US) also covers cameras, photographers, and the company's broader history.
