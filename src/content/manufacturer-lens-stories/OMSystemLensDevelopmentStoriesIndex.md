# OM System and Olympus: Lens Development Stories

OM System publishes interviews with the engineers behind its M.Zuiko lenses. Olympus also preserves designer Yoshihisa Maitani's lectures on the Pen, OM and XA cameras. The XA chapters explain why Maitani chose a short, fixed lens and how a sliding cover let him dispense with a separate lens cap.

## M.Zuiko Developer Interviews

The two-part 90mm Macro story follows the same lens from optical design through mechanical design and field use. Part 1 describes the design for 2× reproduction and compatibility with teleconverters. Part 2 explains the weight target and the stabilization needed to shoot handheld at high magnification.

## Olympus XA

In his [XA design lecture](https://www.olympus-global.com/technology/museum/lecture/vol2_05/), Maitani explains why the lens determined the depth of the camera and why he rejected a collapsible design. A [companion chapter](https://www.olympus-global.com/technology/museum/lecture/vol2_06/) describes the sliding cover that protects the fixed lens.
