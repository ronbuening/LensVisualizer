Added 19 historical lenses: 13 reconstructed from Moritz von Rohr's 1899 history of the photographic objective, from Petzval's 1840 portrait lens to Rudolph's Planar, and six from their 1890s patents: the Goerz Doppel-Anastigmat, Voigtländer Collinear Type II and two Steinheil Orthostigmats from German patents, Rudolph's Zeiss Anastigmat-Satzlinse from its Swiss patent and von Höegh's four-lens Goerz double objective from its British patent
