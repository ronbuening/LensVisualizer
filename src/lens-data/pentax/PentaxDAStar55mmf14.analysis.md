## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** US 2010/0073777 A1
**Inventors:** Jun Hirakawa
**Assignee:** Hoya Corporation
**Published:** March 25, 2010
**Example details:** Table 1, physical PDF page 12; Figures 1 and 2, page 2.

The association with the DA★ 55mm f/1.4 is probable, not factory-certified. The official Ricoh specification agrees on nine elements in eight groups, 28.6° full field and 0.173× maximum magnification. The patent gives a 56.36 mm design focal length. A product review rates the lens Very Good (8.2), with chromatic aberration, flare and slow autofocus caveats; this does not certify modeled MTF.

## Optical Architecture

A modified Gaussian design has nine spherical elements in eight groups. The central L5–L6 cemented pair combines negative and positive power. L4 is a weak plano-concave element immediately after the diaphragm, and L9 remains fixed during focusing. A camera-side cover is traced separately and not counted as a lens element.

## Glass Identification and Selection

All source nd/νd pairs are retained. These coordinates alone do not identify glass melts, suppliers or partial dispersion. Chromatic calculations are approximate. The source prints a 2 mm cover but no index or Abbe number. This reconstruction explicitly assumes nd=1.5168 and νd=64.2, without claiming source-printed glass. Polychromatic MTF is unavailable because the low-dispersion νd=68.3 element lacks partial-dispersion data; the guard is retained.

## Focus Mechanism

Source focus motion: first eight elements and iris advance 12.27 mm; rear element and sensor cover fixed. Endpoint object distance is a catalog approximation.

The source d15 changes from 1.00 to 13.27 mm. Surfaces 1–15 and the diaphragm translate objectward by 12.27 mm relative to fixed surfaces 16–19. The 0.45 m slider endpoint is only a product-based distance annotation, not a solved source conjugate.

## Aperture, Geometry and Validation

The source places the diaphragm 7.49 mm behind surface 6, leaving 2.19 mm to surface 7. Its 27.75 mm paraxial diameter is derived from the f/1.4 header, not printed. All clear rims are inferred. L4's flat rear face meets the deep concave front of L5 at a height of about 13.5 mm, so L4 is drawn square at 13.45 mm, just inside that contact, and the rims of L1, L6, L7 and L8 are raised only as far as the f/1.4 axial beam needs; the model then traces the full f/1.4 aperture.

The model's EFL is 56.357 mm against the patent's 56.36 mm.

The physical source image plane remains at the back of the cover (zero final air gap). Under the assumed cover material the paraxial offset is +0.001768165 mm. Reference-line diffraction MTF was computed at source/design and separately best-axial focus, at center, half and edge fields and 10/20/40 lp/mm, with a 64-grid ceiling. Grid convergence and support status are retained per field in the diagnostics; finite values alone are not a convergence or quality certification.
