The manufacturer lens brochure identifies the SMC Pentax-F 135 mm f/2.8 [IF] as eight elements in seven groups, with an 18° full angle, a 0.7 m minimum focus distance and an f/32 minimum aperture. The patent has the same construction and optical class, but prints a 9.4° half-field, or 18.8° full field. Its normalized prescription does not independently establish the dimensions or exact production revision of the retail lens. Uniform scaling to 135 mm is a correlation hypothesis, not a factory measurement.

## Optical Architecture

Eight spherical elements in seven air-separated groups form a telephoto with four source focus units. Unit 10 comprises L1–L4; unit 20 is the cemented L5/L6 doublet; unit 30 is L7; unit 40 is L8. The source describes unit 10 and unit 40 as fixed relative to one another while unit 20 and unit 30 move by different amounts. This model authors only their infinity positions. Surface 10 is a glass/glass interface, not an air gap.

## Focus Mechanism and Image Plane

Only infinity is supported here. The source publishes infinity and 1/4-magnification gap endpoints, but no physical object distance or continuous mechanical cam. Earlier calculated object conjugates and the two hypothetical iris attachments are research diagnostics, not certified factory states, and are not advertised in this model. The retail 0.7 m minimum focus does not establish them. The last image gap 46.38522359437118 mm and total image position 125.800323594371 mm are computed from the literal scaled infinity prescription. They are not printed patent BFL values. EFL is 135.006899408977 mm. The source near-endpoint gap sum differs by −0.001 normalized unit; no silent correction is made or finite state inferred from that rounding.

The canonical infinity-only control state keeps finite focusing unavailable. Neither a retail minimum-focus label nor a calculated conjugate is presented as source-certified motion.

## Stop and Clear-Aperture Assumptions

No source iris symbol, numeric stop or clear-aperture table is established. The retained stop is the previously frozen plane 75% through the infinity S8–S9 air gap, z = 30.7101375 mm from the first vertex. Its native f/2.8 radius is 14.101875566938 mm. Full-disk cap clearance is positive at the wide opening; smaller advertised openings use the same native physical iris rule. This is not a blade-assembly or barrel-mechanics proof. The manufacturer f/32 endpoint is represented by the aperture slider and quick selections.

The front and intermediate clear semi-diameters retain the original entrance-pupil/figure-based inference. A later full-figure audit found that the initial rear-element radius 9.77 mm was too small relative to the drawing. The existing conservative lower figure bracket sets both S14/S15 to 17.238 mm, versus the separately preserved 19.0944/20.6856 mm controls. It was nominated before contrast comparisons; this integration does not select apertures for favorable performance. These are schematic envelope hypotheses, not measured factory clear apertures. At the exact 9.4° source field, retained 208-ray checks transmit 122 rays under this branch; upstream and rear losses remain. The initial 9.77 mm clipping is not evidence of production-lens vignetting.

## Modeled Diffraction and Coverage

The displayed diffraction result is the native scalar sheared-pupil estimate from traced ray landings and the actual surviving pupil. Numerical sampling checks do not establish independent pupil-phase or factory validation. No measured-production MTF or absolute illumination is claimed.

The retained infinity checks use 0°/4.7°/9.4° and keep physical clipping. Finite attachment experiments do not certify a production iris mechanism and are excluded from the supported focus state. Normalized contrast can increase when a strongly aberrated portion of a pupil is clipped; that is not proof of better physical lens performance.
