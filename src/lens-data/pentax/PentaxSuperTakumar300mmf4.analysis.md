## Optical Architecture

All spherical, 5 elements in 5 components. A large front collecting group is separated from the small rear group by 111 mm of air. The stop subdivides this gap without changing its total length. The F=1 prescription is uniformly scaled by 300 to millimetres.

## Focus Mechanism

Static infinity geometry only. No focus travel or minimum focus distance is asserted. The patent does not print a numeric rear image distance. The model uses computed d-line paraxial BFD 74.838385816 mm and EFL 300.022065541 mm. Separately computed best-axial focus must remain labeled as an alternative diagnostic plane.

## Apertures and Validation

Stop position (82 mm behind S6) and physical surface apertures are secondary PhotonsToPhotos assumptions, not original patent measurements. The runtime derives the nominal f-number stop; its result is checked independently against the secondary stop diameter. Full-pupil center, mid-field and edge transmission and rendering checks accompany the package. No factory-accuracy or real-product MTF claim is made.

## Verification Summary

Primary R/d/nd/νd values match the archived source packet exactly, including combined air gaps across the added stop. Geometry validation passes with no override and zero hidden cap trim. Runtime nominal-f-number iris diameter is 30.39612 mm versus secondary 30.977 mm (−1.88%); it is a model calibration rather than literal adoption of the secondary iris.

Reference-wavelength diffraction MTF converges at 10, 20 and 40 lp/mm at the center, half-format and edge-format fields, on both the unchanged paraxial plane and separately labeled best-axial plane. These MTF field angles are format-based and are stored explicitly in the diagnostics; declared-field transmission uses 0°, 2.25°, 4.5°: 100%, 73.79%, 33.33%. This is pupil transmission through modeled apertures, not radiometric illumination or coating loss. No failed or unaimed samples occur. The strong edge vignetting is retained rather than hidden by enlarging source-derived apertures.

C/d/F MTF is unavailable because partial dispersion is missing; it is not a passed polychromatic gate. No browser UI verification is claimed.
