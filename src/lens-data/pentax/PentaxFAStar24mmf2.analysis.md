## Optical Architecture

The retrofocus arrangement contains eleven glass elements plus one optically distinct resin layer, in nine air-separated groups. Twelve material elements are therefore drawn while the catalog count remains eleven glass elements; the resin is not silently counted as glass. The source's front group has weak net divergence and the rear group converges. The first three glass elements form the front group. Two glass doublets and the resin/glass hybrid are retained explicitly.

The resin occupies source S14–S15, thickness 1.22 normalized units or 0.2928 mm at the declared scale. Source radii −114.137 and −88.794 remain distinct. A preliminary coordinator reference to 0.200 was a handoff error from another design and is not used.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The source explicitly defines its refractive indices at the d line. No catalog names, partial dispersions, C/F/g indices or vendor formulas have been invented. Abbe-only dispersion estimates cannot establish factory secondary-spectrum or APO performance. Reference-wavelength MTF is separate from any broadband capability.

## Focus Mechanism

The source moves its rear group toward the front: at the printed 1:10 state, d6 changes from 15.87 to 4.95 and BF from 153.12 to 164.04, leaving normalized track 441.09. Independent paraxial calculation gives magnification −0.0999035 at that literal image plane. This is not the maker's closest-focus state. Maker specifications of 0.3 m minimum and approximately 0.13 maximum magnification do not authorize relabeling the source 0.1 state as retail closest focus.

The runtime is deliberately infinity-only: no variable gaps, no interpolated cam law and no finite-conjugate validation. Its required close-focus scalar of 1e9 m is an unvalidated infinity sentinel, not a recovered near endpoint. Pinned-baseline precedents include KinoptikTegea57mmf18 (1e6) and PentaxM28mmf28 (1e15). Generic focus-slider or fallback labels must not be interpreted as validated source states.

## Aspherical Surfaces

Only source S14 is aspherical. The source equation uses 1 + K under the conic radical, with K = 0 and even powers A4, A6, A8 and A10. Source coefficients are A4 = −7.869e−7, A6 = −5.331e−11, A8 = +1.765e−14 and A10 = 0. Under length scale s = 0.24, each Ap becomes Ap × s^(1−p); K is unchanged. No conic-convention offset is applied. The front resin face and its distinct spherical glass junction are both retained.

### Reference-wavelength optical quality

The source-angle set at 0°, 21.05° and 42.1° converges through 40 lp/mm for geometric and diffraction methods at both the unshifted source plane and separately labeled best-axial diagnostic, with grid cap 128. The returned outer angle is 42.099995°, and its chief height is 97.6563% of the declared format-corner radius. This is not a format-corner test.

The separate 35mm-format-corner set reaches 42.723628°. At the unshifted source plane its outer geometric result converges only through 20 lp/mm (maximum delta 0.010297 at grid 128); diffraction converges only through 30 lp/mm (delta 0.010219). These bounded convergence limitations are retained, not solved by raising the grid cap or substituting refocus. Best-axial corner diagnostics converge through 40 lp/mm.

At the source image plane, reference diffraction MTF at 40 lp/mm is approximately 0.0584 on axis and 0.0298/0.1134 sagittal/tangential at the source 42.1° edge. Convergence certifies numerical sampling, not high optical performance. The separate best-axial shift is −0.057136 mm; it raises axial diffraction MTF at 40 lp/mm to 0.8636, but this is not applied to the authored plane. The plane's paraxial mismatch is only +0.000579 mm and is not flagged inconsistent. All completed field samples have zero failed and unaimed rays. Spectral material models are Abbe-only estimates, not factory broadband performance.
