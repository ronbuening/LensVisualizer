Two genuine printed sign conflicts require a qualified reconstruction. The table prints R3 = −117 and R9 = +18 in native millimetres. Claim 2 prints R3 = +1.1700F and R9 = −0.1800F. This model chooses the negative R3 from the table and negative R9 from the claim, consistent with the concave second element and negative fifth meniscus in Figure 1. These are not OCR repairs. The literal table gives EFL 30.37696764 mm, literal claim 39.37356304 mm, and selected combination 100.01711173 mm before scaling. Both source readings remain in the evidence packet.

## Optical Architecture

A five-element, five-group spherical telephoto layout combines a positive front collector with separated correcting elements and a negative rear meniscus. The large central separation compresses the overall optical length relative to the focal length.

## Aperture, Geometry and Validation

The diaphragm position (3 mm after surface 8) and clear diameters come from secondary assumptions, not the primary patent. The engine derives the iris opening from the f/4 header. The secondary 44.785 mm diameter is not a primary published dimension. No gap-sag override is used.

Fresh EFL: 300.051335191 mm. Every rendered element has zero hidden rim trim. Full-pupil sampled transmissions (32-grid, center / half / three-quarter / edge field) are 100.00% / 78.08% / 60.10% / 43.84%. These are geometric transmitted fractions, not exposure measurements or coating efficiency. No failed or unaimed rays occurred in those field samples.

The printed scaled rear gap remains 120.48 mm. The computed paraxial back focus is 120.530934465 mm; it does not replace the source plane. Reference-line diffraction MTF was computed at source/design and separately best-axial focus, at center, half and edge fields and 10/20/40 lp/mm, with a 64-grid ceiling. Grid convergence and support status are retained per field in the diagnostics; finite values alone are not a convergence or quality certification.
