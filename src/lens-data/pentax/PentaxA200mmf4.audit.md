# Audit Log — PENTAX SMC A 200mm f/4

Patent: US 4,550,987, Example 1 / claim 3

## 2026-11-01 — Deployment validation and cleanup

**Patent re-read.** Read from the USPTO scan (10 pages, no text layer): the Example 1 table on PDF p. 7 and its repeat in claim 3 on PDF pp. 8–9. Checked at ×2 against the data file: 12 radii, 12 spacings including fB, and 6 index/Abbe pairs. d8 × 2 = 21.28 mm = 2.128 + 19.152. There are no aspherical surfaces or variable gaps. Mismatches: none. The front page agrees with `source` on number, year, inventor and assignee. The patent prints no focus mechanism, close-focus data, stop position, clear apertures or spectral line for n.

**Patent vs model** (patent ×2 / model). EFL 200 / 200.03 mm; F-number 4.0 / 4.000; back focus 65.00 / last gap 65.00 mm, paraxial 65.03 mm; summed track 160.46 / 160.46 mm; half-field 6.3° / 6.3°. The printed f1, f1,2,3, f1,2,3,4 and |f5,6| compute as 0.730, 0.667, 0.506 and 0.406f, as printed. The iris is 12.97 mm under the paraxial stop rule (decisions.md, 2026-10-27). The real marginal ray through it enters at 24.42 mm, a working aperture of f/4.10; a real f/4 beam would need 13.29 mm at the stop. No lens surface clips the axial beam.

**Fig. 2 trace.** An exact trace with Coddington field curves, at infinity and the d-line on the f = 100 scale, compared with readings from the native-resolution scan (read / computed): spherical aberration at the zone −0.069 / −0.066, zero crossing about 94 % / 93 % of the aperture; sine condition −0.149 / −0.146; distortion at 6.3° +1.02 / +1.00 %; DS at 6.3° −0.033 / −0.033; DM at 6.3° −0.058 / −0.051 at the file's stop. This supports the infinity state and d-line indices. Distortion and DS barely move with the stop. DM at 6.3°, 5.0° and 3.9° bounds the stop to about 56–70 mm behind S1 at ×2, which contains the file's 67.81 mm and excludes mid-d8 (76.3 mm). With the catalog labels the paraxial g-line focus sits +0.116 from the d-line focus; Fig. 2 reads about +0.13.

**Maker diagram.** SMC PENTAX-A manual section, printed p. 38, PDF p. 41 (https://www.cameramanuals.org/lenses/pentax_smc_a_lenses.pdf). Axial positions in px along the drawing, against Example 1 ×2 at 4.36 px/mm: L1 front 44 / 44; L3 rear about 116 / 116; L4 318–333 / 317.6–330.4; stop tick 340 / 339.6; L5 front 421 / 423; L6 442–460 / 444–460. Outline widths give semi-diameters of about 26.4 mm at L1, 14.9 mm at L4, 10.1 mm at L5 and 13.2 mm at L6, against 27, 15.93, 12.15 and 13.5 mm in the model.

**Length.** S1 sits 115.0 mm from the K flange (160.46 − 45.46 mm), but lens-db lists a 111 mm barrel, so production may be a variant of Example 1. Example 2 ×2 gives 109.9 mm but is F/4.1. The section's proportions do not separate them: d8/d6 is 0.46 for Example 1 and 0.42 for Example 2. Examples 3 and 4 are near 0.2 and use a 1.49700/81.6 crown.

**Product.** A 200/4: 6 elements / 6 groups, f/32, 1.9 m, 52 mm filter, 111 mm, 405 g, 1983 (lens-db) or 1984 (Cine Lens Wiki). M 200/4: 6/5, 1977, 2 m. JP 53-27422 (Takahashi, Asahi Kogaku, priority 1976) has L5 and L6 cemented; this patent separates them, which matches the step from 6/5 to 6/6. The A* 200/2.8 ED (77 mm filter) does not fit, no F 200/4 was found, and no other corpus file carries this lens or patent. `PentaxA200mmf4MacroED` is US 4,666,260.

**Types and roles.** All six `type` strings agree with the signs of R. L1 is +71.812 / +1486.004, a meniscus by sign with a Coddington shape factor of 1.10, so the nearly plano rear in the figure and the maker's section is still a positive meniscus. The patent calls it only a "first positive lens". L2 and L5 are − / +, biconcave, as the maker's section also draws L5. L3 and L4 are + / +, the patent's "positive-meniscus lenses". L6 is + / −, equi-convex. L1's `role` said "Weak", which was not true: at +145.9 mm it is the strongest positive element of the four in the front group. The patent's words are "relatively small", against the prior art (col. 2). The role and one analysis sentence now say that. Stored `focalLengthMm` values were recomputed as in-air thick lenses and agree to 0.001 mm, and the inspector shows them.

**Groups and labels.** The patent has no G-style notation. Column 3 says the fifth and sixth lenses "correspond to a so-called rear lens group", column 2 speaks of the telephoto's "front lens group", and the conditions use f1,2,3,4 and f5,6. FRONT over surfaces 1–8 and REAR over 9–12 match that, with no change. The patent's "first" to "sixth lens" map to L1–L6 without a clash, and surface labels 1–12 are r1–r12. There are no cemented pairs, aspheric surfaces or `apd` tags, and none is called for: the patent names no glass and the maker's section marks none. All `glass` labels resolve, and the patent gives no further glass identification.

**Stop.** Fig. 1 has no stop mark. The mark in d6 and the one in d8 are dimension leaders that end on the axis. Three separate readings bear on the position. First, the maker's tick: stroke fits on the section give 4.345 px/mm over S1–S12 with residuals of 0.6 px or less, and the two tick halves at rows 341.77 and 339.97 meet the axis at 340.87, which is 68.07 mm behind S1 and 2.39 mm behind S8, against the file's 67.81 and 2.128 mm, a 1.1 px difference. Second, Fig. 1's L4: the f/4 beam is 14.13 mm high at S7, so the drawn 15.45 mm rim passes a full bundle at 6.12° only if the chief ray is within about 1.32 mm of the axis there. My own trace gives 0.84 mm at the file's stop, 1.35 mm at 70 mm behind S1 and 3.12 mm at mid-d8 (76.3 mm), so the drawn rim implies a stop no further back than about 70 mm. Third, Fig. 2: an independent Coddington trace gives DM −0.037, −0.047 and −0.051 at 3.9°, 5.0° and 6.3° at the file's stop, and −0.047, −0.056 and −0.044 at mid-d8. The plot reads about −0.035, −0.046 and −0.058, measured from the right-hand solid stroke, because Fig. 2 is sheared by about 16 px over its height. That favours 60 to 68 mm. All three contain the file's position, so it stays.

**Focus.** There is no zoom. The patent's only uses of the word are "focusing performance" (cols. 1 to 3), which mean image quality. It names no focusing group, direction or close-focus spacing. `focus.description` says the model is static, the slider's far end reads "Not modeled", and the movement overlay reports no modeled group movement. No focus table was added.

Limitations: the two Open lines in the validation section stand. The maker's rim widths read here 26.2, 25.9, 25.9, 14.9, 11.7 and 13.0 mm by outermost stroke centre, up to 0.25 mm above the first pass's reading of the same crop. That spread is the reading noise of the crop, and it was used as a check only.
