## Optical Architecture

The whole system is a retrofocus refractive prescription with nine spherical elements in eight air-separated groups and seventeen curved interfaces. A positive front meniscus precedes the diverging front elements, including the sole cemented L3/L4 pair. Thick positive L6 and the following negative/positive/positive rear elements complete the long-back-focus system. No camera cover, filter plate or asphere is added.

The source's 195.4-unit powered track becomes 46.896mm. R16 is literally +34680.0 in the source and +8323.2mm after scaling; it is not replaced by a plane. The unprinted final gap is a frozen independent paraxial construction, also scaled by 0.24. No source radius, spacing, index, aperture hypothesis or image plane is adjusted after exact tracing or contrast inspection. The inferred stop remains in air gap d9; source d10=43.2 is glass, becoming 10.368mm, and is never split.
