# PENTAX SMC ZOOM 85-210mm f/4.5 — audit

## Source

Yasuo Takahashi, *Variable Focal Length Lens System Suitable for 35 mm. Cameras*, assigned to Asahi Kogaku Kogyo Co., Ltd. Japanese priority September 14, 1966, US filing September 6, 1967, granted April 14, 1970. Table 1 is the sole numerical example. Claim 1 repeats the prescription. Figures 1(a–c) give the lens sections, Figures 2(a–c) the aberration curves, and Tables 2 and 3 the Seidel coefficients at the two ends of the zoom. [US 3,506,337](https://patents.google.com/patent/US3506337A/en)

## Transcription

Table 1 supplies 21 spherical radii, 20 axial spacings including the three variable air spaces, and eleven d-line refractive index and Abbe number pairs. The lengths are in millimetres. Surface 10 is the single cemented interface between L5 and L6. The example has no aspheres or plates.

The front and rear groups stay fixed during zooming. The wide and tele limits of d6 and d11 give the respective triplets of gaps (2.00, 68.50, 0.10) mm and (64.78, 5.72, 0.10) mm. The endpoint value of d13 follows from the constant span between the stationary groups. The body table prints an extra d2 = 1.50 above the first surface. Claim 1 starts at d1 and lists d2 once, establishing the intended sequence.

The glass names denote catalog equivalents. The largest absolute differences from the printed pairs are 0.00002 in nd for L3 and 0.11 in νd for L2. These matches do not identify the production glass supplier.

**Diaphragm.** The text places the stop between L9 and L10. Dividing the 15.00 mm gap into 8.00 mm behind R17 and 7.00 mm ahead of R18 reproduces the tabulated coma coefficients to about 0.001. The paraxial entrance pupil at f/4.5 gives a stop radius near 13.10 mm.

**Image plane and zoom motion.** The endpoint back focal distances bracket 111.740 mm. The 22 calculated intermediate positions hold the image at this distance and preserve d6 + d11 + d13 = 70.600 mm. Each position uses the desired effective focal length and paraxial focus to determine the moving gaps. Where exact focus calls for d13 above 7.41 mm, d13 stays at that limit and d6 sets the focal length.

With the residual defined as image distance minus paraxial back focal distance, the endpoint values are +0.0014 mm at wide and −0.0014 mm at tele. At calculated positions where d13 is below its limit, the absolute residual is under 0.0001 mm. At the three positions with d13 = 7.41 mm, it is −0.0050 mm at 135.71 mm, −0.0257 mm at 138.52 mm and −0.0012 mm at 142.28 mm. Including travel between positions, the greatest absolute residual is 0.0191 mm below the gap limit and 0.0260 mm at the limit near 138.9 mm.

## Product Identification

The association with the 1975 K-mount SMC Pentax Zoom is probable. A reproduced crop attributed to printed p. 25 of Asahi’s 1976–77 catalog matches the long-end section in outline, in the sequence of eleven elements with one cemented pair, and in the rear triplet’s two broad air spaces. The diagram gives no dimensions or glasses. Asahi specifies 85–210 mm, f/4.5, ten groups, 3.5 m minimum focus and a 29°–11° field. The patent gives 85–205 mm and describes zoom compensation at infinity. The 1971 S-M-C Takumar Zoom shares the nominal range and maximum aperture in screw mount. The f/3.5 K version has twelve elements in eleven groups. [Asahi K-mount catalog](https://hirokun.jp/pentax_lens_accessory_catalog_1976-77.pdf) · [Asahi Takumar manual](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/04347/04347.pdf)

## Corrections

### 2026-11-25

- The numerical prescription follows Table 1 at its printed scale. The duplicate d2 line above R1 is omitted as in Claim 1. The long endpoint follows the patent’s 205 mm designation. The production barrel is marked 210 mm.
- The text assigns Figure 1(b) to the intermediate position and 1(c) to tele. The drawn gaps put 1(b) at tele (d13 about 0.1 mm) and 1(c) near the middle (d13 about 7.2 mm). The optical spacings follow Table 1.
- Four entries in Table 2 disagree with its prescription. At R3 the Petzval contribution is about +0.320 (printed +0.310). At R9 the astigmatism term is +0.054 (printed −0.054). At R20 the Petzval contribution is +0.032 (printed +0.331). The astigmatism sum is +0.067 (printed +0.670). These discrepancies concern derived coefficients and require no change to a radius, thickness or glass value.

## Remaining Questions

The factory dimensions, glass types and intermediate cam positions remain undocumented. The catalog drawing supports the product identification, but its scale cannot resolve the difference between the 205 mm design and the 210 mm barrel marking. The clear radii are figure measurements, and the iris position is inferred from the Seidel coefficients. No finite-focus spacings or reproduction ratio are given for the 3.5 m production setting.
