## Patent Reference / Design Identification

**Patent:** [US 8,363,333 B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8363333B2/en)
**Inventor:** Tatsuyuki Onozaki
**Assignee:** Pentax Ricoh Imaging Company, Ltd.
**Granted:** 2013-01-29
**Example details:** Whole Numerical Embodiment 1, Tables 1–3 and Fig. 1; original physical pages 42–43, printed columns 7–10.

This is a maker-authored patent design, not an identified retail lens. All numerical source dimensions are retained at their published scale. No values are borrowed from other examples.

The 12-glass-element/10-group, life-size example is not the retail Pentax 645 90mm macro. The maker’s September 2012 release specifies 11 elements in 9 groups and maximum 0.5× magnification. The separate existing retail model uses US 2013/0222925 A1, Example 4.

## Optical Architecture

The source describes positive G1 (S1–S7), negative G2 (S8–S12), positive G3 (S14–S18) and negative G4 (S19–S24). S13 is a stationary air diaphragm. Twelve glass elements form ten air-separated groups. The explicit 0.200 mm resin layer S2–S3 is an additional modeled material region, not a thirteenth glass element.

No camera-side plate is prescribed or added. Model surface labels append A to the source asphere and map the source diaphragm to STO.

## Glass Identification / Selection

The source gives d-line indices and Abbe numbers, not catalog identifiers or a complete spectral dispersion law. No named glass, anomalous partial dispersion, apochromatic performance or measured broadband response is inferred. In particular, a high Abbe number alone does not determine secondary-spectrum behavior. Broadband calculations would depend on surrogate dispersion and have not been run.

## Focus Mechanism

G1 and G4 remain fixed. G2 moves imageward 15.053 mm and G3 moves objectward 27.125 mm. The source fixes the S13 diaphragm relative to the image. The four variable gaps D7/D12/D13/D18 change from 4.000/16.653/30.475/2.500 mm to 19.053/1.600/3.350/29.625 mm. BF stays 67.93 mm.

The near configuration has EFL 58.798751 mm versus printed 58.80 mm; its derived object distance before S1 is 69.996823 mm and magnification −0.9999955. Neither the derived 275.937823 mm object-to-image distance nor the source’s effective F/4.2 label establishes certified finite native performance. The 9.131511 mm collimated BF of this near configuration is not its finite image distance.

Only the tabulated endpoints are source-supported. Continuous interpolation is a viewer convention, not a recovered cam law. No focus motor, travel mechanism or minimum working distance is claimed.

## Aspherical Surfaces

S3 is the rear face of the bonded resin layer and uses the standard 1 + K conic convention. K = 0.000; A4 = 0.3350E−06, A6 = 0.1236E−10 and A8 = −0.2339E−12. Unlisted higher terms are zero. The polynomial has competing positive lower-order and negative eighth-order departures; its effect depends on radial height. The resin thickness remains exactly 0.200 mm.

## Verification Summary

At infinity, EFL is 87.798627 mm versus printed 87.80 mm; paraxial BF is 67.930463 mm versus printed 67.93 mm. The literal first-vertex/image track is 205.941 mm versus printed 205.94 mm. The terminal table gap is blank; the separately printed BF is applied rather than treating that blank as a zero image gap.

Both discrete endpoint configurations passed native construction, iris-in-air checks and zero rendered trim. The official infinity aperture census passed its 3% criterion. Geometric and diffraction reference MTF converged through 40 lp/mm at axis, half and full source image height, at the unchanged printed infinity image plane, using grids up to 128. Numerical convergence is not measured optical performance.

The initial conservative Pentax caps clipped S12 and gave f/3.14402 versus f/2.9. An explicit inferred square-rim hypothesis increases S10/S11/S12 semi-diameters from 17.824 to 19.387 mm, rounded up from the census ray requirement. Fig. 1 supports the square cemented-group outline, not a measured dimension. The corrected census gives f/2.90000. No source surface, material, gap or asphere value changed.

## Model Limitations

Rims and iris size are inferred geometry, not factory measurements. No gap-sag override is used. The reference calculation uses the runtime conventional d-line, 587.5618 nm. Finite MTF is unavailable because there is no source-certified object-distance convention. Finite aperture performance has not been qualified. Broadband, full integration gates and normal installation remain pending. The draft remains hidden until integration review; native checks alone are not a delivery qualification.
