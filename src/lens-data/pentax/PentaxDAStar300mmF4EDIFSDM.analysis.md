## Optical Architecture

The spherical telephoto arrangement contains eight elements in six air-separated construction groups: L1, L2, L3, the L4/L5 cemented pair D1, the L6/L7 cemented pair D2, and L8. Cemented interfaces are source S8 and S11. The patent's four functional groups and the six construction groups count different subdivisions and are compatible. No new per-group power calculation is asserted here.

The broad layout places the large front elements ahead of two separated cemented pairs and a rear meniscus. This is a descriptive interpretation of Figure 19 and the prescription, rather than a measured allocation of aberration correction. The patent identifies a negative third functional group that moves toward the image for close focus; the private input freezes the published infinity position.

Fourteen native refracting interfaces are supplemented by a flat air stop, giving fifteen native surfaces. The infinity stop lies 3.500 mm object-side of S10. Source d9 = 7.00 mm is represented by 3.5 mm from S9 to STO and 3.5 mm from STO to S10. No source refracting radius, glass thickness or total gap was changed.

The source's final table gap is a dash, retained as such in the source evidence. The separate printed BFD 56.93 mm supplies the native final image-space gap. It is not replaced with the computed paraxial BFD. Air `n = 1` is the native normalization of source air dashes. All source radii, thicknesses, indices and Abbe numbers remain unscaled.
