# BOOTH 300mm f/8 (US 1,156,743 Design) — Audit

## Source

[US 1,156,743 A, _Objective for Photographic and Like Purposes_](https://patents.google.com/patent/US1156743A/en), Lionel Barton Booth, filed January 25, 1915 and published October 12, 1915. No assignee is printed. The numerical source is Example 2 on specification p. 2, corresponding to Figure 2 on the drawing sheet. There are two numerical examples.

## Transcription

Two independent readings of the page images agree on all seven signed radii, six printed axial distances, four D-line indices and the cemented R2 interface. Every printed dimension is represented at 25 mm per source unit. The 35.175 mm first air space is divided by an inferred stop into 15 mm and 20.175 mm; the 1 mm rear air gap retains the printed proportion. No transcription mismatch was found.

The printed nD / nF / nG′ values are L1: 1.5899 / 1.5967 / 1.6022; L2: 1.6259 / 1.6381 / 1.6485; L3: 1.6129 / 1.6247 / 1.6348; L4: 1.6119 / 1.61965 / 1.62593. The patent identifies G′ as hydrogen Hγ. It is not the mercury g line. No C-line index, Abbe number or glass identity is printed, so tracing remains monochromatic at the D line, taken as sodium at 589.3 nm.

## First-order Properties

The patent describes a focal length of approximately twelve units and prints f/8. At the chosen scale, the nominal focal length is 300 mm and the computed EFL is 302.098689 mm, a +0.70% difference. No single image-supported correction has been identified. The approximate wording supports retaining the printed prescription; the historical cause of the residual is unconfirmed.

The computed paraxial back focal distance is 152.880940 mm, equal to the stored last gap. The powered-surface track is 59.4875 mm and the total first-surface-to-image distance is 212.368440 mm. The paraxial f-number is 8.000 with stop radius 14.444011 mm; the real-ray entrance-equivalent aperture is approximately f/8.16. The front cemented pair has focal length +107.52 mm; the individual element focal lengths in air are +46.74, −76.07, +65.00 and −35.49 mm.

## Values Not Printed

The patent supplies no stop station or diameter, clear semi-diameters, back focus, field angle, image format or close-focus data. The stop, rims and paraxial image plane are inferred. The ±7° display field is illustrative. The front rims are estimated at 22.5 mm and the rear rims at 19 mm; their provenance remains estimated. The air clearance between the R5 and R6 rims is approximately 0.1684 mm.

## Product Evidence

No documented production lens is tied to this prescription. The four-element, three-group telephoto has a positive cemented front pair and a negative rear assembly of air-spaced positive and negative singlets. Booth's later US 1,421,156 A is a different five-element, three-group design.

## Corrections

No radius, thickness, refractive index, stop station, clear semi-diameter or image-plane distance has been corrected. The approximate focal-length statement remains distinct from the computed result. The unprinted display image circle has been removed.

## Remaining Questions

Open: Figure 2 suggests front semi-heights of about 18.1 mm and rear semi-heights of about 12.4 mm, compared with the retained 22.5 and 19 mm. A previously evaluated 19 / 12.5 mm set passed axial f/8 but introduced approximately 15% corner vignetting at the illustrative 7° field. Those rims are not adopted because there is no stated field or numerical diameter and the present geometry has no clipping defect. Stop dimensions, historical glass identity, manufactured coverage and any product association remain unknown.
