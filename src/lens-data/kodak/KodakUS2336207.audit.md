# KODAK 100mm f/1.5 (US 2,336,207 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

[US 2,336,207 A, _Lens_](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2336207A/en), George H. Aklin, assigned to Eastman Kodak Company. The filing and priority date is 8 October 1941; publication was 7 December 1943. Example 1 appears beneath Figure 1 on the drawing sheet and in the upper table on specification page 2 (PDF page 3). Figures 2 and 3 give its rim-ray aberrations at 18.0° and 21.0°. Example 2 is a different numerical prescription.

The certificate of correction dated 15 February 1944 changes claim wording only, leaving the numerical prescription unchanged. No production source is documented for this design.

## Transcription Check

All twelve signed radii, eleven internal thicknesses and air separations, eight D-line indices, eight Abbe numbers and the printed 48.7 mm back focus agree with the page images. The drawing table and specification table agree. Dimensions retain the printed millimetre scale at nominal f = 100 mm. No transcription error was found.

The eight elements form four air-spaced components: 1 | 2+3 | 4+5+6 | 7+8. The cemented interfaces are R4, R7, R8 and R11. All refracting surfaces are spherical. The stop divides the printed 13.9 mm central gap into 9.35 mm after R5 and 4.55 mm before R6; that division is inferred.

## First-Order Check

The patent prints f = 100 mm, f/1.5 and BF = 48.7 mm. The retained prescription gives EFL 100.68 mm, 0.7% above nominal, paraxial BFD 49.10 mm, 0.40 mm above the printed value, and paraxial f/1.50. The physical stop radius is 20.114 mm.

The image plane is 48.7 mm behind R12, 0.40 mm ahead of the paraxial focal plane. The first-vertex-to-image distance is 140.30 mm. At that plane, the RMS disagreement with the Figures 2–3 rim-ray curves is about 0.020 mm, compared with 0.039 mm at paraxial focus. No single numerical correction is better supported than the printed prescription; the focal-length and back-focus differences remain unexplained.

At the retained stop opening, the real entrance-aperture and image-sine equivalents both round to f/1.51. An axial marginal ray at the nominal entrance-pupil radius would require a 20.289 mm stop radius.

The surfaces stay clear of each other and the iris sets the axial aperture. The stop plane clears the adjacent glass rims by about 1.17 mm and 0.49 mm. The 22.0° chief ray clears every optical rim and reaches image height 40.219 mm, corresponding to an inferred 80.44 mm image-circle diameter. This establishes chief-ray coverage; it does not establish full edge illumination or measured image quality.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The patent gives no stop position, iris diameter, element diameters or clear semi-diameters. The chief-ray zeros in Figures 2 and 3 support a stop 9.35–9.75 mm after R5; the retained position is 9.35 mm after R5. Its axial station is 45.25 mm from the first vertex, between R5 at 35.90 mm and R6 at 49.80 mm. The opening follows paraxial f/1.5.

The shared 31.6 mm clear radius at R3/R4 and rear clear radii of 27.2 mm on R10–R12 follow Figure 1's proportions. The other rims remain estimates consistent with the drawing and axial clearance. Local axial spacings disagree with the prescription, so the drawing supplies proportions rather than exact clear dimensions. The 22.0° half-field follows the patent's stated angular coverage. The image-circle diameter is twice the calculated chief-ray height at that field, rounded upward to 80.44 mm; it is not a printed diameter or a production camera format.

`focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. No close-focus distance or focusing motion is supplied. The selectable f-stops extend from f/1.5 to f/22, but the patent does not establish the minimum iris opening or a production stop range.

All printed glass coordinates and their D-line references are retained, with D taken as sodium at 589.3 nm. Elements 2, 3 and 5 use K-SSK4 (Sumita), PBM22 (Ohara) and BK1 (Sumita) spectral-proxy curves, respectively. Elements 1, 4, 6, 7 and 8 remain Unmatched with an Abbe-based dispersion estimate because no catalog row lies within index 0.002 and Abbe number 0.25 at sodium D. No historical glass supplier, code, melt identity, individual C/F/g index, partial dispersion or anomalous-dispersion property is established. No cover glass, filter, baffle dimensions or barrel geometry is supplied.

## Product Identification

No production lens is documented for this prescription. The patent describes sample objectives without naming a product. Its attribution to Eastman Kodak does not establish a production prescription. No marketed focal length or f-number, mount, image format, marketed minimum focus distance, diaphragm blade count or blade shape is assigned.
