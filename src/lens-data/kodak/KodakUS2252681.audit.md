# KODAK 100mm f/1.9 (US 2,252,681 Design) — Audit

## Source

US 2,252,681 A, High Aperture Objective, George H. Aklin, assigned to Eastman Kodak Company. Filed 17 May 1940; published 19 August 1941. The sole numerical example is printed at F = 100 mm and f/1.9 on PDF pages 1 and 3.

## Transcription

All 11 signed radii, 10 thicknesses and air spaces, seven N_D/ν pairs and the cemented interfaces at R4, R7 and R10 agree with both page-image tables. R9 is planar. R10 is +60.8 mm, despite the searchable text's 80.8 reading; the prose's approximate 0.61F supports the page images. No printed prescription value needs correction.

The third component's thickness is 0.116F, and each of the two rear doublets has an index increase of 0.055. The signed radii agree with the drawing's element shapes and left-to-right light path.

## First-order Properties

The prescription gives EFL 100.046850 mm against the stated 100 mm, paraxial f/1.9 and BFD 63.269633 mm. The 78.7 mm vertex track places the image 141.969633 mm behind the first vertex. The last spacing is computed paraxial back focus, not a printed mechanical dimension.

The inferred stop radius is 16.802368 mm. A real axial ray at the nominal entrance-pupil radius reaches the stop at 16.444454 mm; real-ray entrance and image-sine aperture equivalents at the modeled iris are about f/1.857 and f/1.863. Longitudinal spherical aberration is about 0.29 mm at the full pupil.

## Values Not Printed

The patent places the diaphragm between the inner menisci, without specifying its exact station or opening. The model's 9.0/8.8 mm split preserves the printed 17.8 mm air space. The stop clears adjacent glass rims by about 1.30 mm in front and 2.06 mm behind.

Clear semi-diameters, back focus, numerical field, image format, image circle, iris scale and close-focus data are absent. All rims are inferred; ±5° is an illustrative field. The fixed infinity model supplies no focusing movement.

## Product Relationship

The Kodak Ektra 50 mm f/1.9 Ektar is possibly related. Kodak's 1941 catalogue establishes seven elements, and later accounts report four groups, matching this patent's architecture and aperture. No source identifies the patent as its production prescription, and several related applications were filed concurrently. The product link remains uncertain; no Ektra mount, format or close-focus specification is transferred to the model. Sources are listed in the accompanying analysis.

## Corrections

The prescription already carries the correct R10 value. The searchable text's 80.8 reading is a transcription error, not a printed-source erratum. The drawing and repeated table support +60.8 mm.

## Remaining Questions

Open: The figure suggests R1/R2 near 32 mm, the square-cut II/III rim near 26 mm, R7/R8 near 23 mm and the rear doublet near 25 mm. R6's curved clear zone ends near 18 mm with a wider outer land. These observations do not justify changing the remaining inferred rims in this pass. Historical glass identities and a direct production-patent link remain unknown.
