## Scope and assumptions
D-line n and V retained; printed G′ 1.62448/1.63920 retained here as evidence only. Native dispersion approximation from D/V is not a G′ fit or measured modern C/d/F verification. Infinity-only reciprocal projection interpretation, computed paraxial image plane, no finite projection conjugate supplied. A central stop 9 mm after R4 is estimated, not printed. Per-surface semi-diameters [26,22.5,22.5,23,23,21,21,26] mm are conservative optical clear-rim estimates, not the mechanical contact rim. Reducing candidate rims from 23/22 mm avoids native gap-sag failures without changing any radii, thicknesses, spacings or adding an override. No gap allowance override used.
