# Audit: Kodak projection objective, US 1,620,339

The drawing and the printed table agree on the optical prescription, and the drawing and claims fix the signs of the radii. A rim check with 1,001 samples per adjacent surface pair over 0–22 mm found positive separations everywhere and a minimum glass edge thickness of 2.64 mm. A first-order calculation and a real-ray trace close to the axis agree on a back focus of 31.4082 mm. The published 31.3 mm was not fitted. Sampled rays passed 201 of 201 on axis, 9 of 9 at 5°, 5 of 9 at 10° and 0 of 9 at 15°; no image circle is inferred from these counts. Results for the D line and for the printed G′ indices are kept separately.

The model was delivered hidden (closed 2026-11-17: now visible). It runs from the front component to the film gate with the object at infinity, with an estimated stop 1 mm in front of the first surface and estimated 22 mm rims. It does not model projection to a screen at a finite distance and does not identify a production lens.

## 2026-11-18 — Semi-diameter pass against the patent figure

Source: US 1,620,339 A, drawing sheet (PDF page 1), the only figure. The scan is a 300 dpi bilevel image; it was
measured at 600 dpi below the axis, which carries no labels or leader lines. The drawn axis runs at y 3149 px under
the front pair and 3163 px under the rear meniscus (a 0.6° skew), and each component was measured from its own
local axis.

Scale: first vertex (R1, x 1506 px) to last vertex (R5, x 3208 px) is 1702 px for 118.9 mm, or 14.31 px/mm. The
spans disagree with the table: the air space S reads 1269 px (13.12 px/mm), the cemented pair 284 px (21.5 px/mm)
and the rear meniscus 149 px (18.6 px/mm), so the glass is drawn 28–46 % too thick against the air space. The weak
surfaces are also drawn far too curved: the R3 (601.9) and R5 (220.7) rim sags would need semi-diameters of about
110 mm and 45 mm. The drawing is schematic, so it was used for proportions only, under the rule for figures whose
axial spacings disagree with the table by more than about 10 %.

Proportions read from the drawing:

| Feature | Drawing | Model (sd 22) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rim height, cemented pair | 396–398 px | 22 |
| Rim height, rear meniscus | 388–390 px | 22 |
| Rear / front height ratio | 0.98 | 1.00 |
| Rim form | both components square-cut, L1 and L2 to one height | one value per component |
| L1 edge / centre thickness | 0.34 | 0.30 |
| L3 edge / centre thickness | 0.40 | 0.33 |

The edge ratios would put the rims near 21–21.5 mm, within 5 % of 22 mm. The vertex-span scale would put them near
27 mm, but at 27 mm the rear meniscus has a negative edge with this prescription (−0.8 mm; it reaches zero edge near
26 mm). The two readings disagree, so neither gives an absolute size. The arcs of the two strong surfaces alone
(R1, R4) imply about 24–26 mm, but the same drawing's weak arcs are several times too curved, so they are not used.
Result: no rim changed. The relative heights, the square-cut rims and the equal front and rear sizes already agree
with the drawing, and every value is inside measurement noise.

Clearance and light, identical before and after because no rim changed: the f/2.5 axial beam reaches 20.0, 19.65,
