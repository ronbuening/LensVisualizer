# Audit log — US 2,158,179 Figure 2 (Frederick and Aklin, Eastman Kodak Company)

## Transcription notes

- Duplicate check: the catalog had no earlier model of US 2,158,179. No product identity is inferred.
- The title page, the inventors and both prescription tables were read from the patent images. Figure 2 was chosen
  because Figure 1's drawing-sheet table prints its central air space as 1.78 mm, while the specification prints
  17.8 mm.
- Clear semi-diameters were estimated. Rims of 23 mm pass the surface validator's air-gap sag check. A first set of
  23.3 mm failed that check at the rear air gap and was rejected.
- Geometry checks on the delivered file:
  - no validation errors and no drawing trims over 0.25 mm;
  - the iris clears the nearest glass by 10.80 mm, in a 10.9 mm half-gap.
- Sample-ray pass counts, nine rays per field, with the original 23 mm rims: 0° 7/9, 5° 6/9, 10° 6/9, 30° 1/9, and the
  engine's 35.0° maximum 1/9. The central 90 % of the axial beam passed. The patent states no field angle.

## 2026-11-17 — Second review on the local site

**What was compared.** The live page at 1400×900 (infinity, the focus-movement overlay and the element inspector of all
four elements) was set beside Fig. 2 rendered at the scan's native 300 dpi (patent PDF p. 1). The drawing-sheet table,

**Scale, re-measured.** Vertex lines on the axis give 8.55 px/mm over R1–R4 (150.5 px for 17.6 mm), 8.49 px/mm over
R5–R8 (157 px for 18.5 mm) and 8.53 px/mm over R1–R8 (494 px for 57.9 mm). The front pair is drawn to the table within

4.15 mm, S3 about 1.5 mm against 2.45 mm, and R7 with about twice the sag of a 238 mm radius.

**Rims.** No change.

- Edge heights: the top and bottom edge lines are 452.5 px (front pair) and 453 px (rear pair) apart, 26.5 and 26.6 mm
  semi-diameter. The stored 26.5 mm on faces 1, 4, 5 and 8 and on the four lands agrees.
- Contacts: in the front pair the V above the contact closes at 24.8 mm, the prescription's 24.76 mm contact. In the rear
  pair it closes at about 25.4 mm, higher than the prescription allows (24.24 mm), because of the drawing errors above;
  the line centres meet near 23.5–24 mm, consistent with the earlier 23.9 mm reading. Below about 20 mm the two face
  lines separate in both pairs.

  corner 3.31 mm modelled, 3.17 mm drawn; 2′ front corner 2.85 mm modelled, 1.82 mm drawn; 4′ front corner 1.11 mm

**Lands.** The four `rimExtensionSd` 26.5 lands on faces 2, 3, 6 and 7 stay. They give the square-cut edge Fig. 2 draws,
