# SAMSUNG 18-200mm f/3.5-6.3 ED OIS — source and model audit

## 2026-11-06 — Package history

- Source: EP 2 469 319 A2, Example 2 (pp. 16–17), transcribed whole at scale 1: R, d, nd, νd, the three aspheres (K = 0, A4–A10), the stop at surface 16, the six variable gaps at the three printed stations and the 2.24 mm plate (1.51633/64.1) with its 0.60 mm image gap as a rear plate. No source value was tuned.
- Derived, not printed: every semi-diameter, read from FIG. 3 and then reduced where needed before tracing (S7 cap 9.7 → 9.4 mm with its 13.5 mm land kept; S12/S13 10.0 → 9.0 mm), and the per-station iris radii from the printed F-numbers.
- The package kept the US family member's L15 index (1.53123) as a separate, unshipped variant and modelled only the three infinity states, with a placeholder close-focus distance and duplicate focus rows. It did not use the printed L9 travel.
- All 18 glasses were left as Unmatched with their printed nd/νd.

## 2026-11-08 — Deployment validation and production cleanup

- Source re-read: pp. 16–17 rendered at 200 dpi and compared with the file: 32 lens surfaces and the stop (R, d, nd, νd), three aspheres × A4–A10 (all K = 0), six variable gaps × three stations, the plate and its 0.60 mm gap. No mismatches. ¶0202 prints L9's travel at an object distance of 0.5 m: 0.691 / 1.750 / 6.822 mm. ¶0145–0148 give the zoom motion, L9 moving toward the image for close focus and the L13–L14 doublet decentring for stabilization. Front page: application EP 11194965.7 filed 2011-12-21, published 2012-06-27, priority JP 2010-284858 (2010-12-21) and KR 20110123237 (2011-11-23), Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd., inventor Makoto Misaka.
- US family member: US 2012/0154913 A1 (granted as US 9,140,905 B2) prints η15 = 1.53123 for Example 2's L15. EP prints 1.58123, and so do EP Examples 1 and 3 for the corresponding element. With 1.53123 the EFL is 20.94 / 88.33 / 259.88 mm, the image falls 4.4 / 18.8 / 24.8 mm behind the printed image plane, and fRb/ft becomes 0.263 against Table 1's 0.227. The US value is a misprint; the EP value stays, and no errata entry is needed because the transcribed EP publication is consistent. The B2 text was not checked.
- Prose typo: ¶0192 and ¶0205 write the near distance as "0.5mm"; the ¶0202 heading and ¶0207 say 0.5 m.
- First order, model against patent: EFL 18.502 / 68.413 / 193.872 against 18.50 / 68.50 / 194.15 mm (within 0.14%). Infinity image within −0.003 / +0.027 / +0.008 mm of the printed plane. F/3.55 / 5.81 / 6.50 met by the derived iris (stop radius 6.58 / 6.72 / 7.31 mm). Total length 129.57 / 170.14 / 194.20 mm (not printed). - Close focus: the close rows are the printed D16 + travel and D18 − travel, matching L9 moving toward the image. Traced, they focus at 499.4 / 500.2 / 500.2 mm from object to image (369.8 / 330.1 / 306.0 mm from the front vertex), magnification 0.046 / 0.155 / 0.281×. The printed 0.5 m is therefore an object-to-image distance.
- Maker comparison: Samsung's NX 18-200mm F3.5-6.3 ED OIS (announced 2011-02-21, available May 2011) has 18 elements in 13 groups with three aspherical and two ED elements, 0.50 m minimum focus at 1:3.6, a 67 mm filter, F22, a voice-coil AF motor and OIS (Photozone review; Photography Bay launch report). Example 2 has the same counts, three aspheres on L9, L13 and L15 and two 1.49700/81.6 elements (L2, L10). Example 3 has the same construction except that the meniscus ahead of the stop is convex rather than concave toward the object; its numbers differ in detail. No Samsung construction drawing was found, so the two examples cannot be told apart and Example 2 is the representative. Example 1 (17/12) is excluded.
