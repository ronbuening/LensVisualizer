# SAMSUNG 85mm f/1.4 ED SSA

## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** [US 8,498,063 B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US8498063B2/en)\
**Inventors:** Shuji Yoneyama\
**Assignee:** Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.\
**Filed:** July 7, 2011 (Korean priority KR 10-2010-0088052, September 8, 2010)\
**Published:** July 30, 2013 (B2 grant)\
**Title:** Telephoto lens system\
**Example details:** First embodiment, Fig. 1 and Tables 1 and 7. Figs. 3–8 show aberrations at the three focus positions.

Samsung announced the 85 mm f/1.4 for the NX system in February 2011. Three details tie the first embodiment to the production lens.

1. Samsung's construction drawing shows ten elements in eight air-spaced groups, with the same two cemented pairs as Fig. 1. The first pair sits ahead of the stop and forms the internal focusing group shown in the patent. The second pair sits just behind the stop.
2. The drawing marks only the fourth element as ED and marks no aspherical surfaces. The patent describes an all-spherical design and gives the fourth element the lowest dispersion in the prescription (νd = 81.6).
3. The patent's 85.01 mm, f/1.44 design closely matches Samsung's 85 mm f/1.4 specification. The 14.24 mm image height in Fig. 3 corresponds to a diagonal field of about 19°, close to the specified 18.8°. The nearest patent position is 0.81 m from object to image at −0.1306×. Samsung specifies 0.82 m and a magnification of approximately 0.13×.

Embodiments 2 and 5 have eleven elements, and 3 and 6 have nine. Embodiment 4 has two ED-class elements. The first embodiment is the only one that matches the construction and ED count. The drawing establishes a close match in layout, while the production radii, spacings and glass types remain unpublished.

## Optical Architecture

This is a positive–negative–positive telephoto design. G1 (surfaces 1–8) is the fixed front group of four singlets, three positive and one negative. G2 (surfaces 9–11) is the moving −42.21 mm cemented doublet C1. The iris separates G2 from the fixed positive G3 (surfaces 13–19).

The +45.82 mm cemented doublet C2 forms G3a just behind the iris. Its net positive power gathers the beam before it enters the camera body. The positive–negative pair in G3b controls the exit pupil position. The patent gives the last negative lens much of the responsibility for the spherical aberration correction that a strongly concave surface beside the stop provides in a conventional double-Gauss.

The patent prints the radii, axial spacings, d-line indices and Abbe numbers, the stop position and the rear filter. The filter is 2.800 mm thick, followed by 0.522 mm of air to the image. The model retains these dimensions at the printed scale. The paraxial focal length at infinity is 85.014 mm, and the paraxial back focus is 0.524 mm behind the filter, or 21.817 mm in air from the last powered surface. The physical distance from the first vertex to the image plane is 109.871 mm.

Fig. 1 gives the rim proportions. The rim of surface 11 extends beyond the drawn arc to clear the axial beam. The 13.78 mm iris radius follows from the design aperture of f/1.44. Samsung lists a nine-blade circular diaphragm and a minimum aperture of f/22.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

Element labels follow the lens numbers in Fig. 1. The individual focal lengths are calculated in air.

### L111 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.61800, νd = 63.4. HOYA PCD4 equivalent. f = +89.48 mm.

The large front crown starts the convergence of the incoming beam. Its rear face is only weakly curved, so most of the refraction occurs at the front.

### L121 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.80420, νd = 46.5. HOYA TAF3 equivalent. f = +91.64 mm.

The second positive element shares the front group's convergence. Its rear face is separated from the negative meniscus L131 by a narrow air gap.

### L131 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.69895, νd = 30.1. HOYA E-FD15 equivalent. f = −44.77 mm.

The nearly flat front face leaves most of this element's negative power at its strongly concave rear. Its dispersive glass balances part of the chromatic power of the positive elements in G1.

### L141 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.49700, νd = 81.6. HOYA FCD1 equivalent, ED class. f = +75.76 mm.

The low-dispersion crown restores positive power after L131 and contributes comparatively little chromatic power. It is the ED position marked in Samsung's drawing.

### L211 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.84666, νd = 23.8. HOYA FDS90 equivalent. f = +79.82 mm.

The dense-flint front member of C1 contributes positive power and strong dispersion. Its rear face is the cemented interface with L221.

### L221 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.74400, νd = 44.9. HOYA LAF2 equivalent. f = −27.55 mm.

The biconcave rear member gives C1 its net negative power. The two glasses provide opposite chromatic contributions as the pair moves to focus.

### L311 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.69895, νd = 30.1. HOYA E-FD15 equivalent. f = −128.48 mm.

The front member of C2 presents a convex face to the iris. Its negative power and relatively high dispersion balance the positive partner in G3a.

### L321 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.80420, νd = 46.5. HOYA TAF3 equivalent. f = +33.93 mm.

The strongly positive rear member gives C2 its net converging power. The pair reduces the height of the beam going into the rear section.

### L331 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.77250, νd = 49.6. HOYA TAF1 equivalent. f = +113.42 mm.

This positive meniscus leads G3b. Its convex front face converges the beam before the final negative element.

### L341 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.72825, νd = 28.3. HOYA E-FD10 equivalent. f = −64.47 mm.

The rear concave face supplies nearly all of the negative power of this element. The patent constrains that surface's power to balance spherical aberration and uses the power of the final pair to control the exit pupil and back focus.

## Focus Mechanism

Only G2 moves. It travels 1.080 mm toward the image at the middle position and 7.277 mm at the nearest position. G1, G3, the iris and the image plane stay fixed. Samsung's announcement identifies the production autofocus motor as a Supersonic Actuator (SSA) and specifies full-time manual focus.

D1 plus D2 stays at 17.990 mm. The printed object distances focus within 0.003 mm of the fixed image plane when measured from the first vertex. The spacings vary linearly between the printed positions. At the midpoint of the two finite settings, the paraxial image falls about 0.50 mm in front of the image plane at the indicated distance. The closest state still reaches the APS-C corner, though the gap between surfaces 4 and 5 limits the on-axis beam at full aperture. At that object distance the ray aimed at the iris edge would reach surface 4 beyond the radius at which the faces meet.

## Design Conditions

The patent limits back focus in air relative to focal length to 0.17–0.35, leaving room for the filter behind a bright telephoto design. The first embodiment gives 0.257. Its focal length divided by the focal length of G3a is 1.856, within the stated 0.50–2.00 range. Dividing the system focal length by L341's focal length gives −1.319, within the −3.00 to −0.25 range. The power of the last refracting surface times the system focal length is −1.288, within the −3.00 to −0.30 range.
