## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** US 8,385,002 B2
**Application Number:** 12/972,304
**Filed:** December 17, 2010
**Granted:** February 26, 2013
**Inventor:** Makoto Misaka
**Assignee:** Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.
**Title:** Zoom Lens and Photographing Apparatus
**Example details:** Tables 1–4, Figure 2; scale 1, millimetres.

Embodiment 1 is a probable match to the original Samsung S1855SB family. Samsung's 2010 NX100 brochure identifies that model as a 12-element, 9-group zoom with one aspherical element, optical image stabilization and a 0.28 m minimum focus distance. The patent has the same element/group count, one rear aspherical face and three cemented pairs. These construction details support a family association, but do not establish a unique embodiment or an exact factory prescription. The specialist index calls the lens OIS III; equivalence to that later version remains unproved. Production tolerances, coatings and glass suppliers are unknown.

## Optical Architecture

Twelve taking elements form nine physical groups; three cemented doublets account for the group reduction. A camera-side 2.24 mm low-pass plate is modeled separately. The five moving zoom groups use the patent's L1–L5 notation and have positive–negative–positive–negative–positive power: L1 is the front cemented pair, L2 contains three singlets, L3 is the focusing singlet, L4 contains two cemented pairs, and L5 contains the aspherical positive singlet and final negative meniscus. The individual elements use the patent's numbers 1–12. All five groups move toward the object from wide to tele, while the stop moves with L4. Five variable air spaces reproduce the three printed zoom states. The iris remains at S12; its radius follows an inferred aperture schedule between the marketed endpoints.

## Focus Mechanism and Zoom Motion

Only infinity focus is modeled. The patent focuses by moving group L3 toward the image; Table 3 gives travel of 1.810 / 2.109 / 3.149 mm at wide / middle / tele. Those movements produce approximately 285 / 283 / 281 mm object-to-image distances, rather than the stated 450 mm. The printed spacings and image gaps remain unchanged. The production lens's 0.28 m minimum focus distance is a separate Samsung specification.

The model contains 17 zoom stations: three printed states and fourteen calculated states, with eight equal interpolation steps between each printed pair. D1, D4, D5 and D2 + D3 are interpolated; group L3 translation is solved to retain the interpolated defocus of the printed states. Residual defocus is −0.033 to −0.039 mm. Between stored stations, interpolation adds at most 0.026424 mm of defocus, so continuous parfocality is not established. Intermediate focal-length labels use calculated paraxial EFL; the three printed labels remain 18.55 / 28 / 53.4 mm. These positions are not published production cam data.

## Aspheric Surfaces

S20, the rear face of element 11, is the sole aspherical surface. Formula 20 uses the standard 1 + K convention. Table 4 gives R = −27.831 mm, K = 0, A4 = 7.44814 × 10⁻⁵ mm⁻³ and A6 = 4.32792 × 10⁻⁷ mm⁻⁵; A8 and A10 are zero.

## Aperture, Geometry and Model Scope

The f/3.5 and f/5.6 endpoints come from Samsung's brochure; the patent prints no iris schedule, so intermediate apertures are inferred. Samsung lists minimum apertures of f/22 at wide and f/36 at tele. The stored f-stop list ends at f/22; it does not reproduce that zoom-dependent minimum.

Semi-diameters are estimated from Figure 2, PDF page 3, at approximately 9.50 native pixels/mm. At S5, the curved face ends near 9.75 mm radius and extends peripherally to 13.93 mm. The model treats that extension as opaque, although the patent does not specify its material or opacity. The extension adds no geometric light loss. The allowed gap-sag intrusion is 96%, the smallest two-decimal allowance covering the calculated 95.0676%; the remaining physical clearance is 0.35020 mm.

The NX100 sensor measures 23.4 × 15.6 mm; the model uses the slightly larger 23.6 × 15.7 mm APS-C format. The earlier projection cap has been removed, allowing the engine to solve the APS-C corner at every stored station. Geometric pupil transmission at the corner is approximately 69.0% / 73.3% / 85.0% at wide / middle / tele, with a 68.9–85.0% range over all 17 stations. These are modeled pupil-area fractions rather than measured photometric illumination. Axial and corner-chief rays clear every stored rim; the separate meridional clearance solver does not find the lower corner marginal ray at the first seven stations, so complete corner-bundle clearance is not established. The patent supplies no circular image diameter.

## Image Stabilization

The elements 9–10 doublet C3 shifts perpendicular to the optical axis to correct image shake, as described with Figure 1. Samsung's brochure identifies a lens-shift optical stabilizer. The centered prescription does not model stabilization travel or decentering.
