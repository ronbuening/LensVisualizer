# Goerz / Weidert cemented achromat, US1643865 Example 1

This hidden model is a patent reference, not an identified retail lens. The [1927 US grant](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/01/b4/9e/c2cb43ba5315b4/US1643865.pdf#page=2) is based on a 1923 German priority. Example 1 gives two spherical glass elements cemented at a shared interface, nominal 100 mm f/9, and a rear iris. The model preserves +21, −35, +38.82 mm radii, 3 and 1.25 mm center thicknesses, 14.7 mm free lens diameter, and a 9.1 mm iris 11.5 mm behind the last vertex. No estimated optical or mechanical geometry, no synthetic air gap, and no allowance override.

The calculated paraxial EFL is 100.002397 mm and infinity BFD is 93.307976 mm from the last glass surface. Approximate modeled f-number is f/8.99. At the marginal input height cited in the patent (5.56 mm), an independent exact Snell trace gives −3.089159 mm longitudinal spherical aberration, in agreement with the approximately −3.1 mm cited. The residual is intentional undercorrection, not focus to remove. The calculation uses printed D-line refractive indices and Abbe numbers; the engine approximates dispersion from those constants, not from a measured modern glass substitute. The original nF and nG′ rows remain in the scans.

Native samples transmit 181/181 within the center 90% of the axial pupil radius and 199/201 across the full axial meridional scan. Diagnostic off-axis meridional transmission is 7/9 at 10°, 5/9 at 20°, and 3/9 at 30°. This is sample clipping evidence, not measured illumination, certified coverage, or MTF.
