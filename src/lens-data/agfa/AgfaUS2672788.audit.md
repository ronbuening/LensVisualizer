# AGFA 100mm f/2.5 (US 2,672,788 Design) — Audit

## Source

US 2,672,788 A, Theodor Brendel, assigned to Agfa Camera-Werk AG. Filed July 19, 1951; German priority July 26, 1950; published March 23, 1954. The patent prints f = 100 mm for all three examples and a 55° picture field. Figures 3–4 extend to 25°.

## Transcription

Example 2 on PDF page 4 supplies nine signed radii, including plane R4 and R8, five glass thicknesses, three air spaces, five d-line indices and five Abbe numbers. Two independent readings of the page images agree with every value. Figure 1 agrees with the five-element, four-group arrangement and the L2/L3 cemented interface. No aspheric terms or variable gaps are printed.

## First-order

The prescription gives EFL 100.000530154 mm, paraxial f/2.5 and BFD 70.930726228 mm. The optical length is 46.655 mm; the first-surface-to-image distance is 117.585726228 mm. The image plane is the unprinted paraxial infinity plane. The nominal 100 mm focal length is printed, rather than inferred from the calculation.

The modeled iris radius is 13.937449104 mm. An exact axial ray entering at 20 mm height reaches the stop at 13.800673152 mm; the real-ray entrance-equivalent aperture is about f/2.475. These pupil definitions do not alter the printed f/2.5.

## Not printed

The 7.68/6.19 mm diaphragm split, its bore, all clear semi-diameters and the final image distance are inferred. Figure 1 places the diaphragm in the 13.870 mm central gap; a drawing measurement gives about 7.72/6.15 mm. No close-focus spacings, camera mount, camera format, individual spectral-line indices or glass suppliers are given.

The optical boundaries have positive glass thickness and central-gap clearance. The former ±10° field was narrower than the patent's stated field.

## Product

No production lens is documented for this prescription. Its five-element, four-group construction does not match the six-element Solagon or the four-element Tessar-type Solinar.

## Corrections

The former description incorrectly treated the 100 mm focal-length header as unconfirmed and omitted the printed 55° picture field. Both are explicit on PDF page 3. The numerical prescription, stop split, clear radii and image plane require no correction.

## Remaining questions

Open: Figure 1 suggests R1/R2 ≈ 20.4 mm, R3/R4 ≈ 18.1 mm, R5 ≈ 13.6 mm, R6 ≈ 14.0 mm, R7 ≈ 16.3 mm and R8/R9 ≈ 16.25 mm. The current radii are 22/22/20/19/15/15/19/20/20 mm. The drawn R3 and R5 would clip the f/2.5 axial beam, which needs about 18.82 and 14.04 mm. The larger clear apertures remain; their relation to the drawing is unresolved.

Open: Vignetting at the printed field edge and the unprinted iris bore remain uncertain. A production identity and the historical glass melts are not established.
