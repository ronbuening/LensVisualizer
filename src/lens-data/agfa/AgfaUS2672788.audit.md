# AGFA 100mm f/2.5 (US 2,672,788 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

[US 2,672,788 A, _Photographic Objective Comprising Two Outer Positive Single Element Members Enclosing Two Meniscal Shaped Negative Members_](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/2672788), by Theodor Brendel, assigned to Agfa Camera-Werk Aktiengesellschaft, Munich (Agfa Camera-Werk AG). Filed July 19, 1951; German priority July 26, 1950; published March 23, 1954.

Example 2 is in printed column 3 on PDF page 4. Figure 1 on page 1 shows the arrangement; page 2 contains the aberration curves. Page 3 explicitly gives f = 100 mm for all three examples and a 55.0° picture field. Example 1 is f/2.8; Examples 2 and 3 are f/2.5. The astigmatism and distortion plots extend to 25.0°.

## Transcription Check

The nine radii, five glass thicknesses, three air spaces and five index/Abbe pairs agree with the Example 2 page image. All finite radius signs are printed. R4 and R8 are plane, and R4 is the cemented L2/L3 interface. No transcription mismatch, aspheric term or variable spacing was found. Dimensions retain the printed 100 mm scale in millimetres.

Figure 1 shows five elements in four air-spaced groups, with the two inner negative menisci facing the diaphragm and positive outer singlets. The very short rear air space and thick final element agree with Example 2. All five conditions stated for the design hold: optical length 46.7% of focal length, front compound-meniscus thickness 28.5% of optical length, rear/front meniscus thickness ratio 0.448, L2 focal length 55.8% of the whole lens, and cemented index difference 0.00069.

The patent prints nd and names the d-line, without naming helium or a numerical wavelength. The helium d interpretation is consistent with Example 1's final glass: 1.62040/60.5 agrees with SK16 at helium d to about +0.00001 in index; the sodium D value is about 1.62032. All five elements retain `indexReference: "d"`.

## First-Order Check

Calculated EFL is 100.00 mm against the printed 100 mm; paraxial aperture is f/2.50 against the printed f/2.5. Calculated BFD is 70.93 mm, which the patent does not print. The image plane lies at paraxial infinity focus, 70.93 mm behind R9 and 117.59 mm behind R1. The first-to-last optical vertex distance is 46.655 mm.

The iris radius is 13.94 mm. An axial ray entering at 20 mm height reaches the stop at 13.80 mm; the real-ray entrance-equivalent aperture is about f/2.48 because of pupil aberration. The surfaces stay clear of each other, and the iris sets the full aperture. Minimum glass thickness is about 1.003 mm; central-gap clearance at the shared rim is about 4.211 mm, and the greatest rim angle is 41.5°.

The 27.5° chief ray clears every rim and reaches an image height of 51.70 mm; the smallest optical-face margin is about 6.17 mm at R1. Chief-ray coverage extends across the stated field. Geometric pupil transmission at 27.5° is about 42.9%, while an opposite-rim meridional ray remains unsolved. At 25.0°, front- and rear-face meridional bundle-width clipping is about 44% and 35%, respectively. Those widths are not transmitted-flux percentages.

The computed Petzval sum is 0.002162 mm⁻¹. The longitudinal spherical zone is about −0.275 mm, close to the patent's stated 0.25% of focal length. These results do not establish measured MTF or production-lens performance.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The diaphragm lies within the printed 13.870 mm central air space, but its 7.68/6.19 mm split and bore are inferred. This places it 26.253 mm behind R1. Figure 1 suggests about 7.72/6.15 mm, consistent with the retained split. The iris radius follows paraxial f/2.5, and the image distance follows the calculated paraxial back focus.

All clear semi-diameters are inferred: R1/R2 22 mm, square-cut R3/R4 19.4 mm, R5 14.2 mm, R6 15 mm, R7 19 mm and square-cut R8/R9 19.6 mm. Drawing estimates are approximately 20.3, 20.3, 18.1, 18.1, 13.6, 14.0, 16.3, 16.3 and 16.3 mm in surface order. The drawing's R3 and R5 would clip the axial f/2.5 beam, which reaches about 18.82 and 14.04 mm there. The larger rear rims preserve off-axis light and the nearly equal outer heights of the rear groups. No rim crosses the diaphragm plane.

The 55.0° field is printed; the corresponding 103.41 mm image-circle diameter is derived from twice the 27.5° chief-ray height at the infinity plane. It is not a published camera format or a fully illuminated image diameter. The off-axis ray fan is at 16.5°, within that field.

`focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. No close-focus state or mechanism is supplied. The selectable aperture range extends from f/2.5 to f/22; the patent supplies no minimum iris opening.

All five glasses retain their printed index/Abbe pairs and remain unmatched. No individual C-, F- or g-line indices, partial dispersions, glass trade names, suppliers or melts are printed. Spectral behavior therefore depends on Abbe-based dispersion estimates.

## Product Identification

No production lens is documented for this prescription. Its five-element, four-group construction differs from the six-element Solagon and the four-element Tessar-type Solinar. No mount, production image format, blade count or shape, marketed focal length, marketed aperture, marketed minimum focus distance or production stop range is assigned.
