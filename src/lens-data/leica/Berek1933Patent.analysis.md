## Optical Architecture
Six elements/four groups. Two air-separated front positives, a cemented negative member, and cemented positive rear member. Two cemented interfaces R6/R9. All spherical. The source splits the front member of a triplet-derived architecture. Numerical stop location is not given: iris estimated inside7.56mm rear airspace,6.5mm afterR7,1.06mm beforeR8. Not a factory iris claim.

## Element-by-Element Analysis
### L1: positive meniscus
nD=1.6185, νD=60.5. Unidentified source glass, no vendor assigned. Front collecting element.
### L2: biconvex
nD=1.6185, νD=60.5. Second front positive separated by1mm air.
### L3/L4: cemented negative member
L3 nD=1.6890, νD=31.2, positive meniscus; L4 nD=1.6034, νD=38.0, biconcave. Shared curved interface R6. Literal5.04mm L4 thickness retained.
### L5/L6: cemented rear positive member
L5 nD=1.6034, νD=38.0, negative meniscus; L6 nD=1.6580, νD=51.4, biconvex. Shared R9 interface. Source claim addresses collective cement faces; no measured performance inferred.
