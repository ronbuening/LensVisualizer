# CARL ZEISS 100mm f/2 (US 2,760,406 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

[US 2,760,406 A, _Three Component Photographic Objective_](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2760406A/en), by Johannes Berger, was filed July 8, 1953, with German priority of July 24, 1952, and published August 28, 1956. The publication names Carl Zeiss, Heidenheim (Brenz), Germany, as assignee; the source record uses Carl-Zeiss-Stiftung. Its sole numerical example, headed “Example,” is on PDF page 2, with the section drawing on PDF page 1. [CH 314696 A](https://patents.google.com/patent/CH314696A/en) is the Swiss counterpart. No production-lens source is documented.

## Transcription Check

All seven signed radii, four glass thicknesses, two air separations, four refractive indices and four Abbe numbers agree with the page images. No numerical transcription mismatch was found. In particular, element 4 prints an Abbe number of 49.2, not 49.3. The text and drawing confirm four elements in three components, with elements 3 and 4 cemented at R6 and the diaphragm between the second and third components.

The text states an approximate focal length of 100, relative aperture 1:2 and image angle ±14.0°. It supplies no length unit. Numerical dimensions retain their printed scale and are interpreted in millimetres. Isolated digit alternatives that bring the EFL closer to 100 mm worsen the marginal spherical correction and have no support in the page image; the printed prescription is retained.

## First-Order Check

The model has paraxial EFL 100.89 mm, 0.9% above the stated approximate value of 100, and paraxial aperture f/2.00, matching the printed 1:2. Calculated back focus is 69.02 mm; the patent gives no back-focus value. The image plane is at paraxial infinity focus, 69.02 mm behind R7. The first-to-last surface track is 70.3 mm and the first-surface-to-image distance is 139.32 mm. The stop radius is 17.82 mm.

The surfaces stay clear of each other, and the iris sets the axial aperture without a limiting clear rim. The 14.0° chief ray reaches an image height of 24.80 mm without blockage, supporting the 49.6 mm image circle. Portions of the off-axis bundle are vignetted; chief-ray coverage does not establish full-pupil transmission.

The claim's geometric conditions hold. R2 is shorter than ten focal lengths; |R3| exceeds both three times R4 and 80% of EFL. The R4-to-R6 vertex distance of 35.3 mm exceeds both 25% of EFL, 25.22 mm, and 115% of |R6|, 27.27 mm.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The exact iris station, bore, clear semi-diameters and image-plane distance are not tabulated. The drawing supports an estimated iris station 9.0 mm after R4 and 2.3 mm before R5, preserving the printed 11.3 mm separation. This places the stop 39.0 mm behind R1. Its radius follows the f/2 entrance pupil and is close to the approximately 17.7 mm opening read from the drawing.

The estimated clear semi-diameters at R1–R7 are 28, 26, 20.6, 17.3, 21.1, 21.1 and 21.1 mm. The drawing suggests approximately 25.3, 23.7, 20.6, 17.2, 20.7, 20.7 and 20.7 mm. The middle element follows the drawn unequal rims, and the rear cemented pair has a common rim. The larger front rims and slightly larger rear pair retain corner light. These dimensions pass the axial f/2 beam and the 14.0° chief ray. No clear diameter is printed.

The 49.6 mm image circle is twice the calculated 14.0° chief-ray image height, using the patent's stated field at the adopted millimetre scale. It is not a printed image-circle diameter or a production film format.

`focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. The patent gives no close-focus prescription or moving-group law. The selectable aperture range from f/2 to f/22 is retained; only f/2 is stated by the patent.

The model uses helium d at 587.6 nm because the printed glass coordinates resemble d-line LaK9, SF12 and BaF5 values. The text instead names D, conventionally sodium at 589.3 nm, and this discrepancy remains unresolved. All printed indices and Abbe numbers are unchanged. All four glasses remain unmatched; their dispersion is estimated from the printed Abbe numbers. No historical supplier, melt or measured partial dispersion is established.

## Product Identification

No production lens is documented for this exact prescription. The stated intention for small focal lengths is consistent with a cine application but does not identify a marketed lens. The title retains the patent's approximate 100 focal-length scale, interpreted in millimetres. No marketed focal length, marketed f-number, marketed minimum focus distance, mount, production image format, diaphragm blade count or blade shape is assigned.

## Corrections, 2026-11-21

- The maker changed from unspecified to Carl Zeiss Oberkochen. The title and key identify the maker, focal length, aperture and patent, and the design is publicly listed. Patent authorship remains Johannes Berger, and the sole example and 1956 publication year are retained.
- The patent designation gained the publication suffix A. The assignee changed from Carl Zeiss to Carl-Zeiss-Stiftung, while the bibliography retains the assignee wording printed in the publication.
- The subtitle is “US 2,760,406 A, sole example — Zeiss / Berger.” The specifications are “4 ELEMENTS / 3 GROUPS,” “ALL SPHERICAL,” “DESIGN f = 100 mm” and “DESIGN F/2”; the focal-length chip uses the stated approximate value at the adopted millimetre scale.
- The design focal-length value changed from 100 to 100.89 mm to describe the calculated EFL. The printed aperture f/2 and the four-element, three-group counts are retained. No prescription scale change was made.
- All four index references changed from sodium D to helium d, with the conflicting printed wording recorded explicitly. Every printed index and Abbe number is retained.
- The former sodium-D glass descriptions were replaced by Unmatched labels containing the printed coordinates: 1.69100 / 54.8 for elements 1 and 3, 1.64831 / 33.8 for element 2, and 1.60729 / 49.2 for element 4. LaK9 (Schott) and SF12 (Schott) are unavailable catalogue curves. The proposed BaF5 (Schott) association was removed: it selects Hoya BAF5, with d-line differences of Δn = +0.00000225 and Δν = +0.137. The Abbe-number difference exceeds the printed precision, and the supplier differs. At sodium D, the index difference is −0.000106. No historical glass identity is inferred.
- The element labels became Element 1 through Element 4. Their shapes are Positive Meniscus, Biconcave Negative, Biconvex Positive and Negative Meniscus. Optical roles and in-air focal lengths of +65.57, −32.65, +29.08 and −61.68 mm were added. The rear cemented component has combined focal length +51.80 mm.
- The rear cemented-pair identifier changed from G3 to C1, spanning R5–R7. The three brackets name the front positive, middle negative and rear cemented components.
- The R4-to-stop and stop-to-R5 gaps changed from 10.06/1.24 mm to 9.0/2.3 mm to follow the drawn station while preserving their printed 11.3 mm sum. The stop coordinate changed from 40.06 to 39.0 mm behind R1, and its derived radius changed from 17.95 to 17.82 mm.
- R4's clear semi-diameter changed from 20.3 to 20.2 mm. Its rim sag is then 8.895 mm, ahead of the stop plane 9.0 mm behind the vertex; the larger radius extended slightly past that plane. The other clear rims remain unchanged.
- The full field and maximum half-field changed from 10.0°/5.0° to the printed 28.0°/14.0°. The 49.6 mm image circle was added from the calculated chief-ray height. No format or mount is inferred. The image plane remains at calculated paraxial infinity focus.

After these corrections, EFL is 100.89 mm, aperture is f/2.00 and back focus is 69.02 mm. The optical track is 70.3 mm and the first-surface-to-image distance is 139.32 mm. The stop radius is 17.82 mm and the half-field is 14.0°.

## Remaining Questions

- The source's D designation conflicts with the d-line glass coordinates. The historical glass suppliers, melts and partial dispersions remain unknown.
- Exact clear rims and iris dimensions are not printed. The estimated front rims exceed the drawing to retain light; full off-axis pupil transmission is not established.
- The physical length unit, any production-lens identity, marketed specifications, lens mount, production image format, diaphragm blade count and shape, and minimum aperture are undocumented.
- No close-focus prescription, focus travel, measured MTF or transmission data is supplied.
