## Optical Architecture

Four power groups follow a positive–negative–positive–negative sequence. Source group focal lengths are58.53,−44.28,46.64 and−101.75mm. G1 has five singlets. G2 has a cemented pair and negative singlet, G3 two doublets, and G4 a doublet plus negative rear meniscus.

The source defines57.4mm and31.8mm as effective diameters of the first and last lens surfaces. Only S1 and S27 therefore receive the source-backed caps28.7mm and15.9mm. They are not entrance-pupil, iris or all-surface dimensions. Every other cap remains inferred.
