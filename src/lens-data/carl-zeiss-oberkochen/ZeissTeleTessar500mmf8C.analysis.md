## Optical Architecture

Five spherical glass elements form three air-separated components: one front singlet, a front cemented doublet and a rear cemented doublet. The positive front assembly and negative rear assembly produce the short telephoto layout. Eight refracting interfaces plus the explicit diaphragm are represented.

The source splits its long air gap into159.5 mm before the diaphragm and5 mm after it. The stop remains at191.7 mm behind the first vertex. Its diameter is derived from f/8, rather than treating the patent's24 mm shutter-clearance discussion as an exact iris measurement.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

- L1 is the positive biconvex front singlet, nd1.51009/νd63.37,12 mm thick.
- L2 is the positive member of the front doublet, nd1.51821/νd65.18,12 mm thick.
- L3 is its negative biconcave member, nd1.75520/νd27.53,8 mm thick. The common interface is a single glass-to-glass surface.
- L4 is the positive member of the rear doublet, nd1.62004/νd36.34,3.5 mm thick.
- L5 is the negative biconcave final member, nd1.62041/νd60.29,3 mm thick. The rear component's net power is negative.

## Glass and Dispersion

The patent supplies d-line index and Abbe number only. Those native constants remain unchanged. Closest catalog labels are modeling surrogates, not recovered supplier/melt identities: BK1 (Sumita), PC2 (Hoya), E-FD4 (Hoya), F2 (Schott) and S-BSM16 (Ohara). Catalog dispersion and partial-dispersion behavior are not established by the two source constants. No apochromatic performance claim follows.

## Focus Mechanism

A derived unit-focus extension moves the complete optical assembly relative to the image plane, from the Gaussian infinity conjugate to the manufacturer's8.5 m object-to-image distance. Seventeen calculated focus keyframes interpolate the back gap; the close endpoint is206.209998 mm. Internal source intervals and the stop relationship remain fixed.

This is an explicitly reconstructed focus law, not a patent-published finite state or recovered mechanical cam. No source-certified finite MTF stations are marked. The source's printed infinity BFL and the calculated image-plane adjustment remain distinct in the package.

## Aspherical Surfaces

All eight refracting surfaces are spherical. No aspheric coefficients are supplied or inferred.

## Apertures, Field and Validation Limits

Clear semi-diameters are not printed. The model uses inferred43 mm front-component and16 mm rear-component radii, guided by the optical-section proportions and outer-envelope plausibility. The86 mm filter and90 mm barrel are not claimed as measured clear apertures. These rim assumptions materially affect vignetting; some outer pupil rays clip and are not counted as successful rays. No gap-sag or rim-angle override is used.

The canonical6x6 format is56x56 mm; the historical sheet gives56.5x56.5 mm. Additional validation targets the larger sheet diagonal, approximately39.9515 mm semi-height, separately from the canonical display format. A chief ray reaching this field does not establish complete pupil illumination.

The package records exact-ray, geometry, source-roundtrip, focus-interpolation and schema checks. Manufacturer white-light MTF has not been independently reproduced, and full browser integration is not certified. The source and product association remain qualified rather than factory-exact.
