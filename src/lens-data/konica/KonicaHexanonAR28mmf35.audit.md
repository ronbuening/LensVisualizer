# KONICA HEXANON AR 28mm f/3.5 — audit

## Source

Toshiko Shimokura's US 4,170,403, assigned to Konishiroku Photo Industry Co., Ltd., was granted October 9, 1979. Example 3 appears in column 5 on PDF page 8. Figure 5 shows the five lenses and Figure 6 gives image heights and aberrations at infinity. Konica's 1980 dealer sheet for catalog no. 703-191 supplies the production section and specifications. [Patent](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/04/e4/41/0cfb6ccdc6aeed/US4170403.pdf) · [Dealer sheet, p. 6](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/02831/02831.pdf#page=6)

## Transcription

All ten signed radii, nine center thicknesses and air spaces, and five d-line index and Abbe-number pairs agree with the Example 3 table. A scale factor of 28 converts the lengths at unit focal length to millimetres. The footnote places the diaphragm 0.087 focal lengths behind surface 4. This divides the 4.690 mm space into 2.436 mm before the stop and 2.254 mm after it. The final air gap is the printed back focus. All five groups are spherical singlets. Even the closely spaced L4 and L5 have an axial air gap of 0.098 mm.

Example 3 also satisfies the four limits in Claim 1. The mean index of L4 and L5 is 1.71455. In units of focal length, the L3 thickness, L2–L3 air space and L2 thickness are 0.0698, 0.1675 and 0.2094 respectively.

The first and last vertices in Figure 5 span about 770 pixels for 32.2448 mm of axial length. At this scale the following clear radii follow the curved faces to their edges. On the back of L1 the optical face ends at the flange step, and the front of L3 ends at the chamfer.

Figure 5 draws the adjacent edges of surfaces 6 and 7 at roughly 6 mm from the axis. With the printed curvatures and spacing those surfaces meet at 5.29 mm. Ending the clear faces at 5.00 mm keeps an air space and passes the f/3.5 axial marginal ray, which reaches 4.86 mm and 4.88 mm on the two faces. The f/3.5 entrance pupil sets the paraxial stop radius to 4.8504 mm.

For close focus, the unit-focus calculation holds the positions of the elements and iris fixed relative to one another. A 3.4688 mm advance gives a 38.2168 mm final air gap. Allowing for the infinity film-plane offset gives a 300.00 mm subject-to-image distance with an additional image-plane residual below 0.0001 mm. At exactly 300 mm from the physical film plane, the residual is −0.0169 mm, compared with −0.0172 mm at infinity. Using the physical plane directly as paraxial focus gives a subject-to-film distance of 301.11 mm.

The glass names are dispersion equivalents. In L1–L5 order, S-BAL35, S-LAM52, PBH23, S-LAH53 and S-BSM15 all match Ohara catalog entries. The signed differences in index (catalog minus patent) are +0.000030, 0, +0.000002, −0.000002 and −0.000008. The corresponding differences in Abbe number are +0.035, −0.010, +0.017, +0.026 and −0.034.

## Product Identification

Konica's 28 mm, 75°, f/3.5–22 lens for the AR mount focuses to 0.30 m. The dealer section agrees with the element sequence and axial proportions of Example 3. Relative to the front element, the second and fifth element diameters in Figure 5 are about 0.57 and 0.54. The dealer drawing gives 0.59 and 0.53. Example 1 covers 64° at f/2.8, and Example 2 covers 75° at f/2.8 with a proportionally thicker L2. These differences favor Example 3 for the late five-element f/22 Hexanon. The similar third-party f/16 Hexar and the possible relabeling of an f/16 Hexanon leave their identities separate from this attribution. [Konica dealer sheet](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/02831/02831.pdf#page=6) · [Buhl's comparison of the Hexanon and Hexar](https://www.buhla.de/Foto/Konica/Objektive/e28_35.html)

## Corrections

### 2026-11-25

The patent table is reproduced as printed. Its infinity back focus places the film 0.0172 mm ahead of paraxial focus. The 5.00 mm clear radii on surfaces 6 and 7 allow for the intersection of the spherical faces beyond their optical edges. The 0.30 m extension is calculated from the dealer's focusing distance. The aperture scale includes an intermediate f/4 setting between the production ring's f/3.5 and f/5.6 marks. A documented five-element specimen has six straight blades. [Photographed lens and aperture markings](https://japb.net/gear/gear-review-index/ds_konica-ar-28f35-5/)

## Remaining Questions

The manufacturer's diagram does not give working diameters at the L3–L4 air space or identify the original glass melts. The patent and dealer sheet supply no finite-focus section, mechanical travel or close-focus magnification. The estimated 1:8.1 reproduction ratio can be compared with 1:8.6 listed for a production specimen. [Five-element lens data sheet](https://japb.net/gear/gear-review-index/ds_konica-ar-28f35-5/)
