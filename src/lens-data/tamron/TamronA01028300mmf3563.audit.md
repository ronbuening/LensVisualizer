### Source erratum: the middle f-number label

The patent prints no f-number table. ¶0061 (p. 20) gives only f and the five gaps; the f-numbers exist only as labels
on the spherical-aberration panels: Fno = 3.628 on Fig. 26(a) (p. 33), 3.628 on Fig. 27(a) (p. 34), and 6.459 on
Fig. 28(a) (p. 34), each read from a 600 dpi crop. The middle label is corrected under the source-errata standard on
two independent kinds of source-internal evidence.

- **`source-summary` — the stated f-numbers traced through the prescription.** By real marginal ray the wide label
  needs a stop radius of 9.4992 mm (9.4979–9.5005 within print rounding) and the tele label 9.4998 mm
  (9.4990–9.5005): one radius to 0.006 %. The printed middle 3.628 needs 14.24 mm by paraxial inversion and 15.06 mm
  by real marginal ray through the prescription without rims, 52–53 % and 59 % more than the wide and tele labels
  need by the same reckoning. The prescription itself cannot pass that beam: L9 (S17/S18, R 45.4709 / −223.1183,
  d 2.3000) reaches zero edge thickness at a semi-diameter of 13.06 mm and L10 (S19/S20, R 32.9276 / −59.4375,
  d 3.8000) at 12.51 mm, both directly behind the stop (¶0059, p. 19). With 5.546 at the middle station the three
  stations need 9.4992 / 9.4998 / 9.4998 mm.
- **`aberration-figure` — the curve plotted under the label.** On the 600 dpi crop of Fig. 27(a) the ±1.00 mm axis
  spans 629 px and the pupil axis 953 px. The d-line curve lies at −0.05, −0.06, −0.11, −0.20, −0.28, and −0.36 mm at
  0.6, 0.8, 0.9, 0.95, 0.975, and 1.0 of the pupil. An independent real-ray trace of the prescription with a 9.499 mm
  stop gives −0.054, −0.060, −0.106, −0.193, −0.269, and −0.377 mm at the same pupil fractions. An f/3.628 beam at
  91.5 mm gives −2.4 mm at 0.75 of its pupil, outside the plotted axis, with its marginal ray 17.1 mm high on S17.
  The same iris reproduces the other two panels at the pupil edge: −0.18 mm plotted against −0.189 mm traced on
  Fig. 26(a), and +0.20 mm against +0.201 mm on Fig. 28(a).
- Supporting, not counted toward the two kinds: the sibling examples print a middle f-number between their wide and
  tele values at their own 91.5 mm states. Figs. 23(a), 31(a), and 35(a) (Examples 6, 8, and 9, pp. 33, 35, 36) print
  4.777, 5.624, and 5.628. The figure labels of this patent are not free of slips elsewhere either: Fig. 32
  (Example 8 tele, p. 35) prints ω = 2.248° where the other tele panels print 4.24–4.25°.
- The replacement is the traced value rather than a reading of the source, because the source carries no other
  middle f-number for Example 7. The iris-only figure of f/5.440 that paraxial scaling of the printed label gives is
  not used: inside the file's G2 rims no real marginal ray reaches the stop at f/3.628, so that figure rests on the
  paraxial 14.2441 mm radius. A real marginal ray through the 9.4992 mm iris gives f/5.546.
- `sourceErrata` covers surface values only, so the file carries no `sourceErrata` entry for this label. The
  correction is recorded in the inline comment on `nominalFno`, in the file header, and here.

  wide-end section and Figs. 26–28 to its wide, middle, and tele aberrations.
