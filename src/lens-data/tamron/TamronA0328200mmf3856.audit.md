### Why 8.8 was not written

- Surface 21 (R = 157.33, L9 rear) and surface 22 (R = −38.27, L10 front) face each other across a fixed 1.400 mm air space and close toward the rim. Their sags sum to 1.2140 mm at 8.6 mm, 1.2718 mm at 8.8 mm, and the full 1.400 mm at 9.228 mm, where the two faces meet.
- The validator allows 0.90 of the gap, 1.260 mm, which is reached at 8.7595 mm.
