# Audit — MINOLTA MD 28mm f/2.8 (5 elements / 5 groups)

Patent: US 4,493,536, Example 1.

## 2026-09-21 — Patent figure, glass and metadata integration

### Semi-diameters

Reviewed local `US_4493536_A.pdf, p. 2, FIG. 1` at 600 dpi and compared the optical outlines with the viewer. Retained SDs. Front, second and final optical rims agree within about 10%. The automatic L3 reading near 13.6 mm includes its leader: the clean rim is approximately 7.5 mm, close to the authored 6.9 mm. The thin rear positive menisci retain their separate rims.

### Glass classification

The patent reference coordinates are retained unchanged. Catalog curves are qualified spectral proxies unless the patent explicitly names the glass supplier; no production-melt identity is inferred from a coordinate match. Compatibility uses the authored d/e reference system, not a mixed-line comparison.
