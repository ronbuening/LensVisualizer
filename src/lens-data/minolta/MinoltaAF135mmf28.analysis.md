## Optical Architecture

Seven spherical glass elements form five groups. Two positive singles and a cemented positive/negative pair form the positive front unit. The middle positive/negative cemented doublet has net negative power and is the source’s sole moving unit. A positive rear singlet forms the final unit. Twelve refracting faces include shared interfaces S6 and S9. R8 remains positive9885.29native, not a plane. No aspheres or rear plates are introduced.
