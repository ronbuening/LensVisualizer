# Nikon AF-S NIKKOR 85mm f/1.4G

## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** US 8,767,319 B2 — *Inner-Focus Large-Aperture Medium-Telephoto Lens System, Imaging Optical Device, and Digital Appliance*

**Inventors:** Yoshihito Souma; Satoru Shibata

**Assignees:** Konica Minolta Advanced Layers, Inc.; Nikon Corporation

**PCT filed:** February 24, 2011 (PCT/JP2011/054084)

**Priority:** March 2, 2010 (JP 2010-045271)

**Granted:** July 1, 2014

**Embodiment analyzed:** Example 1, Figure 1, numerical data in columns 11–12 (PDF p.17).

The 85.000 mm example has ten elements, nine air-separated components and an internal focusing group. These support the catalog association with the AF-S 85mm f/1.4G, but the patent does not establish an identical production prescription, supplier roster or coating placement. The source f/1.450 is used for numerical controls; f/1.4 remains the marketing designation. The published half-field is14.333° and image height21.630mm.

## Optical Architecture

Three positive groups have source focal lengths +252.708, +61.918 and +684.522mm. Gr1 contains L11–L13. Gr2 contains L21–L24 and the aperture stop. Gr3 contains the cemented L31/L32 pair and the final L3P singlet.

Gr2 and its stop move toward the object for close focus while Gr1 and Gr3 remain fixed. This is a single rigid internal focus group. The weak rear group contains a negative cemented component balanced by a positive final singlet. All prescription surfaces are spherical; this alone does not establish a particular bokeh quality or absence of production artifacts.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

Element focal lengths below describe each medium isolated in air, not its effective power inside a cemented pair. Aberration roles are qualitative interpretations of the prescription.

### L11 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.62041, νd = 60.3. Glass: S-BSM16 (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +106.6 mm.

Front positive meniscus — collects the f/1.4 beam and begins convergence.

### L12 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.80400, νd = 46.6. Glass: S-LAH65V (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +133.9 mm.

High-index positive meniscus — continues convergence with low Petzval contribution.

### L13 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.80610, νd = 33.3. Glass: NBFD15 (HOYA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ -52.2 mm.

Negative meniscus — Gr1 chromatic/field correction; diverges beam into Gr1–Gr2 gap.

### L21 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.83481, νd = 42.7. Glass: S-LAH55V (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +139.4 mm.

First positive in focusing group — re-converges beam from Gr1; f21/f22 ratio sets SA balance.

### L22 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.69895, νd = 30.1. Glass: S-TIM35 (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ -35.7 mm.

Only negative in Gr2 — chromatic correction and residual spherical aberration control (patent conditional (1)).

### L23 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.88300, νd = 40.8. Glass: S-LAH58 (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +96.8 mm.

Post-negative positive meniscus — begins re-convergence after L22 divergence.

### L24 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.72916, νd = 54.7. Glass: S-LAL18 (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +45.1 mm.

Strong biconvex — primary crown element of Gr2 achromatic pair with L22; highest Abbe number in Gr2.

### L31 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.90366, νd = 31.3. Glass: S-LAH95 (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +92.1 mm.

Highest-index element — first half of L3N cemented doublet; sets refractive index difference at cement surface for SA tuning.

### L32 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.64769, νd = 33.8. Glass: S-TIM22 (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ -33.4 mm.

Second half of L3N doublet — cement surface (|Δn| = 0.256) changes the doublet power and spherical-aberration contribution.

### L3P — Biconvex Positive (equi-radii)

nd = 1.83400, νd = 37.2. Glass: S-LAH60V (OHARA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +52.4 mm.

Equi-biconvex (R = ±85.957) — exit angle control and field correction; symmetric shape minimizes coma.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The source provides nd/νd coordinates but no commercial glass identities. Named catalog counterparts are inferred dispersion models. Their compatibility does not prove a historical supplier, composition or manufacturing process. No patent-measured anomalous partial dispersion is provided; its absence does not establish that the glasses have no anomalous dispersion.

Gr2 combines its only negative element L22 (νd30.1) with three positive elements, including lower-dispersion L24 (νd54.7). The L31/L32 cemented junction has a refractive-index difference0.25597. These published coordinates support discussion of chromatic and spherical-aberration balancing, but not claims of a measured production bokeh signature. L24 uses the compatible S-LAL18 counterpart; the older analysis's S-LAL14 name was inconsistent with the data.

## Focus Mechanism

| Gap (mm) | Infinity | Source near station |
|---|---:|---:|
| D6, Gr1–Gr2 | 17.306 | 7.706 |
| D15, Gr2–Gr3 | 1.700 | 11.300 |

Gr2, including its stop, travels9.600mm objectward. The equal and opposite gap changes keep total track126.370mm and rear air-equivalent BF41.050mm fixed. Intermediate focus positions are interpolated, not additional published stations.

The source near object lies719.0mm ahead of the first vertex. Adding the126.370mm source track gives0.845370m object-to-image distance, used for the focus slider endpoint. The patent lists β=−0.1175. An independent matrix calculation reproduces EFL84.99993mm at infinity and β=−0.117538 near, with a reconstructed object leg718.946mm; the0.054mm difference is consistent with the rounded published prescription. Source distance is retained rather than replacing it with the reconstructed value.

## Diagram Dimensions and Limits

No clear-aperture radii are tabulated. Figure1 was inspected from the original PDF at600dpi. Scaling its85.320mm glass span gives a front optical-rim radius about27.9mm and rear radius about15.1mm. The front value is smaller than the29.31mm entrance-pupil radius implied by85mm at f/1.45. Thus the drawn outline cannot be used directly as the clear aperture for the full nominal axial beam.

Existing inferred semi-diameters are retained pending a reliable resolution of that figure/aperture discrepancy. They pass surface-clearance and image-circle checks, but are not claimed as exact published rims. The automatic L21 measurement hits a label leader; it is excluded from rim evidence. Production filter-thread diameter does not establish any optical clear aperture.

The source explicitly defines BF as air-equivalent. The prescription already ends in41.050mm of air; no cover glass or filter is added, and no second air-equivalent conversion is applied.
