# NIKON TELECONVERTER TC-300 — audit

## Source

US 4,154,508, _Rear Conversion Lens_, Soichi Nakamura, Nippon Kogaku K.K., published 15 May 1979. The 300 mm master is on PDF page 13. The converter table is headed “EXAMPLE 2” on page 14, and claim 4 repeats it on page 15. Figure 2 shows the two optical systems together. [Original patent](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/a3/4d/9d/bc66def5d8957c/US4154508.pdf)

## Transcription

All ten radii, nine internal intervals, the 1.5423 mm junction and five index/Abbe pairs agree between the example and claim 4. The prescription retains the printed millimetre scale. Each element is an air-spaced spherical singlet. There is no cemented interface, converter iris or rear plate.

The Nikkor 300mm f/4.5 ED prescription in US 3,774,991, Example I, shares the master’s radii, spacings and indices. The Abbe numbers of master elements 1 and 4 are 81.9 in the converter patent and 81.5 in the 300 mm lens patent. The first-order comparison below uses the converter patent’s master. [US 3,774,991](https://patents.google.com/patent/US3774991A/en)

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

- **Rims.** Nikon’s 88.5 mm first-to-last vertex dimension sets the scale for its TC-300 section. The heights from the optical axis to the glass edges give 13.3 mm for surfaces 1–6 and 19.8 mm for surfaces 7–10. The patent drawing gives approximately 13.3 and 18.7 mm. With the same master and iris, the production-drawing rims transmit 96.0% of the corner pupil and give 100.7% geometric illumination relative to the axis. Substituting the patent drawing’s rear radius gives 87.1% relative illumination. These are calculated quantities. [Nikon’s dimensioned section](https://www.mir.com.my/rb/photography/companies/nikon/nikkoresources/teleconverter/tc300.gif)
- **Iris.** The master’s iris is placed 1.0 mm behind its seventh surface. Its calculated wide-open model radius is 29.26 mm and stays fixed when the converter is attached. The master’s Figure 2 clear radii fall short of the nominal axial marginal ray at S1 and S3 by 0.03 and 0.16 mm, within the roughly 0.3 mm precision of the drawing. The converter passes the axial and corner chief rays at every surface. A converging axial f/2.8 cone at the same virtual object distance also clears the converter, with the smallest margin about 0.94 mm at the first surface.
- **Image distances.** The master alone has 70.05349 mm of paraxial back focus. Subtracting the printed junction places its image 68.51119 mm behind the first converter vertex. Tracing through the converter gives the 63.11192 mm final gap. Nikon’s section labels this distance 63.1 mm. All element spacings are fixed, and the patent gives no finite-focus distances.
- **Glass.** The wavelength is unspecified. US 4,348,085 gives the front group’s two distinct index/Abbe pairs at the helium d line. In the rear group, Schott K10 matches L4’s printed index exactly with an Abbe difference of −0.09. Ohara S-FTL10 is another possible match. Hoya NBFD3 differs from L5 by −0.00004 in index and +0.13 in Abbe number. The d-line convention is inferred throughout. [US 4,348,085](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4348085A/en)

## Product Identification

Nikon’s TC-300 drawing gives an optical span of 88.5 mm, a back focus of 63.1 mm and a converter focal-length magnitude of 237.7 mm. Example 2 gives 88.54, 63.11 and 236.42 mm respectively. It alone pairs the five-singlet converter with a 300 mm f/4.5 master. With the drawing scaled to its vertex span, the ten calculated vertex positions differ from the drawn positions by about 0.62 pixels RMS over a 339-pixel span, against about 73 pixels for Example 1. Example 11 has seven elements and corresponds to the TC-200. [Nikon TC-300 section and specifications](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/00594/00594.pdf#page=106) · [US 4,154,508](https://patents.google.com/patent/US4154508A/en)

Nikon specifies a 2× multiplier and AI aperture coupling from f/2.8 through f/32 for long Nikkors. At infinity the 300mm f/4.5 ED, both 600mm f/5.6 ED prescriptions and the 360–1200mm f/11 ED reach the full-frame corner with the converter. This includes both ends of the zoom. The separate-mount 600mm f/5.6 ED also depends on its focusing unit for the camera connection. [Nikon TC-300 coupling requirements](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/00594/00594.pdf#page=106)

## Corrections

### 2026-11-25

The prescription follows Example 2 as printed. The small differences between its calculated focal lengths and the rounded patent summaries require no change to the surface table.

## Remaining Questions

The published sections leave the precise clear apertures, the master iris position and the production glass identities uncertain. Mechanical clearance and coupling for the separate-mount 600 mm ED with the TC-300 still need confirmation for the particular focusing unit.
