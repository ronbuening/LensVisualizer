## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** JP 2017-227799 A

**Inventors:** Yasunari Fukuda, Akiko Furuta, Toshinori Take, Takayuki Sensui

**Assignees:** Konica Minolta, Inc.; Nikon Corporation

**Filed:** June 23, 2016

**Published:** December 28, 2017

**Embodiment analyzed:** Example1 (EX1), Figure1, numerical data paragraphs66–71.

The represented prescription is a28.41mm, f/1.45, full-field75.42° design with image semi-height21.6mm. Its fourteen patent elements and eleven air-separated components are associated with the catalog's28mm f/1.4E. This is the source example, without a claim that production glasses, actuators or every manufactured dimension are established by the patent.

## Optical Architecture

The positive front group Gr1 is stationary apart from published spacing rounding; positive rear group Gr2 and the stop translate objectward for closer focus. Two negative menisci start the front assembly, followed by a positive singlet, cemented LS and positive L16. Rear Gr2 has two positive singlets, a pre-stop cemented pair, a post-stop cemented pair, positive L27 and the final aspheric meniscus L28.

The source counts compound L12 as one element. Its glass substrate and0.05mm resin layer require two medium entries to preserve their refracting interface, giving fifteen modeled entries for fourteen source elements. This resin is part of the lens, not a sensor cover or filter. The three ordinary cemented pairs and the compound L12 remain represented.

## Glass Identification and Selection

Named catalog entries are inferred coordinate counterparts; numerical source indices and Abbe numbers remain authoritative. The resin remains unidentified beyond its source description and numerical coordinate. L25's806333 code resolves to compatible catalog material without establishing a production vendor.

Paragraph36 applies the partial-dispersion condition to the glass immediately before the stop, L24. Paragraph71 lists **PgF=0.5899**. The source normal line is0.6415−0.00162νd; the runtime uses0.6438−0.001682νd. Accordingly the stored runtime departure is **0.5899−(0.6438−0.001682×32.3)=0.0004286**. The small near-normal departure does not warrant an anomalous-glass identity claim. Previous copied0.014 departures and inferred APD/chemistry claims on L23/L27 were removed; compatible catalog curves may still supply modeled dispersion.

## Focus Mechanism

Source D12 changes7.70→1.42mm and BF38.47→44.77mm. With a fixed image reference, Gr2 including the stop moves6.30mm objectward, while Gr1 moves0.02mm due to the published gap residual. The stated fixed-front-group intent is preserved without silently altering either source endpoint. Intermediate gaps are linearly interpolated.

The source object leg is105.61mm before the first surface. Adding the near table track144.40mm gives **0.25001m object-to-image**, replacing the old0.28m marketing endpoint. An independent matrix gives105.570861mm object leg and magnification−0.2190535; the small difference is consistent with the rounded prescription. Infinity EFL28.409897mm and track144.38mm reproduce the source summary. The source f/1.45 is retained in controls and its shortcut.

## Semi-Diameter Estimation Notes

Figure1, PDF p.21, was inspected at600dpi with the105.91mm optical vertex span as scale. Optical arcs were separated from front-lens shoulders, rim walls and leader lines. The front rim is30.5mm, its rear optical arc24.2mm; L12 front25mm and junction23mm; successive front components use23mm. Rear positive singlets use21/20mm, pre-stop pair18mm, post-stop pair16.4mm, L27 18mm and L28 18.8mm.

The resin exit retains21.8mm: an attempted23mm optical radius produces a negative0.203mm resin edge thickness. This figure-versus-prescription limit remains a follow-up; no fabricated thickness repair is made. All adopted surfaces pass geometry and image-circle checks, with no runtime trimming at infinity, midpoint or near. These are inferred drawing rims, not listed clear apertures.

## Scope and Limitations

Paragraph62 explicitly defines BF as air-equivalent from the last lens to the paraxial image. Its listed values already satisfy the project's cover/filter omission convention. No plate is added and no second conversion is applied. No separate cover/filter rows occur in Example1.

The source's rounded spacing residual and resin-edge drawing limit are documented above. The previous notes' confirmed production correspondence, supplier/process assertions, motor rationale and unverified comparisons with competing products were removed. The model reproduces the selected numerical example, with estimated optical rims and interpolated intermediate focus.
