# Audit Log — Nikon UV-Nikkor Auto 55mm f/4


## 2026-10-30 — Original patent rims, concave ends and glass

Checked the original untracked `patents/JPB 1967009417-000000.pdf` directly; SHA-256 `bbae6fd314b19f5b3954a847dbb3187306c1531fcf6f3797c907fd8d63579632`. The prescription on PDF pages 2 and 3 confirms all three n(365 nm)/νd pairs. The reference wavelength is stated on pages 1–2 and the UV/visible plots are on page 4. All three materials remain explicitly unresolved for full spectral tracing; their modern crown/flint names are comparison classes. Added an index-reference note to each element so the inspector does not identify these source values as nd/νd. No partial dispersion, line indices, supplier, special melt or UV Sellmeier curve was inferred. The d-line Abbe fallback cannot verify the published UV spectrum.

Native Figure 1 on page 3 (352×353 ppi) gives a source vertex span of about 385 pixels for the scaled 16.093 mm prescription. Half-heights read approximately 7.3/6.4/6.35 mm. Common 7.5/6.6/6.6 mm radii include scan stroke and schematic allowance, replacing the prior asymmetric 8.85/8.65, 6.65/6.55, 8.25/8.45 mm pairs. The L2 ends are oblique bevels; no flat concave-end trim or independent annulus is supported. These are inferred rims, not published clear apertures.

All 97 aimed axial samples clear at infinity, middle and estimated close focus. A union of identical launches from both models over ten angles through 21.5° at those three focus states gives 5,820 comparisons: 4,748 pass both, 872 pass only the old larger rims, and 200 pass neither. These counts are not flux or measured production vignetting. Canonical/surface, format-corner, image-circle and nominal f/4 checks pass. All nine actual SVG states are finite with zero hidden trim. Source radii, gaps, stop station, focus travel and apertures are unchanged.
