**Title:** Large-Aperture-Ratio Internal Focusing Telephoto Lens  
**Example details:** Table 3 (printed columns 9–10/PDF 24), Figure 7 (PDF 8). Literal millimetre scale.

This is a Grade B probable optical-family reconstruction associated with the original AF-S VR Nikkor 200mm f/2G IF-ED. Nikon does not identify the patent with that retail lens. The source 195 mm, f/1.84 and 12.62° full field remain distinct from the marketed 200 mm, f/2 and 12°20′. The matching 13/9 topology, three low-dispersion plus one very-low-dispersion front positions, four cemented pairs, focusing and stabilization groups, and timing support a family correlation rather than exact factory identity.

## Optical Architecture

Thirteen spherical powered elements form nine air-separated components. G1 has six elements, G2 has four elements in two doublets, and G3 has three singlets. The iris follows G2. Nikon lists an additional protective glass in the production lens; the source table has no such plate, so this model adds none.

## Glass Identification and Selection

Literal d-line indices, Abbe numbers and g-line indices are preserved on every element. The reference calculation uses 587.5618 nm. The source publishes no full supplier dispersion formula or complete C/F/g set; no C/F indices, modern vendor identity or full spectrum is fabricated. The ED/Super-ED positional comparison is a correlation with Nikon’s section, not a historical melt identification.

## Focus Mechanism and Image Plane

Only the source infinity configuration is modeled. The source near table has β = −0.1258, d1 = 15.0995 mm, d2 = 5.9244 mm and Bf = 59.4386 mm; these remain context, not a certified retail 1.9 m conjugate or continuous cam. VR decentering is not simulated. Canonical closeFocusM = 1e15 keeps finite focusing disabled.

The printed infinity d1 = 5.5000 mm, d2 = 15.5239 mm and final gap 59.4575 mm give image z = 217.3714 mm. Literal first order yields EFL 195.00190445157241 mm and diagnostic BFL 59.46097273677807 mm. The computed BFL does not replace the printed gap and no best-focus correction is applied to the authored plane.

## Stop and Clear-Aperture Assumptions

Source surface 17 explicitly marks the iris, at z = 133.5139 mm, with the printed 1.20 mm gap before S18. The native source f/1.84 iris radius is 18.207283861172133 mm. Exact operating-disk/finite-cap checks pass for the supported openings through f/22, with at least 1.20 mm clearance. This does not prove blade or barrel mechanics.

The clear apertures are inferred. Initial paraxial design-aperture heights plus 10% and 0.5 mm rounding were compared with the complete Figure 7 and Nikon section; the rear singleton was set from its relative outline before native tracing. The first S9 estimate exceeded the literal spherical domain. A bounded solid-geometry correction changed only inferred S1/S3/S6/S9 caps to 57.5/50/39/30 mm, preserving every radius, gap, index, stop and image-plane value. That branch has zero hidden render trim. Some faces therefore have less than the initial paraxial clearance allowance; no physical aperture accuracy is claimed.

Native wide-open pupil sampling retains 209/163/103 valid rays out of 209 at source angles 0°/3.155°/6.31°. Clipping is retained, including two S4 cemented-junction samples at the middle field and outer-field front/rear losses. These are consequences of the inferred clear-aperture hypothesis, not measured production vignetting. No semi-diameter is enlarged after tracing to improve contrast. The raw source-pupil engine log also retains 246 failures already certified blocked by the aiming stage; there are zero unblocked failures and zero unaimed samples. These raw statuses are not added to overlapping certified-block/clipping totals.

## Modeled Diffraction and Coverage

The native reference-wavelength, design-plane audit completes 27 format-reference fields across nine apertures at the six sampled frequencies 0/10/20/30/40/50 lp/mm. Every final field is converged through 50 lp/mm with zero failed/unaimed rays and zero unknown flux. These are scalar sheared-pupil estimates from the surviving traced pupil. The actual native-format field angles are 0°/3.1753006221°/6.3231225586°, distinct from the source angles used for pupil checks. Numerical stability is not independent optical-path phase or factory validation. No withdrawn one-dimensional OPL reference is used.

The source half-field is 6.31°. Source angles, the manufacturer’s field, the rounded 21.65 mm format reference and the exact 36×24 mm corner (21.6333076528 mm) remain separate; a format field outside source coverage is a modeled extrapolation.
