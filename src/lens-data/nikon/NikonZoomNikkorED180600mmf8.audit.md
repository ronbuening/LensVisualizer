# NIKON ZOOM-NIKKOR ED 180-600mm f/8 — audit

## Source

Soichi Nakamura, Nippon Kogaku K.K., US 3,743,384, granted July 3, 1973. Example II gives the prescription for 35 mm still photography. Fig. 3 shows its four assemblies and Fig. 5 gives aberration curves for the short, middle and long settings. The Certificate of Correction is dated March 5, 1974. [Patent and certificate](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/80/e7/33/3dc167dab27fbf/US3743384.pdf)

## Transcription

The corrected table accounts for 29 spherical refracting surfaces, 25 fixed axial thicknesses and air spaces, and the two printed values of each of D6, D13 and D19. All lengths are in millimetres at the original scale. The 18 helium d-line index and Abbe-number pairs agree with the table. The prescription includes one cemented triplet (L2–L4) and five cemented doublets (L5–L6, L9–L10, L11–L12, L14–L15 and L17–L18). Each shared interface appears once. The 0.200 mm gap after L1 and 0.100 mm gap between the compensator doublets are air.

The full f/8 axial bundle clears the rims at every zoom setting examined. The corner chief ray reaches the 35 mm frame at all 56 settings. The surfaces have positive edge thickness and clear their neighbours. At the corner, some outer pupil rays are vignetted.

**Iris and image plane.** Fig. 3 gives the long relay gap D24 without locating the diaphragm. Its assumed position is 35.75 mm from either face, 228.568 mm behind R1. The paraxial stop radius for f/8 is 12.785 mm wide and 12.782 mm tele. The fixed film spacing of 140.2743 mm is the rounded mean of the two endpoint back focal distances.

**Intermediate zoom settings.** At infinity the fixed front group and relay leave 124.918 mm for the three moving air gaps. For each intermediate position of the variator, the position of the compensator is solved for paraxial focus on the fixed film plane. The 54 calculated settings are more closely spaced in variator travel toward the long end, where the compensator motion curves more sharply. On the unrounded focus curve, 55 settings give a minimum peak defocus of about 20.95 µm between settings. With 56, rounding each gap to 0.0001 mm preserves the sum and leaves at most 0.30 µm of defocus at a calculated setting. The maximum between adjacent calculated settings is 19.95 µm. At the two printed settings the paraxial image falls 20.006 µm behind the film plane and 20.062 µm in front of it, respectively. Their 40.068 µm separation sets a lower bound on the error achievable with one film plane.

**Glass.** The catalog equivalents apply to 13 elements and reproduce their printed indices and Abbe numbers within 0.00001 and 0.05, respectively. L2/L14, L4/L18 and L17 have three distinct pairs without a close catalog match. Nikon identifies ED glass in the lens. L2 and L14 are the probable positions from their common 1.48606 / 81.5 pair.

## Product Identification

The match to the pre-AI Zoom-Nikkor is strong. Nikon's May 1976 drawing shows the same sequence of 11 air-separated groups, including the front triplet, paired compensator doublets and the distinctive long gap within the relay. The four assemblies at the wide end fall within about 0.4 mm of their drawn positions. Nikon labels the optical stack 281 mm and the back focus 140 mm. Example I belongs to the larger 360–1200 mm f/11 design. Nikon's 2.5 m minimum distance and the listed 1:3 maximum reproduction describe the production lens. Example II gives spacings only at infinity. ## Corrections

### 2026-11-25

The certificate changes the Example II radius R17 from −251.3 to +251.3 mm, the stated compensator focal length from 133.2688 to 113.2688 mm and the relay designation from f14 to f4. With the corrected radius the calculated compensator focal length is +113.2535 mm. The calculated long-end focal length remains 0.95% below the nominal 600 mm with the corrected prescription. [Certificate, final page](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/80/e7/33/3dc167dab27fbf/US3743384.pdf)

## Remaining Questions

- Nikon's drawing shows slightly larger relay radii than Fig. 3, about 18.4 and 18.0 mm ahead of the iris space and 15.5 mm behind it. The production clear diameters, exact diaphragm position and film spacing are unknown.
- The patent leaves the intermediate zoom cam, the moving group or groups for close focus, and their travel to 2.5 m unspecified.
- Nikon's unshaded drawing cannot establish which elements carry ED glass. The production glass types and individual partial dispersions of the five unmatched elements remain unknown.
- Vink's version-specific listing gives the early lens seven blades and the AI-S nine. Cavina gives nine in his account of the early design. Nikon's early specification describes the diaphragm as automatic and leaves the count open.
