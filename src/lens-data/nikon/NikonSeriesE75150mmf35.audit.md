## Historical audits prior to 2026-10-30

The controlling source is US 4,398,807 A, sole unnumbered embodiment, Figure 1 (PDF page 2), numerical table (page 11) and repeated claim. Independent source extraction and actual-file reconciliation established the 12-element/9-group, four-component model. No national-family values were substituted. Detailed frozen review transcripts remain in git history.

The patent gives source infinity fields 16.6°/11.5°/8.0° and close-plot image heights 0/15.1/21.6 mm, with magnifications −0.104/−0.147/−0.208. These close heights are not infinity field angles. The stop is inferred 1 mm before r14, using the production section; splitting d13 preserves its total. The production section also depicts an unmodeled fixed flare stop about 20 mm behind L10. Actual factory iris, clear apertures, spectral melts and serial-specific prescription remain unconfirmed.

Only G1 moves at close focus. The compact reconstructed solution for a 1 m object-to-film distance extends it about 14.7 mm and agrees with the rounded source magnifications; the other mathematical root near 722.5 mm is physically incompatible with the package. The patent prints no finite-focus spacings. Seven solved intermediate zoom stations reduce linear-interpolation image error from about 1.46 to 0.09 mm. These controls are reconstructions, not a recovered factory cam law.

Group/bracket and close-motion wording was synchronized.

All 12 elements resolve to coordinate-compatible catalog curves in six coordinate families: N-SK16, E-FD4, SF56A, N-BK7, LAFN7 and F1. Source nd/νd are retained; these are qualified spectral proxies, not historical supplier/melt identities or evidence of APD. Published source pairs do not establish full secondary spectrum. Explained outer-field cap misses remain lost rays, and sampled pupil fractions are not photometric throughput.
