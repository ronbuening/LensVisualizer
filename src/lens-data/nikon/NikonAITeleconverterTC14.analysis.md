# Nikon Ai TC-14 1.4× (original, 1978)

This is a hidden V2 integration model of the original five-element, five-group Nikon Ai TC-14, not the TC-14A, TC-14B, TC-14C, TC-14E or its successors.

## Production confirmation

[Nikon’s factory section](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/00594/00594.pdf#page=107) has five individually air-spaced elements. At one uniform scale the ten measured vertices agree with [US4206971](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4206971), Embodiment I, to 1.10 px RMS and 1.85 px maximum over a 176.6 px optical span. The scanned factory drawing is not a precision measurement of glass or clear rims. Embodiment II cements the rear two elements and is not the production match. Signed radii, distances, indices and Abbe numbers are unchanged.

## Optical validation

- The patent's negative d0 means that the absent host image would form 48.968 mm behind the converter’s front vertex. It is not a mechanical air gap. Keep the printed converter-front-to-image distance of 71.001 mm, including the derived 40.118 mm from the final vertex to the image.
- An independent first-order reconstruction of the signed 18-surface reference master gives 200.000531 mm before the converter, 280.007073 mm combined and a 1.40003164× ratio. The inferred gap from the last master surface to the first converter surface is 14.628683 mm. Paraxial focus is 0.627 µm behind the printed image plane.
- The native synthetic-host readback repeats the same ratio with 280.006329 mm combined focal length and 2.80006 effective f-number from an f/2 host. No geometry trimming or overrides were needed. It is a repeatable diagnostic, not a test of a commercial host.
- Semi-diameters of 12.7 mm (front six surfaces) and 14.0 mm (rear four) are computational test caps; no actual retail clear diameters or baffle data were inferred. In the synthetic native fixture, the f/2 host clears axis and ±1°, but ±2° clips at the first converter surface. Stopping the synthetic host to f/4 or f/8 clears the tested ±2° band. These bounds do not establish factory illumination or full-field performance.
- Specific Nikon F catalog lenses are excluded pending separate checks. This is not a claim of physical incompatibility. Commercial-host fit, performance, MTF, finite focus, broadband and UI behavior are not qualified here.

### Signed-master native cross-check

The same runtime reproduced 280.007073 mm and 1.40003164× using the complete patent reference prescription, with zero trim. This test used a deliberately synthetic external iris 1 mm in front of the master and estimated clear semi-diameters. Under those assumptions, the axis probes were 91/101 at f/2 (ten clips at master surface 7, not the converter) and 101/101 at f/4, f/8 and f/16. That is not a measurement or prediction of actual master vignetting.
