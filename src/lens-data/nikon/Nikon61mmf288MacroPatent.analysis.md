The 11-element/8-group example is distinct from the 12-element/9-group retail AF-S Micro-Nikkor 60mm f/2.8G model associated with US 7,898,744 B2, Example 2. Matching maker and approximate focal length do not establish commercial identity.

## Optical Architecture

The source describes positive G1 (S1–S8), positive G2 (S10–S14) and negative G3 (S15–S20). An air stop at S9 separates the first two groups. Eleven glass elements form eight air-separated groups; cemented pairs occupy S10–S12, S15–S17 and S18–S20.

No camera-side plate is prescribed or added. Model surface labels append A to the source asphere and map the source diaphragm to STO.

## Glass Identification / Selection

The source gives d-line indices and Abbe numbers, not catalog identifiers or a complete spectral dispersion law. No named glass, anomalous partial dispersion, apochromatic performance or measured broadband response is inferred. In particular, a high Abbe number alone does not determine secondary-spectrum behavior. Broadband calculations would depend on surrogate dispersion and have not been run.

## Focus Mechanism

The table gives two discrete states: D8 = 3.20/6.08 mm, D14 = 1.20/30.32 mm and BF = 40.00/40.00 mm at infinity/life-size. Relative to the fixed image and rear group, G1 moves objectward 32.00 mm and G2 moves objectward 29.12 mm. The stop remains 4.00 mm ahead of G2 under this table-governed interpretation.

At the near state, reduced-angle arithmetic gives an object distance of 82.722852 mm before S1 and magnification −1.0000787. The resulting object-to-image distance is 206.022852 mm. These are derived conjugates, not printed working distances or certified finite-analysis endpoints. Fig. 2B’s NA 0.10 label does not establish a measured fixed iris.

Only the tabulated endpoints are source-supported. Continuous interpolation is a viewer convention, not a recovered cam law. No focus motor, travel mechanism or minimum working distance is claimed.

## Aspherical Surfaces

S17 uses the source form with 1 − κy²/r² under the square root. Printed κ = 1.0000 converts to standard K = 0, not K = 1. A4 = −2.47160E−06 and A6 = −1.42350E−09; A8 = A10 = 0. The negative polynomial departure reduces sag relative to the spherical base at nonzero radius. No manufacturing method is established.

## Verification Summary

At infinity, EFL is 60.985549 mm versus printed 61.00 mm. The paraxial BF is 39.989285 mm versus printed 40.00 mm, a −0.010715 mm residual. The literal track is 91.30 mm while the source summary prints 91.29 mm; both are retained without retuning.

Both discrete endpoint configurations passed native construction, iris-in-air checks and zero rendered trim. The official infinity aperture census passed its 3% criterion. Geometric and diffraction reference MTF converged through 40 lp/mm at axis, half and full source image height, at the unchanged printed infinity image plane, using grids up to 128. Numerical convergence is not measured optical performance.

The Nikon aperture census gave f/2.88354 versus f/2.88. No inferred cap changes were needed after the static draft.

## Model Limitations

Rims and iris size are inferred geometry, not factory measurements. No gap-sag override is used. The reference calculation uses the runtime conventional d-line, 587.5618 nm; Nikon prints 587.6 nm. Finite MTF is unavailable because there is no source-certified object-distance convention. Finite aperture performance has not been qualified. Broadband, full integration gates and normal installation remain pending. The draft remains hidden until integration review; native checks alone are not a delivery qualification.
