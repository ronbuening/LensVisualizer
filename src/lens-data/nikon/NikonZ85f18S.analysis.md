The diagram reproduces the twelve-element, eight-component spherical patent example associated with the catalog's85mm f/1.8 lens. Its source summary says83mm, f/1.85 and full field29.26°. The association is not proof of an identical production prescription, commercial glass identities or motor arrangement. These distinctions replace the previous assertions of confirmed production correspondence and hypothetical production re-optimization.

## Optical Architecture

Four optical groups have positive–negative–positive–negative powers. Gr1 contains two singlets and a cemented pair; Gr2 is a cemented pair; Gr3 is a singlet; Gr4 has two cemented pairs and a final negative meniscus. The fixed stop lies between Gr2 and Gr3. There are eight air-separated glass components; this count differs from the four movement/power groups.

The source has no aspheric rows for Example3. All twenty glass boundaries plus the stop retain their printed radii, central thicknesses, indices and Abbe numbers. Source prose calls L42 plano-concave, while numerical R16=64.869mm and Figure3 show a curved rear surface; the numerical surface is retained.

## Source Limitations and Omitted Plate

PT surfaces22–23 are a1.6mm plate with nd1.51680. Paragraph60 defines bf0.92mm from its rear face to the image. Per the lens specification, PT stays excluded and the rear equivalent-air spacing is **11+1.6/1.51680+0.92=12.974852mm**, replacing13.52mm. This conversion preserves the d-line paraxial transfer; plate chromatic and higher-order effects are absent.

The printed rows sum to110.810mm including the physical plate, whereas the source summary says TL111.35mm. Their calculated infinity focal length is82.222194mm, whereas the source summary says83mm. Neither discrepancy is explained by adding the cover plate or choosing a different focal-length definition. The former notes' rounding and matrix-method explanations were unsupported and are removed. The source table remains intact; these summary/table discrepancies require follow-up. After plate conversion the modeled track is110.265852mm and its infinity matrix residual is approximately0.00002777.
