## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** JP 2015-041003 A; corresponding grant JP 6413211 B2.
**Published:** March 2, 2015; grant publication October 31, 2018.
**Inventor:** Haruo Sato (佐藤治夫).
**Applicant:** Nikon Corporation.
**Title:** Rear wide-converter lens, imaging apparatus having the rear wide-converter lens, and method of manufacturing the rear wide-converter lens.
**Example details:** The shared master Gm in Example 1, Table 1 and Figure 1. Tables 2 and 3 repeat the same master.

This is one conditional, standalone research representation of the patent's master Gm. It is not the complete converter system and is not established as the manufactured AF-S NIKKOR 50mm f/1.4G prescription. The corresponding original A and B tables agree in all 17 master rows, including 16 curved interfaces and the explicit stop row.

The production-family correlation rests on three bounded observations:

1. Nikon specifies eight glass elements in seven groups. The patent master has three front singlets, a joined pair behind the stop and three rear singlets, consistent with the broad sequence of Nikon's section.
2. The master calculates to 51.5980519 mm, consistent with a nominal 50 mm family. This does not establish exact production radii or materials.
3. Nikon describes movement of all lens groups during focusing. No master-only finite spacing schedule, invariant-spacing proof, iris travel or precise source distance datum has been recovered.

The retail 2008 launch predates the converter patent. Chronology and a similar section are context, not factory identity proof. Retail f/1.4, 46° coverage, 0.45 m and 0.15× reproduction are not substituted for missing master inputs.

## Optical Architecture

The isolated Gm is an asymmetric Gaussian-family normal lens, a descriptive architectural interpretation rather than the patent's claimed converter design. It has eight physical glass lenses in seven components. A separately printed 0.0100 mm material layer between the joined glasses adds a ninth modeled material segment. Both equal-radius 154.1 mm boundaries are retained. The layer is not replaced by air or collapsed into one surface.

The stop remains at source row 7, 18.402 mm after the first vertex, with the literal 6.822 mm preceding and 9.6123 mm following distances. The total first-to-last master vertex track is 44.0843 mm. All master radii, internal thicknesses and nd/νd values remain unscaled and unchanged.

Source d17 = 3.0 mm in Example 1 is the master-to-converter space, not standalone back focus. The isolated research model uses a separately calculated Gaussian air back focus of 39.0174010578 mm. No real-ray best-focus adjustment has been made. The converter, compound-system plate, compound image distance, 14.25 mm image height and 21.840° half-field are not imported into the master.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

The segment labels M1–M9 below match the data file. M5 is the finite joining layer, not a ninth physical lens. Focal lengths below are isolated thick-segment-in-air calculations at the source d-line index. For joined components these are descriptive comparison values, not their in-situ powers or proof of the designer's aberration allocation.

### M1: Front positive meniscus

nd = 1.804000, νd = 46.57. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = +62.917 mm.

The stronger front curvature gives this first singlet positive power. It begins the converging front section. A particular manufacturer's glass or a specific aberration-correction claim cannot be established from the two published material coordinates.

### M2: Positive meniscus

nd = 1.903658, νd = 31.31. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = +86.719 mm.

This high-index, lower-Abbe singlet adds positive power before the negative pre-stop element. Its close preceding gap is retained at 0.15 mm. The palette permits chromatic balancing, but the source does not establish a supplier, melt or anomalous partial dispersion.

### M3: Pre-stop negative meniscus

nd = 1.755200, νd = 27.53. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = −36.724 mm.

The stronger rear curvature makes this a negative element immediately before the stop space. Its frozen outer extent is the geometry-bound 15.1 mm alternative, while the S6 optical radius remains 13.7 mm. The resulting working-aperture limitation is reported rather than removed by enlarging this element.

### M4–M6: Joined negative-positive component with an explicit layer

M4: nd = 1.755200, νd = 27.53. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = −22.106 mm.

M5: nd = 1.514000, νd = 42.83. Material: source-listed joining layer, chemistry unidentified. Isolated f is approximately +13.61 million mm for the two equal-radius boundaries and 0.0100 mm thickness.

M6: nd = 1.834807, νd = 42.71. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = +37.008 mm.

The negative front glass and positive rear glass form the patent's joined optical component behind the stop. The intervening material has nonzero thickness and its own refractions, even though its standalone power is very small. Its full physical annulus and chemistry are unknown. Neither numerical weakness nor the shared radii justify deleting it. The opposing powers and differing Abbe values support a qualitative chromatic-balancing interpretation; they do not prove exact secondary-spectrum behavior.

### M7: Rear positive meniscus

nd = 1.834807, νd = 42.71. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = +85.440 mm.

This is the first of three rear singlets. Its stronger rear curvature provides positive power. The source supplies its prescription, not a separate measured field-flattening or coma-correction allocation.

### M8: Rear positive meniscus

nd = 1.816000, νd = 46.62. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = +111.065 mm.

This weaker positive singlet shares the rear convergence with M7 and M9. The two adjacent air gaps remain the printed 0.18 mm and 0.10 mm values; no spacing was optimized.

### M9: Final positive biconvex lens

nd = 1.729157, νd = 54.68. Glass: unmatched source coordinates. Isolated f = +110.413 mm.

The final glass is lower in index and higher in Abbe number than the preceding rear singlets. It completes the master before the separately derived standalone image space. The source's converter separation has not been mistaken for this image space.

## Glass Identification and Selection

All nine modeled material segments use the exact source nd/νd coordinates. Seven distinct coordinate pairs occur: 1.804000/46.57, 1.903658/31.31, 1.755200/27.53, 1.514000/42.83, 1.834807/42.71, 1.816000/46.62 and 1.729157/54.68. Repeated coordinates do not establish shared melt or supplier identity.

No commercial glass has been substituted. The joining-layer label describes its position, not proven adhesive chemistry. Normal-line dispersion estimated from nd/νd supports qualified numerical photopic calculations, but does not recover partial dispersion, measured transmission, coatings or the response of a camera sensor. No apochromatic or measured-production-performance claim is made.

## Focus Mechanism

The authored model contains one infinity state. Its very large close-focus sentinel disables a meaningful finite-focus interpretation; it is not a physical minimum focusing distance. The manufacturer describes all-groups movement, but the master source gives no validated finite spacing schedule or iris-motion law. Conditional rigid-translation arithmetic in the earlier research is not authored as production focus behavior.

## Aspherical Surfaces

The master is represented by its spherical radii. No master aspheric schedule is supplied or inserted. The repeated 154.1 mm boundaries belong to the explicit material layer, not an aspheric or zero-thickness simplification.

## Geometry, Aperture and Field Qualifications

Clear radii and iris opening are not printed for the standalone master. Original A/B Figure 1 supplies an independently reviewed outline hypothesis; it is not metrology. Vertical scale and opaque-land interpretations remain conditional.

The nominal figure hypothesis crosses at S4–S5. The separate frozen geometry-only alternative reduces the paired M3 outer boundaries from 16.6 mm to 15.1 mm while retaining the 13.7 mm S6 optical cap. This is the largest 0.1 mm step preserving the unchanged 10% gap reserve. No gap-sag override, source-radius change or ray-driven enlargement was used. The S6/S8 opaque lands and limited joining-layer footprint remain modeled conventions, not recovered manufacture.

The nominal master f-number 1.4505060101 is inferred as compound f/1.015 divided by the rounded compound/master focal ratio 36.106/51.598. This assumes a shared physical iris and no downstream aperture taking control. It is neither a printed master aperture nor retail f/1.4. The frozen model transmits an on-axis working aperture of f/1.4554508153, limited by S5, a 0.340902% difference. The value is retained. The repository's 3% aperture reporting band is not an acceptance tolerance and does not erase this measured difference.

The modeled full-frame 21.65 mm image-height basis is an explicitly assumed family-format diagnostic. No source master-only field is available. The inferred corner chief is about 23.2045°; this is not a source field specification. Wide-open 256-grid corner transmission is approximately 31.30% in the unchanged cap hypothesis, not measured retail illumination.

The authored circular-iris settings extend through f/22. Nikon's retail manual specifies a minimum aperture of f/16. The f/22 entry is only a research diagnostic setting of this master model; it is not a factory detent or evidence that the retail lens closes to f/22.

## Verification Summary

The earlier independent source and geometry reviews remain bound to the unchanged input. Canonical validation, standalone typing, all nine modeled native solids and 36 pairwise checks passed within their stated scopes. These are not manufacturing-CAD claims for the unknown lands or layer annulus.

The reviewed wide-open continuation passed nine signed chiefs, 14 pupil cases, eleven reference-wavelength MTF fields and eleven estimated-photopic MTF fields. All reported MTF fields converged through at least 50 lp/mm at the fixed design plane. The compiled worker matched the native numerical results in a Node VM. These checks did not validate a browser, viewer route, production build or full repository integration.

The November 6 reserve adds bounded stopped-down aperture and pupil coverage without changing optics. Its detailed status, sampling limits and queued higher-density/MTF work are recorded in the accompanying reserve report. Preliminary sampling is not complete throughput convergence or release acceptance.

The A/B prose discrepancy between master 51.6 mm and the printed calculation using 51.5 mm is preserved; the table value 51.598 mm and all prescription numbers remain unchanged. Exact factory identity, physical caps and lands, joining-layer footprint, partial dispersion, finite focus and integration acceptance remain qualified or unresolved.
