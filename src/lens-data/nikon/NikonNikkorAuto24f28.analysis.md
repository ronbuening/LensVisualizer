# Nikkor-N Auto 24mm f/2.8

## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** US 3,622,227
**Inventor:** Yoshiyuki Shimizu
**Filed:** December 24, 1969
**Granted:** November 23, 1971
**Title:** Retrofocus-Type Wide-Angle Camera Lens
**Embodiment analyzed:** Example I, Fig. 1, normalized dimensions multiplied by 24

Example I supplies a nine-element, seven-group f/2.8 prescription with an 84°
field and normalized back focus of 1.5597. The source title page names the
inventor but does not list an assignee. The Nikon product association rests on
its matching architecture and Nikon's account identifying Shimizu as the basic
designer of the Nikkor-N Auto 24mm f/2.8. Mount metadata describes that product;
it is not a patent mount specification.

The original PDF has five pages. Figure 1 is on page 2 and Example I's table is
on page 4. Positive signs on r5, r6 and r13 are visible in the original table;
they are not speculative corrections based only on a focal-length fit.

## Optical Architecture

The optical sequence contains three front singlets, a cemented doublet, a
negative singlet, a second cemented doublet and a final positive singlet.
The first three elements form a net negative front section; the remaining six
form the positive rear section of the retrofocus architecture.

The focus groups have a different boundary. Nikon documents a moving separation
between L6 and L7, narrowing at close focus. The movement chart therefore uses
L1–L6 and L7–L9. The former optical-power grouping hid relative movement within
the rear section and was unsuitable for the motion chart.

The diaphragm lies between L5 and L6. The source gives their total gap, 0.1042
normalized, but no exact diaphragm coordinate. The midpoint placement divides
the scaled 2.5008 mm gap into two equal 1.2504 mm intervals.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

### L1 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.62041, νd = 60.3. Glass: N-SK16 catalog equivalent, supplier unspecified.
The authored isolated focal length is approximately −54 mm.

This large front element establishes divergence and the long back focus.
The source emphasizes the contribution of negative crown power to correction.
Its optical edge is distinguished from the large bevel in the drawing.

### L2 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.62041, νd = 60.3. Glass: N-SK16 catalog equivalent, supplier unspecified.
The authored isolated focal length is approximately +72.5 mm.

This positive singlet balances the front negative elements. Its two radii have
equal magnitude and opposite signs. The very short rear air space is published.

### L3 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.62041, νd = 60.3. Glass: N-SK16 catalog equivalent, supplier unspecified.

Its positive radii are 30 and 10.9992 mm at the chosen scale. The strongly
concave rear face supplies the negative power; its sign is directly sourced.

### L4 and L5 — First Cemented Doublet

L4: nd = 1.62004, νd = 36.3; F2 catalog equivalent, supplier unspecified.
L5: nd = 1.51823, νd = 59.0; BSC3 catalog comparison, supplier unspecified.
Their authored isolated focal lengths are approximately +17.3 and −76.7 mm.

A positive biconvex flint component is cemented to a negative meniscus crown.
The index step is −0.10181. Comparing L4's index with a nonadjacent N-SK16
component does not describe this cemented interface or neutralize its Petzval
contribution.

### L6 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.7847, inferred νd = 26.1; S-TIH23 catalog comparison, supplier unspecified.
The authored isolated focal length is approximately −29.9 mm.

Example I leaves this element's Abbe-number cell blank. The model retains 26.1
by assuming the same medium as L7, which has the identical published index and
an explicit Abbe number. This is an inference. Its bending and position help
control internal coma; the patent discusses it between the positive subgroups.

### L7 and L8 — Second Cemented Doublet

L7: nd = 1.7847, νd = 26.1; S-TIH23 catalog equivalent, supplier unspecified.
L8: nd = 1.62041, νd = 60.3; N-SK16 catalog equivalent, supplier unspecified.
Their authored isolated focal lengths are approximately −29.1 and +18.8 mm.

The positive-radius cemented interface has a negative index step. This is part
of the source's internal-coma correction strategy. The pair belongs to the rear
focus assembly together with L9.

### L9 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.744, νd = 44.9; LAF2 catalog comparison, supplier unspecified.
The authored isolated focal length is approximately +50.4 mm.

Its front face is weakly curved and its rear face is stronger. It supplies final
convergence after the preceding correction groups.

## Glass Identification and Dispersion

The source supplies reference indices and Abbe numbers, apart from L6's missing
Abbe entry. Catalog names indicate optical-coordinate comparisons, not confirmed
production suppliers. No chemical composition or exact partial-dispersion data
is established by these coordinates. Chromatic calculations use catalog curves
where compatible and model approximations elsewhere. No source APD claim is made.

## Focus Mechanism

Nikon's historical account confirms the L6/L7 separation and its narrowing
during close focus. It also identifies the 0.3 m focus-ring marking. Neither
that account nor this patent publishes the travel amounts used here.

The model retains an estimated 0.70 mm contraction. For that assumed contraction,
the rear image gap is solved through the complete paraxial prescription at
300 mm object-to-image distance, rather than using a thin-lens extension formula.

| Quantity | Infinity | Modeled 0.30 m |
|---|---|---|
| L6–L7 separation | 2.3496 mm | 1.6496 mm |
| Last surface to image | 37.4328 mm | 40.3567350 mm |
| Front assembly travel toward object | 0 | 2.2239350 mm |
| Rear assembly travel toward object | 0 | 2.9239350 mm |

The source infinity back focus is retained exactly after scaling. A paraxial
trace gives 37.43605 mm, within rounding of the source prescription. Calculated
EFL is 23.999607 mm. Intermediate travel and distance labels are approximate;
there is no zoom. The model demonstrates the documented topology without
claiming an exact production cam schedule.

## Diagram Dimensions and Verification

The 600 dpi drawing's approximately 1,249-pixel displayed first-to-last vertex
span corresponds to a 54.252 mm assembly. Its large front optical half-height
is about 500 pixels and the second singlet about 400. The current front rims
retain ray allowances; the second singlet is limited to 15.9 mm by the adjacent
front-element sag. A 17.5 mm trial failed the cross-gap constraint and was rejected.

Rear-doublet shared/exit rims increase to 10 mm, and L9's front rim increases
to 11.5 mm, matching their relative heights in the drawing. Other rims remain
within figure/geometry allowances. These are inferred dimensions, not published
clear apertures. All powered surfaces are spherical. The stop-down range is a
visualization setting beyond the source's f/2.8 specification.
