## Optical Architecture

Five motion groups contain L1–L6, L7, L8–L12, L13 and L14–L16. Their published focal lengths are +89.30, −69.14, +116.43, +126.75 and −71.11 mm. These five motion groups differ from the 14 air-separated components. Cemented pairs L4/L5 and L9/L10 account for the difference between element and component counts.

The stop follows L5 and precedes the single asphere on L6. Figure 1 also uses GF for the pre-stop group and GR for the following optics; those are not the five focus-group boundaries. Source surface 10 is displayed as STO.

The optical rims are inferred from Figure 1 at 600 dpi using the 133.0137 mm first-to-last-surface span. The front radius is about 37.9 mm; L6 is about 21.8 mm and the rear elements about 21.1 mm. These are drawing measurements, not published clear apertures. Surface 29 retains a 19.3 mm radius because extending both sides of the last air gap to 21.1 mm violates surface-clearance limits. The larger drawn edge is therefore not treated as a validated usable optical aperture. Stop size is calibrated from the source f-number.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The patent gives nd and νd, together with anomalous-dispersion conditions. It does not name production glasses. The catalog counterparts used for spectral modeling are J-SFH4 (L1), J-FKH1 (L2–L4), NBFD25 (L5/L9), N-BK7 (L6), S-NPH1 (L8), J-PSKH1 (L10), S-LAH63Q (L11), S-LAH55VS (L12), NBFD30 (L13), S-LAH51 (L14), S-TIH53 (L15) and J-LASF09A (L16). L7 remains vendor-unresolved at 1.69680/55.52. Numerical source coordinates remain authoritative; matching a catalog coordinate does not identify the production supplier or establish a product ED/SR classification.

Conditions 14 and 17 on original PDF pp. 56–57 give departures +0.035 and +0.010 from the patent normal line 0.6415−0.00162ν. The engine uses 0.6438−0.001682ν, so the stored departures are +0.0343957 for L1 and +0.0092593 for L5/L9. These conversions preserve the source values to their published three-decimal precision; they do not create a more precise measurement. The inferred L8 catalog dispersion supplies its curve without a copied approximate departure overriding it.

The finite station independently gives approximately −0.20001 magnification and 98.82 mm paraxial EFL. Its object-to-image distance is inferred as about 0.82455 m, including the physical thickness of the source rear filter. Intermediate slider distances and group positions are interpolated estimates, not additional published focus stations.

## Aperture and Image Formation

The nominal aperture and first shortcut are f/1.85. The product's f/1.8 designation is retained in its name. The stop stays fixed during focusing; finite-conjugate effective aperture and pupil readouts are calculated quantities. A pupil estimate should not be interpreted as an independently published patent measurement.

The source rear stack is 11.4681 mm air, 1.6000 mm glass at nd=1.51680 and 1.3712 mm air. Omitting that plate requires a final air-equivalent spacing of 13.8941523 mm, not the 14.4393 mm physical sum. This preserves paraxial back focus while omitting the filter's higher-order and chromatic effects. The physical total length is approximately 147.453 mm; the model's equivalent air track is 146.908 mm.

## Model Limitations

The prescription, conic conversion and focus gap endpoints are source-based. Semi-diameters, catalog material identities, the finite distance inferred from the matrix and intermediate focus states are explicitly reconstructed. The drawing does not establish production vignetting, bokeh performance, coatings or actuators. Surface clearance and image-circle checks constrain the inferred rims; they do not replace measurements of a manufactured lens.
