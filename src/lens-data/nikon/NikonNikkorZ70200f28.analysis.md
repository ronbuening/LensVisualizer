# NIKKOR Z 70–200 mm f/2.8 VR S — Patent Model

## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** WO 2020/105104 A1

**Inventor:** Takeru Uehara

**Assignee:** Nikon Corporation

**Filed:** 2018-11-20

**Published:** 2020-05-28

**Embodiment analyzed:** Example 1, Figure 1 and Table 1

**Title:** Variable Magnification Optical System, Optical Device, and Production Method for Variable Magnification Optical System

The diagram associates this example with the NIKKOR Z 70–200 mm f/2.8 VR S
through its 21 elements, 18 air-separated components, two aspheres, low-dispersion
front elements and opposing focus groups. This correspondence does not prove
identical production geometry, glasses or actuators. The numerical example
is the authority; the product designation remains separate.

Table 1 is on original PDF pp. 22–25. Its infinity focal lengths are
71.50119, 135 and 196 mm; the viewer's wide station rounds this to 71.5 mm.
The source f-numbers are 2.88277,2.8637 and 2.87906. The aperture control
preserves these station values, while the summary rounds the design to f/2.88.
The full fields are 33.79332°,17.81742° and 12.27158°, with image height 21.70 mm.

## Optical Architecture

The nine optical groups have powers positive–negative–positive–positive–negative–
positive–negative–positive–negative. The source's nine motion groups differ
from the 18 physical components: the three cemented doublets reduce 21 glass
elements to 18 air-separated units.

During wide-to-tele zoom, G2 and G3 move toward the image, and G5 moves with
the stop. G7 and G8 also change position along the source's reversing zoom
paths. G1, G4, G6 and G9 remain fixed (¶0082). During focus, only G7 and G8
move, in opposite directions (¶0092). This is an internal-zoom prescription.

The diaphragm is source surface 20,3.50 mm before L51. It is part of G5,
not an independently moving stop. The groups and doublet labels follow the
patent's surface boundaries.

## Glass Identification and Dispersion

Table 1 publishes nd and νd without naming commercial glasses. The viewer's
catalog names identify dispersion counterparts; they do not establish a
production supplier, proprietary recipe or manufacturing process.

The six 1.49782/82.57 elements use **J-FKH1**, an exact coordinate counterpart,
instead of the former S-FPL51 approximation. L81 uses **J-LASFH9**, whose
index matches 1.90265 and whose catalog Abbe value is close to 35.77. The old
J-LASFH9A token did not resolve and caused the less close S-LAH93 fallback.
L62 uses an explicitly approximate **J-PSKH4** counterpart. No glass-molding
claim follows from these coordinate matches.

L23 is different: the patent directly gives θgF=0.6319 at nd 1.66382/νd 27.35.
Its stored rounded ΔPgF=0.0341 represents this anomalous partial dispersion.
J-SFH4 supplies an inferred compatible spectral model; the patent does not
establish a proprietary production recipe or branded SR classification. Other APD badges on
low-dispersion glasses are catalog-inferred, not source-listed identities.

## Focus and Zoom Mechanisms

The source gaps are retained at all three zoom stations and both conjugates.
G7 moves imageward and G8 objectward during close focus. Their displacement
magnitudes depend on zoom; equal-and-opposite travel occurs at the tele end,
not throughout the range.

| Station | G7 imageward | G8 objectward | Close magnification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wide | 1.28505 mm | 2.24883 mm | −0.08318 |
| Middle | 5.66574 mm | 5.94902 mm | −0.14416 |
| Tele | 11.52543 mm | 11.52543 mm | −0.19832 |

Independent reduced-angle propagation through the full prescription gives
close object-to-image distances 1000.008,1000.010 and 999.466 mm. These agree
with an approximately **1.0 m** source conjugate. The former 0.5 m slider
label did not describe these published gaps. The gaps were preserved and
the endpoint label corrected; no production minimum-focus value was substituted.
The source magnifications are reproduced to their listed precision.

The three focus gaps sum to approximately 35.72963 mm at each station,
within rounding. Fixed groups and the image plane stay stationary while
the focus groups rearrange within this envelope. Intermediate focus and zoom
states are model reconstructions, not additional patent stations. The source
does not establish the displayed model's motor type.

### Source Total-Length Inconsistency

The listed surface gaps sum to approximately 232.43316 mm including the
constant 32.5469 mm back focal distance. Table 1 instead prints TL199.88619 mm,
which equals the sum excluding BF to source rounding. The definition on
PDF p. 18 explicitly says TL includes BF. Thus the table's TL summary conflicts
with its definition and detailed prescription. The viewer retains the detailed
spacings and calculated 232.433 mm track; it does not rescale the lens to
force agreement with the inconsistent summary.

## Aspherical Surfaces

Equation (B), ¶0076 on PDF p. 19, uses κ directly in 1−κy²/R² under the square root.
The standard viewer convention uses 1+K, so **K=κ−1**. Source κ0 therefore
means K−1 at both aspheres. The previous K0 interpretation was incorrect.

| Surface | Source κ | Viewer K | A4 | A6 | A8 | A10 | A12 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 26 | 0 | −1 | −2.00e−6 | 8.31e−10 | −6.83e−12 | 2.63e−14 | −3.55e−17 |
| 37 | 0 | −1 | 1.18e−6 | 1.63e−9 | −7.32e−12 | 2.41e−14 | −2.65e−17 |

The polynomial terms match the source exactly and are not refitted. The
source does not identify a polishing, molding or hybrid manufacturing process.

## Diagram Dimensions and Limits

Figure 1 on PDF p. 64 was inspected at 600 dpi across its three zoom drawings.
The 199.886 mm first-to-last vertex span calibrates the optical rims, separate
from the inconsistent TL label. Rims are estimated, not tabulated clear apertures.
The front group is approximately 35–36.5 mm in semi-diameter; the variator
narrows from 24 to about 20 mm. G5 and G6 are approximately 17–17.5 mm, while
G8 is about 18 mm. The old 12.5 mm G8 radius made this focus element too small
relative to the neighboring fixed rear elements.

The revised geometry respects the exact surfaces and clearance constraints.
A 20 mm trial at surface 12 caused 11→12 gap intrusion 6.05 mm, exceeding the
4.95 mm allowance, so its prior 18.1 mm rim was retained. All accepted rims
pass surface and image-circle checks. Fifteen sampled zoom/focus combinations
render without hidden rim trimming. Optical outlines, not labels or leaders,
were used for the comparison.
