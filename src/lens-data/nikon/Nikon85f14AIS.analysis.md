## Optical Architecture

L1 and L2 are positive menisci. L3 combines a biconvex and biconcave medium into a negative cemented component. The stop precedes the second cemented component L4, followed by positive singlet L5. The two cemented components and their medium boundaries remain intact.

The patent discusses forward power distribution, a diverging air lens and the cemented L3 interface as mechanisms for controlling spherical aberration and its wavelength dependence. All surfaces are spherical. The qualitative element roles below are distinguished from isolated-medium focal lengths, which do not represent powers within cemented assemblies.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

### L1 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.77279, νd = 49.4. Glass: N-LAF34 (Schott, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +105.1 mm.

Front positive meniscus, convex toward object. Provides initial convergence through the full f/1.4 beam. High-index lanthanum glass limits curvatures to control high-order aberrations.

### L2 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.69680, νd = 55.6. Glass: N-LAK14 (Schott, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +81.2 mm.

Second positive meniscus (φ₂ > φ₁). Higher power than L1, immediately preceded by a divergent air lens. Lower dispersion (vd=55.6) reduces chromatic contributions from this stronger element.

### L3a — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.78470, νd = 26.1. Glass: SF11 (Schott, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +89.9 mm.

Front element of cemented negative doublet L3. Dense flint chosen for g-line spherical aberration control via patent conditions (4)–(6).

### L3b — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.75520, νd = 27.5. Glass: E-FD4 (HOYA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ -25.9 mm.

Rear element of L3 doublet. Nearly matched Abbe number to L3a (Δvd=1.4) creates wavelength-selective negative spherical aberration at the cemented surface, counteracting g-line over-correction. Rear surface r7 (+23.6 mm) is the steepest in the system.

### L4a — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.58144, νd = 40.8. Glass: E-FL5 (HOYA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ -38.6 mm.

Front (negative) element of rear doublet L4. Low-index light flint; the concave-concave shape provides the rear Gauss negative corrector function.

### L4b — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.74443, νd = 49.4. Glass: NBF1 (HOYA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +36.8 mm.

Rear (positive) element of L4. Same glass as L5. Doublet net power is very weak positive (fl ≈ +448 mm); L4 functions primarily as an aberration corrector.

### L5 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.74443, νd = 49.4. Glass: NBF1 (HOYA, inferred coordinate counterpart). Isolated-in-air focal length ≈ +107.7 mm.

Rear positive singlet. Same source glass coordinates as L4b. Provides final convergence. Separated from L4 by the CRC variable gap d10, which enlarges at close focus for aberration correction.

The source provides indices and Abbe numbers, not commercial names. The named counterparts above are coordinate-compatible catalog inferences, without historical supplier or chemistry claims. L4b and L5 have identical source coordinates; this does not prove a manufacturing-logistics rationale.
