**Filed:** 3 September 1993  
**Granted:** 25 June 1996  
**Inventors:** Atsushi Shibayama; Susumu Sato  
**Assignee:** Nikon Corporation  
**Title:** Zoom Lens  
**Example details:** First embodiment, Table 1 and Figure 3. Source millimetres retained.

This is a Grade B probable family reconstruction associated with the Nikon Zoom 700 VR, also marketed as Zoom-Touch 105 VR QD. The patent frontpage spells the second inventor “Susumo Sato”; the same publication’s recorded assignment identifies SATO, SUSUMU, supporting the existing canonical catalog spelling Susumu Sato. The 12-element/10-group construction, paired-doublet topology, centered stabilization subgroup and rear focusing group agree with Nikon service manual FCA25001-R.3355.A, page M11. These are one physical family rather than separate alias counts; exact production radii and cams are not established. The source 39–102 mm, f/4.08–8.05 and 58.20°/23.32° endpoint full fields remain separate from the retail 38–105 mm f/4–7.8.

## Optical Architecture

Three front singlets form G1. G2 contains front and rear three-element subgroups, each including one cemented doublet; the iris lies between the subgroups. The rear G2 subgroup is centered here and is correlated with the service manual’s VR assembly. Three rear singlets form G3, held at its source infinity setting. Source surface 12 explicitly marks the stop with 2 mm air gaps on both sides.

## Focus Mechanism and Zoom States

Only the three printed infinity prescriptions are modeled, with linear interpolation between published zoom stations. Intermediate gap positions are inferred and do not establish an infinity-focused or parfocal factory cam. Design-plane curves retain any resulting defocus. The separate bounded best-axial diagnostic changes only the chart calculation, not an authored gap; Patent positions mode selects the three printed configurations. The source gaps d6/d17/BF are 3.2347/15.0298/8.9728 mm, 10.3035/7.2691/31.0851 mm and 17.0677/3.1954/48.8923 mm. They give source image coordinates 70.9373/92.3577/112.8554 mm relative to the first vertex. The computed first-order EFLs are 39.0017983691/70.0051626711/102.0096840915 mm. No computed best-focus plane replaces any source gap.

The service manual supports rear focusing and VR subgroup identity but does not certify a continuous finite conjugate, motor cam or stabilizer decenter law for these numbers. Canonical closeFocusM = 1e15 makes this an infinity-only reconstruction.

## Stop and Clear-Aperture Assumptions

The independent endpoint paraxial iris estimates are 4.9897818942 and 4.9883956049 mm, a 0.028% difference consistent with the table’s two-decimal FNo rounding. One fixed iris, sized at wide f/4.08, is modeled across zoom. The middle f/6.4032461643 is calculated, not printed; the tele label f/8.05 remains the rounded source value. This is an explicit reconstruction assumption, not a measured camera iris. The f/16 endpoint is a modeled control range, not a sourced minimum retail aperture.

Clear SDs are inferred as the larger of a 10% paraxial axial allowance and a 5% paraxial source-H21.6 chief floor, maximized over the three source stations and rounded upward to 0.5 mm. Figure 3 and service M11 confirm topology and taper but are schematic. The first S21 cap of 12 mm fails the actual 4 mm air-gap guard. Before pupil or MTF calculations it was reduced to 11 mm, admitting 3.5917215115 mm intrusion under the unchanged 3.6 mm guard. This sacrifices some initial paraxial allowance and retains the resulting native losses. No aperture was enlarged after tracing.

Exact operating-disk cap checks pass at every sampled opening. At wide f/4.08, the printed endpoint pupil fields 0°/14.55°/29.1° retain 209/155/35 rays out of 209; the losses are recorded by source surface. They describe this inferred aperture branch, not measured camera vignetting. Certified pre-stop blocks and downstream clipping are retained separately, including front S1 and internal cemented/air surfaces.

## Modeled Diffraction and Coverage

The native scalar sheared-pupil estimator records 63 converged fields across 21 aperture/zoom requests at five zoom states and six sampled frequencies 0/10/20/30/40/50 lp/mm. Maximum consecutive-grid contrast delta is 0.0099174687; the largest final grid is 128. All final MTF fields have zero failed/unaimed rays and zero unknown flux, with clipping retained. These numerical gates apply to sampled frequencies and this estimator, not continuous-frequency, independent optical-phase or factory accuracy. No withdrawn one-dimensional OPL reference is used.

The MTF audit uses the native 21.65 mm format reference with traced field mapping at each zoom. The table’s H = 21.6 mm and exact 36×24 mm corner radius 21.6333076528 mm are separate quantities. Printed endpoint half-fields are checked by the pupil audit; no middle source half-field is invented. Format-reference field angles therefore differ from source angles and any excess is a modeled extrapolation. All calculations retain the source design plane and apply no refocus.
