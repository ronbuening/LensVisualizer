# Canon New FD 200mm f/4 (June 1979)

This is the non-macro 7-element/6-group rear-focus redesign, not the earlier 6/5 FD/SSC lens or the 1981 1:1 macro.

## Production match

[Canon's product record](https://global.canon/en/c-museum/product/nfd224.html) and an [independently archived production cutaway](https://mir.com.my/rb/photography/companies/canon/fdresources/fdlenses/200mmf4optic.jpg) corroborate [Kikuo Momiyama's US4240703 Example I](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/4240703). One scale fitted only to stationary vertices plus one rigid translation of the sole moving doublet aligns all 13 axial positions (0.339 native px RMS, 0.822 px maximum over 118.8 px). Exact retail focus setting of the cutaway is unknown. Examples II and III differ in element layout. The native drawing is not a source for clear aperture dimensions.

## Reconstructed focus and validation

- The signed normalized radii, glass, and gaps use one scale of 200 mm. At infinity the d-line focal length is 200.893803 mm and BFD is 61.155968 mm.
- Example I also specifies D8 and D11 at a nominal 9.13f. At 1.826 m from the image plane, replay on the fixed 163.995968 mm frame shows just +20.631 µm of paraxial residual. Treating it as distance from the first surface instead gives −2.184 mm; that interpretation is rejected. The matrix EFL at this station is 169.443183 mm rather than the printed 168.78 mm after normalization; no glass or radius was altered.
- Both published states are preserved. Linear doublet travel between them is a model of the movement, not a measured focus cam or proof of continuous in-focus conjugates. The production lens's 1.5 m minimum is not asserted for this model.
- Iris station 59.4 mm from R1 and clear semi-diameters are estimates. The scope exercised in native validation is f/4 on-axis and f/8 through ±3° at infinity, plus the signed finite station on-axis at f/4 and f/8. The additional 284-sample diagnostic at infinity found 270 surviving at f/4 and 2°, and 258 at f/4 and 3°. The counts reflect model estimates, not measured retail illumination.
- No edge trim or geometry override. UI, measured factory illumination, full-field image quality, broadband or diffraction performance not qualified.
