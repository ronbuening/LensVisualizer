# Audit Log — Canon EF 200mm f/1.8 L USM

Patent: JP H01-102413 A, Numerical Example 1.

## 2026-10-30 — Original-PDF shoulder and source spectral evidence

### Exact publication and native evidence

Fresh inspection used 'patents/JPA 1989102413-000000.pdf', the April 20, 1989 publication JP H01-102413 A,
Hideki Ogawa / Sadatoshi Takahashi, Canon. Numerical Example 1 is on PDF page 5 (printed page 75), including all
18 powered surface rows and infinity/2.5 m variable gaps. The introduction above that table defines Ni and νi.
Fig. 1 is the lower-left section on PDF page 7 (printed page 77); it was rotated 90° for an object-left comparison.
That page's correspondence table gives Example-1 group/conditional values and its two published partial ratios.
The generic partial-ratio condition and definitions appear on PDF page 3 (printed page 73).

These are native 2866 × 3895-pixel scans at approximately 347 dpi. Native numerical/Abbe crops, the partial table,
source definitions and Fig. 1 were inspected directly; lower-resolution whole-sheet context identifies each region.
No upsample is counted as additional detail. In the rotated Fig. 1, R1–R18 vertices span approximately 810 pixels
for 162.41 mm, about 5.0 native px/mm. Two-pixel readings correspond to approximately ±0.4 mm before schematic
drawing uncertainty. R/D leaders, unit brackets and axis/break marks are excluded from optical rim measurements.

The native R10 cap/edge reading is roughly 144/164 pixels, q ≈0.878 ±0.012 using one-pixel endpoint uncertainty;
its optical interpretation also carries the figure's schematic uncertainty. The adopted outer radius follows R9's
existing 36.5 mm inferred height, preserving the working 31.3 mm cap. The schematic axial scale suggests an outer
height nearer 33 mm, so 36.5 mm is not asserted as a measured patent dimension. The feature records a resolved
shoulder while retaining the prior ray/geometry-constrained front cap. R10's sphere cannot be extended optically to
36.5 mm beyond |R| = 34.941 mm; 'rimExtensionSd' preserves the existing valid cap rather than enlarging its aperture.

All ten elements, their concave ends and both bonded interfaces (R12/R15) were inspected. Other square-looking edge
strokes do not resolve a separate cap endpoint from a shoulder. No equal-height block, bonded-interface land, resin
wing or inferred cut is invented. Source R/d, spherical surfaces and focus controls are retained. The native section
supports the existing relative heights within the constrained reconstruction; a complete optical-production blank is
not specified by the drawing. No 'rimCutDepthSource' exemption is introduced.

The strict infinity aperture proposal requires R5 = 46.1 mm instead of 44.8. That trial fails the unchanged R4–R5
intrusion guard: 5.70 mm sag versus the 5.400 mm allowance across a 6.000 mm gap. Physical clearance remains positive,
but the project policy is not relaxed. A separate R2 = 57.0 mm trial is geometrically legal and removes that face's
finite edge clip, but shifts the same full-iris axial samples to R5 without settling the constrained beam; it is not
applied. A cut or unproved shoulder is not used to bypass either outcome. The R10 extension passes normal edge,
stop and cross-gap checks throughout focus without any tolerance change.

### Controls and sampled runtime limits

Original D10 = 21.00/35.49 mm and D13 = 32.20/17.71 mm are retained. The working stop still splits D13 into a
variable 24.20/9.71 mm part plus a fixed 8.00 mm part before R14; neither its coordinate nor its radius is patent-
dimensioned. G2's 14.49 mm motion and the independently derived fixed image gap remain unchanged. At the close source
row, a paraxial object-distance solution gives about 2499.576 mm to the image plane and m ≈−0.090896, consistent with
the printed 2.5 m state. This derived object point is a runtime input, not an additional source spacing.

Fresh actual SVGs at infinity, middle and close states match the shoulder and contain ten finite closed element paths,
with zero hidden renderer trim across all 21 positions of a 0.05 focus grid. Fixed-stop aimed sampling uses 49 pupil
points at axial, 0.6-field and corner fields; finite fields use the current paraxial conjugate and image-format height.
All sampled chiefs clear and every sample is aimed. Full-iris rays are not wholly clear: axial survivor counts at the
two endpoint layouts are 45/49, and corner counts are 25/49. Some off-axis samples first clip at the R15 bonded
interface; no opaque land is added there to conceal that limitation. The ordinary infinity aperture census is within
its 3% gate and image-circle/full-frame-chief checks pass, but those passes do not certify full-aperture transmission.
Older R5/R10 excess numbers at the close spacing refer to collimated input and are now distinguished from the proper
finite-object trace. There is no inferred cut to exempt from its full beam check. Shape fidelity, sampled clearance
and correlation with the production lens, including its unmodeled protective/filter plates, remain separate results.

## 2026-10-31 — d-line inferred from printed coordinates

Scope: reference-line classification and catalog curves only. No source pair, geometry, rim, stop or control changed, and no new whole-lens review is claimed.

The patent names no spectral line for its index and Abbe columns, and the 2026-10-30 review marked every medium `Unmatched` for that reason. Under the coordinate test in `src/lens-data/LENS_DATA_SPEC.md`, 10 of 10 printed pairs match a catalog nd/νd within |Δn| ≤ 6e-5 and |Δν| ≤ 0.15, and none matches a catalog ne/νe. The table is therefore read as d-line, and the per-element `indexReferenceNote` fields are removed.

10 media carry qualified catalog curves. Rows keep the earlier selection where it is still an exact match. This patent dates from 1996 or earlier, so lead-silicate rows replace lead-free rows where the catalog has one at the printed pair. Catalog-copied C/F/g indices and dPgF stay removed. Supplier, melt and chemistry stay unspecified.
