## Optical Architecture

An asymmetric wide-angle design with a negative front meniscus and two positive-power functional blocks around the stop. Its seven elements form five air-separated groups. The first cemented pair and long front air space are characteristic; the rear block combines a negative single, a second cemented pair and a positive final single.

The table stop is retained at its source axial station. The full clear-aperture table is inferred rather than printed, and is frozen independently of MTF contrast. No camera-side plate or protective glass is invented.

Raw source pupils contain 64 engine failures, all already certified blocked by aiming, and zero unaimed rays. These overlapping categories are retained rather than added as separate losses. The reference MTF records retain clipping and do not enlarge inferred apertures to pass. Wide-open valid samples at the three source fields are 209/163/75 out of209 each.

The clipping belongs to this frozen aperture hypothesis and must not be attributed to the patent or production lens. No cap was enlarged to remove it.
