## Optical Architecture

Eight air-spaced elements form four units with power sequence **negative L1 / positive L2 / positive LP / negative L4**. L1 contains the negative G1/G2 pair and positive L1P; L2 is the positive–negative–positive triplet L2a/L2b/L2c; LP is the single positive inner-focus member; L4 is the final weak negative asphere. Source unit focal lengths are −40.14 / +39.51 / +62.75 / −185.51 mm. The architecture has a negative lead; source BF remains shorter than focal length at both ends, so it is not assigned a strict retrofocus classification.

Paragraph 0070, PDF page 19 / printed 5, places L1 on an image-convex zoom trajectory, reversing near the middle. L2 and L4 move together toward the object; LP floats between them. The literal infinity gap sums d14+d16 are 21.28/21.29/21.28 mm, preserving the 0.01 mm middle rounding difference. Only the three infinity stations are published. Current linear zoom interpolation and the calculated close-focus cam are research-model controls, not additional source stations. IS remains centered.

The source explicitly includes both the aperture plane (S13) and flare-stop plane FP (S14). Neither stop has a published diameter. Existing derived iris and FP radius remain unchanged. No filter, rear plate, bonded interface or hybrid layer is listed.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

### G1 — Front negative meniscus

**nd = 1.63854, νd = 55.4; qualified S-BSM18 spectral proxy; isolated f ≈ −31.45 mm.** Its shallow front and strong positive rear curvature provide most of L1's negative power. The source figure shows a connected outer optical blank beyond the rear cap: clear S2 remains 14.4 mm, while `rimExtensionSd=18.8` reproduces the resolved shoulder. The native curve endpoint is about 14.8 mm, within measurement uncertainty. Catalog chemistry and supplier are not established by the coordinates.

### G2 — Aspherical negative meniscus

**nd = 1.53110, νd = 55.9; Unmatched medium; isolated f ≈ −112.73 mm.** Its front S3A carries the positive even-polynomial departure on a weak base radius. Paragraph 0062 permits resin for G2 to reduce weight; it does not identify the selected numerical material, COP, a trade name or a molding method. S3A clear 14.0 mm remains gap limited; S4's rear cap increases to the native 15.0 mm endpoint. A full front 15 mm trial fails the existing adjacent-gap constraint, as recorded below.

### L1P — Positive member in L1

**nd = 1.69895, νd = 30.1; qualified S-TIM35 spectral proxy; isolated f ≈ +67.52 mm.** Its positive power and relatively low Abbe number balance the negative pair's power and dispersion. Native optical rims support clear 14.3 mm on both faces; the source drawing resolves their equal heights, rather than an inferred equal-height block.

### L2a — Positive front of the IS unit

**nd = 1.90366, νd = 31.3; qualified S-LAH95 spectral proxy; isolated f ≈ +23.29 mm.** This strong positive member provides convergence ahead of the stop. Its retained 6.4 mm caps agree with the native source rim.

### L2b — Negative middle of the IS unit

**nd = 1.84666, νd = 23.9; qualified S-NPH53 spectral proxy; isolated f ≈ −16.45 mm.** This is the lowest source Abbe number and provides opposing dispersive power within L2. Its S10 clear 4.9 mm is retained beside the 0.37 mm S10/S11 gap; a source-outline-sized 5.4 mm trial fails the unchanged gap allowance. No opaque land is invented at the nearby air faces.

### L2c — Positive rear of the IS unit

**nd = 1.77250, νd = 49.6; qualified S-LAH66 spectral proxy; isolated f ≈ +25.80 mm.** It completes the net-positive triplet, with retained unequal 5.8/5.2 mm caps. Native uncertainty and the adjacent-gap constraint do not justify equalizing them.

### LP — Positive inner-focus meniscus

**nd = 1.48749, νd = 70.2; qualified S-FSL5 spectral proxy; f ≈ +62.75 mm.** The source concave object-side face reduces incidence angles of oblique rays, as discussed in paragraphs 0067–0068. Its clear caps become 9.3/9.3 mm from the native curve endpoints. The catalog proxy's density or chemistry does not identify the historical focus medium.

### L4 — Final negative asphere

**nd = 1.53110, νd = 55.9; Unmatched medium; f ≈ −185.51 mm.** Both faces are aspherical. The native front short vertical stroke is attached to the optical blank, followed by the curved cap, with no mechanical bracket or detached flange. Clear S17A remains 11.8 mm and gains outer 13.5; the rear S18A clear cap becomes 13.5 mm. Equal coordinates to G2 establish neither equal chemistry nor one molding resin.
