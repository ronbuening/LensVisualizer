## Optical Architecture

Two front positive fluorite singlets precede a cemented correction doublet, a long stop-containing airspace, a rear negative singlet and a final positive singlet. These are six powered elements in five air-spaced groups and eleven refracting interfaces. The front and rear functional units have opposite net powers. All faces are spherical.
