### Controls and sampled optical behavior

Source D6 = 3.97 mm at infinity, D12 = 0.73 mm, D13 = 3.50 mm and the complete L2 translation are retained.
No finite gap row is published. The close D6 = 0.992301 mm / BF = 41.365448 mm state remains an explicitly
paraxial 0.25 m reconstruction, not a patent or factory cam. The finite paraxial matrix independently recovers
250.000 mm object-to-image distance and m ≈−0.136334 at that endpoint. The source's bare 2500-distance plot label
does not establish the distance datum or the 0.25 m endpoint. Aperture f/2.9, declared 94° field, six-blade metadata
and all controls are retained; the iris has no authored radius and derives from the design f-number.

Fresh actual SVG comparison at infinity/middle/close focus shows eleven connected finite closed element paths;
all 21 positions at 0.05 focus steps have zero hidden renderer trim. The three zoom positions repeat this prime's
same prescription. The canonical surface/cross-gap checks pass. The strict infinity f/2.9 axial aperture census
needs no raise; image-circle check finds no undersized modeled format. The exact 47° declared field reaches about
21.53/21.65 mm (99%) half-diagonal, while a separate full-frame corner chief clears at about 47.2°. That real-ray
coverage is distinct from the paraxial 93.1° source-design image-circle arithmetic.

Stop-aimed samples use 49 pupil points at axial, 0.6-field and full-field positions over all 21 focus states.
The infinity angle is source half-field 46.55°; finite fields use current matrix-derived conjugates and a nominal
21.64 mm image-format height. Finite chiefs are first root-solved through the stop center and then passed to the
shared pupil aiming helper. All samples are aimed, every axial pupil sample passes, and every sampled chief clears.
Corner survivors are 21/49 at infinity and 26/49 at close focus. Some peripheral samples first clip at S15's bonded
interface; no opaque interface feature conceals that limitation. This is sampled runtime clearance, not complete
peripheral transmission proof. There is no inferred cut requiring a beam exemption. Production correlation remains
qualified, including the source filing after the marketed lens's introduction.
