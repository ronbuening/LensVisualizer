## Optical Architecture

The first table is a four-unit **positive–negative–positive–negative** telephoto zoom. Its rear B4 is divided into B4a and B4b. This differs from the five-unit layout emphasized in the later claims and narrative embodiment; those claims do not replace the selected first numerical table.

| B1   | 1–5             | L1–L3    | +93.52 mm              | Front positive collector, singlet plus cemented doublet             |
| B2   | 6–10            | L4–L6    | −21.90 mm              | Negative zoom variator, doublet plus singlet                        |
| B3   | 11–23           | L7–L13   | +26.02 mm              | Positive relay containing aperture, asphere and centered IS doublet |
| B4a  | 24–26           | L14–L15  | −33.93 mm              | Negative focus doublet                                              |
| B4b  | 27–29           | L16–L17  | −4057.03 mm            | Nearly afocal rear cemented correction pair                         |

At infinity B1–B4 move objectward toward tele; B4a and B4b follow the same trajectory (¶¶0027–0028). The source's three infinity rows keep D26=12.62 mm. The aperture is surface16, between the front positive portion of B3 and its aspheric element. B3b, surfaces19–21, is the IS doublet (¶0026).

The final source surfaces30–31 are a camera-side optical block:1.50 mm, nd1.54400, νd60.0, followed by1.73 mm physical air. These are retained through `rearPlates`, not counted among the17 lenses. Source BF and total length are air-equivalent conventions (¶¶0039,0063), so the physical model's track is approximately0.5285 mm longer than its air-equivalent value, before source rounding. Neither quantity is a barrel-length measurement.

## Focus Mechanism and Inferred Zoom Motion

Paragraph0028 specifies **B4a imageward translation** for near focus. Only D23 and D26 change with focus; their sum is preserved at each zoom. B4b and all other units retain their infinity zoom positions. The manufacturer MFD is1m, measured object to image; the source does not print finite-focus spacing rows.

Nine focus controls per zoom are solved paraxially at inverse-distance coordinates0,1/8,…,1. The close endpoint reproduces the assumed1m conjugate; calculated maximum B4a travel is7.291073 mm. Native-station paraxial magnifications are−0.061023/−0.140506/−0.205011, with the tele value close to the marketed0.21×. This consistency is not proof of a production focus cam. Finite MTF remains unavailable because no `focus.conjugates` are source-certified.

The source's native infinity rows retain their rounding residuals: +0.021100/−0.004530/+0.017269 mm at wide/mid/tele. Focus labels are estimates between controls. Across129 zoom ×17 focus samples, the largest sampled finite object-distance discrepancy from the inverse-distance label is4.83%, at the wide-end0.0625 focus coordinate (label16m, paraxial conjugate16.773m). Exact source infinity anchors are not altered to hide this limit.

Three native zoom rows interpolated linearly leave up to1.644706 mm sampled intermediate paraxial defocus. A bounded reconstruction therefore adds rigid B4 infinity translation, changing D23 and D29 oppositely while preserving D26 and total track. It uses no MTF-based cam optimization. Tested control densities3/9/17/33/65 gave maximum sampled absolute infinity residuals1.644706/0.336924/0.100041/0.025871/0.021100 mm on257 zoom samples. Thirty-three controls are the smallest tested density below0.03 mm, comparable to the native source-rounding floor. The largest additional B4 shift is under0.4 mm. Only the original wide/mid/tele controls are marked published. Intermediate positions and nominal f-numbers remain inferred, not mechanically certified.

## Image Stabilization

The patent describes transverse B3b motion for stabilization (¶0026), and Figure3 illustrates an off-axis stabilization condition. The present prescription is only the centered state. No decentering, compensating camera movement, stabilization range or IS effectiveness is simulated.

## Semi-Diameters and Verification Limits

The patent supplies no clear semi-diameters. Initial values were measured from the optical rims in Figure1 using its85.68 mm first-to-last-lens vertex span, not the barrel or camera block. Manual pixel inspection takes precedence over the automated figure screen, whose RIM channel underreads thin cemented interfaces and is contaminated by brackets. The final estimates are within about1 mm of the manually read outlines. The initial8.75 mm L6 estimate produced real S8–S9 overlap;8.20 mm was adopted before validation. This is an inferred aperture, not an altered published number. No gap-sag override is used.

The pinned engine checks2,193 sampled zoom/focus states with zero hidden trim. An independent65-point radial clearance sweep per adjacent pair finds minimum glass0.600 mm, air0.200 mm and maximum air-gap sag fraction0.893796 under the default0.90. These are sampled-state results, not proof for an uncountable continuum.

A separate exact-ray audit targets the source13.66 mm semi-height:5 zooms ×3 focus states ×5 field heights ×3 iris-radius ratios ×61 pupil points =13,725 reference-d-line rays. It records8,907 transmitted rays,4,634 ordinary clips and184 raw front-surface no-bracket events. Independent sphere intersections place all184 outside the authored front clear aperture; none is an unexplained in-aperture failure. All75 chief solves converge. Transmitted-ray surface residuals are below1e−12 mm, with zero rim overrun. Vignetting is retained; these counts do not claim an unvignetted full pupil or certify all wavelengths and states.

Thirty primary diffraction MTF requests cover5 zoom positions, native/f8 apertures and reference/C-d-F/photopic spectra, with4 fields through13.66 mm and frequencies0/10/20/40 lp/mm. All120 sampled field computations converge numerically, but convergence is not a high-quality-performance guarantee. The inferred quarter-zoom reference-d-line edge at40 lp/mm is S/T0.254/0.242 at f5.1, versus0.487/0.285 at f8. Nearby zoom0.20–0.30 and best-axial sensitivity add48 converged field computations. The low edge curves persist with correct source image height and are not cured by axial best focus; at quarter zoom best-axial S/T is0.160/0.232. No arbitrary production-cam optimization was applied to improve them. The source native design planes remain unchanged.

These results qualify a source-based reconstruction, not a measured commercial lens. Actual browser/viewer QA and combined integration testing remain separate pending gates at this checkpoint; engine-native SVG inspection is not a substitute.
