# Canon 28mm f/2.8 LTM

[Canon's period catalog](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/00331/00331.pdf) shows the two distinctive outer cemented doublets with two inner meniscus singlets. The sole [Hiroshi Ito US2779239](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/ef/32/af/b8bd555ba0f06c/US2779239.pdf) prescription aligns to 1.266 native pixels RMS (1.805 maximum) across 101 pixels. Nine independently resolvable vertices set one scale; R4 is not a distinct reliable datum in the small crop. Evidence supports a production correlation, not the exact glass melt of every retail lens. A proposed retail L2 change lacks a verified table and has not been applied.

The entire signed spherical geometry is scaled once by 28, with no change to glass data. D-line EFL is 28.096906 mm and derived BFD is 20.189698 mm; the published example does not specify a separate numerical BFD. The two signed air gaps of 0.056 and 0.028 mm are preserved. Default geometry passes without trimming or override. The f/2.8 nominal and 72° published geometric field are kept as source facts separate from actual test scope. The nominal production field is about 75°.

The estimated midpoint iris and clear caps were validated for stopped-down d-line infinity tracing at f/4 through sampled 15° and f/8 through sampled 25° off-axis. At f/2.8 the independent native grid reaches the center but loses samples off-axis; the original diagnostic finds an axial D80 near 104 µm and unresolved wide-aperture MTF. Depending on sampling and aim, counts differ; there is no f/2.8 quality or full-field claim. Finite focus, broadband, measured factory vignetting and UI are unqualified.
