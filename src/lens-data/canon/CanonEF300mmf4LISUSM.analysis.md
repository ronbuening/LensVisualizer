## Optical Architecture

Five functional units have positive/negative/positive/negative/positive power. L1 contains five singlets. L2 is the E6/E7 focus doublet; L3 is E8/E9. L4 contains E10/E11 and E12 and moves transversely for stabilization. L5 is E13/E14. Functional units are distinct from the conventional ten air-spaced groups. All twenty-four refracting faces are spherical.
