## 2026-10-30 — Original-patent caps, outer shoulders, concave ends and glass

Original: supplied `patents/JP2018049102A.pdf`, JP 2018-049102 A, Numerical Example 1. Source definition of the d/C/F reference: ¶¶0105–0107, PDF pp. 14–15. Prescription, effective diameters and two focus columns: ¶0108 on p. 15; group and standalone-element focal data continue on p. 16. Corresponding Fig. 1(a)/(b), infinity/close focus, is on p. 21. ### Shape disposition

Fig. 1 is an embedded native 1182×1796 px raster at approximately 400 ppi. It was extracted and rotated with no resampling. Enlarged 6×/8× reading crops expose the native pixels and add no source resolution. The infinity first/last glass vertex span is approximately 445 native px for 101.00 mm, or about 4.4 px/mm; one pixel is approximately 0.23 mm. Outer-edge ink/schematic uncertainty is approximately ±0.7 mm. Rays crossing the caps, group brackets, stop and film leaders, launch planes and the image plane are excluded.

All nineteen source R/d rows, all ten glass pairs and every non-stop effective diameter match the authored clear prescription. The clear radii remain literal source diameter/2; outer lands never increase them. Every element, concave end, both cemented interfaces and both apodization-bearing faces was inspected in Fig. 1.

No inferred cut, fabricated depth source, disconnected layer or equal-height bonded block is authored. All source R/d, stops and focus controls are unchanged. The stop's printed 35.51 mm effective diameter is distinguished from the current derived iris radius of 17.205209 mm at retained nominal F/2.059788166; prior public wording incorrectly described that effective diameter as the authored physical iris and is corrected without changing the model.

Canonical geometry and actual SVG comparisons cover infinity, midpoint and published close focus, with twenty-one focus-control samples checked for finite connected outlines and hidden trimming. The close-focus finite-object check uses the source 900 mm state; the axial pupil samples clear, while wider field samples retain source-compatible vignetting and an unobstructed chief. These sampled findings do not prove continuous three-dimensional clearance, the production iris or a historical retail prescription. The two source film attenuations are not reconstructed numerically.

All ten compatible curves remain qualified proxies. The source publishes no per-element C/F/g indices, partial-dispersion ratio or dPgF; no unsupported numerical spectral override is authored. L2/L4 inferred APD notes now identify the K-PFK80 proxy curve as their sole spectral basis (catalog-derived ΔPgF approximately +0.030), rather than treating Canon's production UD count as evidence of APD. No structured per-lens dPgF is added and no supplier, melt or historical chemistry is identified.

The two light-absorbing films are explicitly located on surfaces 7 and 17 in ¶¶0091–0092. The source supplies transmission conditions but no additional refractive index/thickness or numerical radial law for those coatings. No synthetic cement, refractive resin, filter or rear plate is invented. Apodization transmission and anomalous partial dispersion remain distinct. Public notes keep the later 2018 apodized embodiment separate from Canon's 1996 retail production lens and retain the documented LF1/LF2 source wording inconsistency.
