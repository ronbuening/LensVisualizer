## Optical Architecture

Fourteen elements in ten air groups, with four cemented doublets. Positive U1, positive moving U2 and negative U3 form the source inner-focus architecture. The explicit stop separates fixed U1 from U2. U2 contains four elements and two aspheric faces on L7. Only centered coaxial geometry is modeled.
