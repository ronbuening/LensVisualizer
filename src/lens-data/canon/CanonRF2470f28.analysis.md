## Patent Reference

**Patent:** US 2019/0278068 A1 — *Zoom Lens and Image Pickup Apparatus*

**Inventor:** Takahiro Hatada (Utsunomiya-shi, JP)

**Applicant:** Canon Kabushiki Kaisha

**Filed:** March 4, 2019 (priority: JP 2018-042214, March 8, 2018)

**Published:** September 12, 2019

US 2019/0278068 A1, Takahiro Hatada, Canon, Numerical Example 5, paragraph 0100 (PDF page 23 / printed page 11). Figure 9 (PDF page 10) shows the wide-end section and movement arrows. The retail name is an association; the source supplies a 24.72–67.89 mm, f/2.91 design with half-field 41.19°–17.68° and image height 21.64 mm.

## Architecture

Twenty-one elements form fifteen air-separated components and seven functional units. The six cemented doublets are L1/L2, L5/L6, L8/L9, L11/L12, L13/L14 and L16/L17. Source stop 16 is STO, moving with the third unit. The fourth unit is the centered stabilization doublet; the sixth unit is the L19 focusing singlet. No filter or cover-glass block appears in this example: all 37 source rows are retained, with BF already extending from the last optical surface to the image plane.

## Glass and aspheres

All source nd/vd values are retained. Catalog names are compatible counterparts, not historical supplier identities. The former patent-only codes resolve to compatible S-LAH96 (L10), J-KZFH9 (L16) and L-LAH85V (L18) through the current catalog. Their labels now identify these qualified counterparts. The previous inferred APD claims for L5/L11/L15 and unsupported molding/chemistry descriptions are removed. The patent lists no partial-dispersion data for these rows.

Five aspheres occur on three elements: S22, S30/S31 and S34/S35. All published K=0 and A4–A12 coefficients are retained. Source paragraph 0093 uses the standard (1+K) base-sag convention. Asphericity does not establish glass molding or a specific manufacturing process.

## Zoom and focus

The three source stations remain 24.72, 44.86 and 67.89 mm. All seven variable-gap arrays retain the source values, including the nonmonotonic D21 values 2.26, 2.23 and 2.54 mm. Independent matrix EFL values are 24.72150, 44.86491 and 67.87629 mm; total tracks are 144.11, 158.48 and 172.88 mm versus the rounded source summary 144.09, 158.48 and 172.87. Numerical rows take precedence; no gap is fitted to the rounded summary.

Camera-relative wide-to-tele unit displacements are −28.77, +0.36, −13.39, −13.11, −21.32, −22.15 and −21.32 mm. Source paragraph 0086 and Figure 9 say all groups move objectward, conflicting with the positive net L2 displacement. The table is preserved and this contradiction remains a follow-up. A changing D21 gap alone does not prove a functional group's reversal in camera coordinates.

Paragraph 0087 establishes imageward focus of the sixth unit, but publishes no finite-conjugate spacings. Focus is disabled. The retained 0.21 m endpoint is legacy retail metadata, not a simulated patent conjugate. For an isolated sixth-unit movement, D31 increases and D33 decreases; BF does not change because the last unit does not focus. The previous statement that BF varies for this motion was incorrect. Lateral IS displacement is not simulated. Finite-focus optical analyses are therefore unavailable as patent reproductions.

The aperture series now starts at source f/2.91. The existing viewer upper limit f/16 is retained as a display setting, not a patent-specified minimum aperture. Intermediate zoom reconstruction remains an estimate between source stations.

## Semi-diameter notes

The published effective diameters are restored as SD=diameter/2 throughout, replacing earlier unsupported reshaping and rounding. Surface and image-circle audits pass. All source zoom stations and half zoom pass diagnostics without hidden rim trimming; the old S6 reduction for a supposed 1.25 ratio limit is unnecessary in the current renderer.

Figure 9 was rendered at 600 dpi and its optical rims visually checked. The automated measurement includes contaminated readings where leaders, brackets or neighboring elements overlap its windows; those are rejected. The clean physical outlines support the published numerical apertures, which remain the primary evidence rather than drawing-ink extrema.

## Inferred zoom iris

The viewer infers physical iris radii at the source zoom stations from their nominal infinity f-numbers. This corrects the previous fixed-iris behavior, which did not reproduce the stated telephoto aperture. Intermediate iris radii are interpolated and focus retains the current zoom iris. This is a calculated model, not a patent-published physical iris schedule.
