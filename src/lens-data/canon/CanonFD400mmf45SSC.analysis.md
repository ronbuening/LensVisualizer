**Patent:** US 4,045,128 A  
**Filed:** April 22, 1975  
**Granted:** August 30, 1977  
**Inventor:** Kikuo Momiyama  
**Assignee:** Canon Inc. (printed Canon Kabushiki Kaisha)  
**Title:** Telephoto Objective  
**Example details:** Table 1 and continuation, Figure 1; all lengths scaled uniformly ×4 from f = 100 mm to nominal 400 mm.

The [original patent](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/24/d6/3e/433afa64651526/US4045128A.pdf) and [Canon museum product record](https://global.canon/en/c-museum/product/fd181.html) support a probable FD 400mm f/4.5 S.S.C. family association:

1. Six elements / five groups: three front singlets, one negative singlet and a rear doublet.
2. Rear focusing, f/4.5–22 and 4 m close focus, per Canon.
3. Canon authorship and timing consistent with the October 1975 product.

The patent places the stationary diaphragm behind moving group C; Canon describes focusing behind the diaphragm. The [Canon Museum section](https://global.canon/ja/c-museum/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/fd181-lens-construction.gif) matches the shapes and cementing but marks no iris and adds a rear filter. Table 3's seven separate elements fit less well. The factory prescription remains unconfirmed.

Table 1's R4 = −258.3399 mm is retained over claim 1's −258.3899 mm. Nominal focal length remains 400 mm; computed EFL is 400.0032373 mm. The printed 80.01 mm first-surface-to-image distance gives 320.04 mm without refocusing.

## Optical Architecture

The all-spherical telephoto comprises fixed positive group A, stationary negative member B and rear negative focusing doublet C. First-to-last glass vertex distance is 161.456 mm.

The Figure 1 iris estimate, z = 194.921425 mm, leaves 3.841425 mm behind the rear vertex at the printed finite endpoint. Neither the exact coordinate nor complete production travel is established.

Semi-diameters are 45.7/45.7, 44.6/44.6, 43.4/43.4, 20.9/20.9 and 18.5/18.5/18.1 mm, estimated from Figure 1 and bounded by the f/4.5 axial beam. The figure's local scale varies by about 5%; its optical endpoints agree within roughly 5–8%. Production clear apertures remain unknown. The engine-derived 13.90143 mm iris admits an axial ray that clears surfaces 7 and 9 by 0.077 and 0.083 mm. Geometric pupil transmission at the 3.0833° field edge is 57.1%; this excludes coating losses and radiometric falloff.

The model's 6°10′ full field comes from the successor FD 400mm f/4.5 in [Canon catalog PDF5/SG2-A5](https://www.pacificrimcamera.com/rl/01152/01152.pdf), without establishing the original S.S.C. field. Patent chart Y = 21.6 is not interpreted as normalized semiheight at f = 100 mm.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

- L1, surfaces 1–2: biconvex positive collector; nd = 1.48749, νd = 70.1; Ohara S-FSL5 equivalent.
- L2, surfaces 3–4: negative meniscus in A; nd = 1.80610, νd = 40.9; Ohara LAH53 equivalent.
- L3, surfaces 5–6: biconvex positive completing A; nd = 1.48749, νd = 70.1; Ohara S-FSL5 equivalent.
- L4, surfaces 7–8: fixed negative meniscus B; nd = 1.58913, νd = 61.1; Hoya BACD5 equivalent.
- L5, surfaces 9–10: positive meniscus; nd = 1.75520, νd = 27.5; Schott SF4 equivalent.
- L6, surfaces 10–11: negative meniscus; nd = 1.71300, νd = 53.9; Hoya LAC8 equivalent. Cemented to L5, it forms negative focusing doublet C1.

The patent balances spherical aberration, coma, astigmatism and chromatic focus variation. Power signs alone do not establish individual aberration contributions or production performance.

Inspector focal lengths are isolated thick-element values in air, calculated from the scaled radii, thickness and printed nd. For L1–L6 they are +184.25, −156.60, +195.72, −1151.57, +287.10 and −130.01 mm; the two cemented components are considered separately for these values.

## Glass and Dispersion

Despite the patent's sodium wording, catalog coordinates support helium d: S-FSL5 gives 1.487490 at d versus 1.487427 at D; N-SK5 gives 1.589130 versus 1.589044. Printed nd/νd remain unchanged.

Catalog equivalents estimate dispersion without identifying historical suppliers, melts, composition or manufacture. The patent supplies no partial-dispersion data; Canon identifies no special-dispersion positions in the cited record. Chromatic predictions remain approximate.

## Focus and Operating Limits

The model represents infinity only. Canon specifies rear focusing to 4 m with a variable-pitch cam. The patent's d8 changes from 2.250 to 9.656 mm: doublet C moves 7.406 mm imageward, or 29.624 mm at model dimensions. Neither that endpoint nor continuous motion is modeled. Tabulated object distance uses the focal plane; the analytical relation uses the first-surface vertex.

Last-glass-to-image distance is 158.584 mm versus 158.578298 mm paraxially, leaving 0.005702 mm defocus.

## Aspherical Surfaces and Special Components

All eleven interfaces are spherical. The [Canon manual](https://manuals.plus/m/4101097043ab87fa383e3d5119d3c96291998eb8b4fe39913b67455c1fd440be_optim.pdf) requires a 34 mm filter in its own holder. Table 1 prescribes no plate, so the filter remains unmodeled. Patent mechanics also describe a filter holder and transparent plate for optical-path adjustment. No stabilization prescription is supplied.
