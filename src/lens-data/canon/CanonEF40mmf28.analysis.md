## Optical Architecture

The source presents an approximately symmetric, two-positive-unit arrangement around the stop. Front unit L1a
contains positive L11 cemented to negative L12, followed by positive meniscus L13. Rear unit L1b contains negative
L21 cemented to positive L22, followed by aspheric positive meniscus L23. This is a modified Gauss/plasmat-style
interpretation of the source architecture, rather than a product lineage named by the patent.

The source unit data give L1a +117.49 mm and L1b +45.50 mm. The cemented combinations have negative focal lengths,
Lp1 −76.465 mm and Lp2 −160.098 mm. Thus the positive singlets and negative doublets distribute power differently
from a simple symmetric achromat pair. The front doublet's large index contrast reduces its Petzval contribution;
conditions (1)–(3) explicitly govern that choice (PDF pages 8–9 / columns 4–5).

The stop remains at source surface 6, followed by the printed 5.12 mm air gap. The source's 12.84 mm effective-ray
diameter at the diaphragm is envelope evidence; it is not independently treated as a physical iris diameter.
The existing derived/paraxial iris radius 6.375182 mm and f/2.8 target remain. No source filter, camera-side plate,
hybrid resin layer or stabilization control is supplied in this example.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

### L11 — Biconvex Positive, Front Member of Lp1

**nd = 1.83481, νd = 42.7. Glass: S-LAH55V (OHARA), qualified spectral proxy; historical identity unresolved. f ≈ +32.87 mm.**

L11 supplies positive collection ahead of the negative member. Its index exceeds L12's by 0.30309. The patent
uses that difference to control Petzval curvature, while the doublet's negative combined power helps retain a long
back focus. The two source radii are preserved; no glass chemistry or supplier follows from the numeric coordinates.

### L12 — Biconcave Negative, Rear Member of Lp1

**nd = 1.53172, νd = 48.8. Glass: S-TIL6 (OHARA), qualified spectral proxy; historical identity unresolved. f ≈ −21.67 mm.**

The steep rear concavity contributes most of L12's diverging action. The paired Abbe ratio is 42.7/48.8 ≈ 0.875,
within condition (2). The source explains that this range balances cemented-junction curvature and chromatic/spherical
correction. Its air-facing S3 has a resolved outer shoulder, modeled beyond the unchanged 6.81 mm clear cap.
The separate L12 catalog relabel review dated **2026-05-20** is retained as prior coverage of that narrow issue.

### L13 — Positive Meniscus, Convex to Object

**nd = 1.69680, νd = 55.5. Glass: S-LAL14 (OHARA), qualified spectral proxy; historical identity unresolved. f ≈ +44.28 mm.**

L13 follows the negative Lp1 pair and makes front unit L1a positive overall. Its two positive radii distribute positive
power near the stop. The published effective-ray caps remain 6.67 and 6.51 mm; their difference is retained rather
than replaced by a uniform-height block.

### L21 — Biconcave Negative, Front Member of Lp2

**nd = 1.69895, νd = 30.1. Glass: S-TIM35 (OHARA), qualified spectral proxy; historical identity unresolved. f ≈ −15.26 mm.**

L21 is the strongest standalone negative element. Its low Abbe number relative to L22 contributes dispersion contrast
in the rear cemented pair. Its concave air-facing S7 has a resolved shoulder; the clear cap stays at 6.235 mm and the
conservative outer rim is anchored to the existing 6.89 mm bonded cap.

### L22 — Biconvex Positive, Rear Member of Lp2

**nd = 1.83481, νd = 42.7. Glass: S-LAH55V (OHARA), qualified spectral proxy; historical identity unresolved. f ≈ +19.26 mm.**

L22 offsets much of L21's negative power, leaving the combined doublet weakly negative. It repeats L11's index/Abbe
pair. Repeated coordinates do not prove the same historical glass or a manufacturing-economy rationale. Source
conditions (7)–(8) control the pair's index difference and Abbe ratio.

### L23 — Positive Meniscus, Concave to Object, Aspheric Front

**nd = 1.58313, νd = 59.4. Glass: S-BAL42 (OHARA), qualified spectral proxy; historical identity unresolved. f ≈ +41.21 mm.**

The final positive meniscus carries the only source asphere, on S10. Its negative A4 adds concave-to-object sag
relative to the spherical base; the higher even terms adjust the outer profile. The original supplies the surface
shape but no resin layer, molding temperature, viscosity or manufacturing process. Its published clear caps and
coefficients are retained without tuning to the drawing.

All internal R/d values remain fixed. The 7.17 mm extension gives computed close magnification magnitude 0.184286×,
