# CANON EF 50mm f/1.4 USM — audit

## Source

Masatake Kato, Canon Inc., JP H06-242370 A, filed 17 February 1993 and published 2 September 1994. Numerical Example 2 is the table introduced in ¶0023. Figure 2 shows the section and Figure 6 gives the aberrations. [Patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/JPH06242370A/en)

## Transcription

Multiplying the 13 radii and 12 axial spacings of the printed f = 1 prescription by 50 gives the dimensions in millimetres. All signs, thicknesses, air spaces and seven index/Abbe-number pairs agree with the table. R8 is the cemented boundary between L4 and L5. R6 and R7 enclose the diaphragm. All surfaces are spherical. The example includes no filter plate or variable spacing.

The seven d-line glass pairs match the Ohara equivalents S-LAH66, S-LAH55, S-TIM22, S-TIH6, S-LAM54, S-LAH58 and S-LAH65V, in element order. The largest absolute differences from the printed values are 0.000003 in refractive index and 0.035 in Abbe number.

The image plane coincides with paraxial infinity focus to within 0.0001 mm. The axial marginal ray and the chief ray to the 21.63 mm frame corner clear every refracting surface. The image circle covers the full 36 × 24 mm frame. The lower marginal ray could not be traced at that field angle. Geometric pupil transmission is 100% on axis and 24.3% at the corner. At the edge of the patent field, distortion is −1.87%, consistent with the curve in Figure 6.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

- **Surface clear radii.** Figure 2 gives the profile. Using the 44.672 mm distance between the first and last vertices to set its scale gives 12.86 pixels/mm in the enlarged drawing. In order from R1 to R13 the adopted semi-diameters are 19.2, 19.2, 16.2, 15.03, 15.0, 12.9, 12.6, 15.6, 15.6, 16.2, 16.2, 15.6 and 15.6 mm. R6 ends at the optical edge inside the flange.
- **Clearance at R4/R5 and R7.** R4 and R5 approach contact at a height of about 15.2 mm. Their 15.03 and 15.0 mm clear radii leave positive edge clearance with a permissible sag difference of 0.96 times the axial gap. Reducing both to 14.85 mm makes R4 limit the aperture to f/1.416. At R7 the drawn land reads about 12.3 mm. The adopted 12.6 mm leaves clearance for the axial bundle. The figures and the ray clearances therefore both contribute to these dimensions.
- **Diaphragm.** D6 totals 15.122 mm. The estimated iris plane lies 8.959 mm behind R6 and 6.163 mm ahead of R7. The drawing suggests about 8.45 mm behind R6, and Canon's section suggests 8.75 mm. At f/1.4 the entrance pupil radius is 17.857 mm. Its paraxial image at the stop has a radius of 12.354 mm. An exact axial ray launched at that entrance height crosses the stop at 12.220 mm.
- **Image and focus.** The last air space is the calculated 37.5752 mm paraxial back focal distance. The patent gives no finite conjugate or focus travel. The commercial lens's 0.45 m limit is a product specification.

## Product Identification

Canon's June 1993 product drawing agrees with the seven elements, six groups, position of the stop, nearly flat R8 cement line, rear-facing L6 meniscus and biconvex L7. The 1.015 mm space between L1 and L2 distinguishes Example 2 from the other three examples, each of which gives 0.146 mm after scaling. The RMS differences between the 13 axial vertices and the Canon drawing are 1.39, 0.84, 1.26 and 1.22 pixels for Examples 1–4, respectively. The evidence favors Example 2. The drawing gives neither production dimensions nor a patent example number. [Canon product record](https://global.canon/en/c-museum/product/ef318.html) · [Canon optical section](https://global.canon/ja/c-museum/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/ef318-lens-construction.gif)

Canon specifies the 50 mm f/1.4 EF lens with eight diaphragm blades, a minimum aperture of f/22 and a 58 mm filter. Its unit-focus mechanism reaches 0.15× at 0.45 m. ## Corrections

### 2026-11-25

The table heading in each of the four examples prints f/1.14. Paragraphs 0001 and 0020 specify f/1.4, and the aberration diagrams in Figures 5–8 give f/1.4 for every example. At 50 mm, f/1.14 would require an entrance-pupil radius of 21.93 mm. The corresponding axial marginal ray misses R6. The design aperture is therefore f/1.4. The numerical surface prescription agrees with the printed table throughout.

## Remaining Questions

Factory dimensions would settle the exact iris position, the clearance between L2 and L3, and the optical edge at R7. The published glass constants do not establish the production supplier or melt. The patent gives no close-focus prescription against which to compare Canon's 0.15× maximum magnification.
