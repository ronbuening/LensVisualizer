## Optical layout and focus

The first group contains three positive menisci, a biconcave corrector, a cemented negative–positive pair and a closing
positive lens. A single negative meniscus forms the second group; a biconcave lens and a final positive lens form the
third. These are seven, one and two physical elements respectively. The source discloses an all-spherical prescription.

Table 2's variable D13 and D15 values describe the two moving groups relative to the fixed rear group. At infinity they
are 3.96 and 4.83 mm at the patent scale; at unit magnification they are 52.78 and 10.22 mm. The stored endpoints remain
4.95/6.0375 and 65.975/12.775 mm after scaling. Intermediate motion is an interpolation, rather than an additional
published conjugate. The existing inferred stop in the L43–L44 gap and its f/2.53 iris are retained; Figure 5 does not
supply a measured stop location or diameter. No source filter or cover plate is listed.

## Patent rims and concave ends

No numerical semi-diameter or cut depth is published. Figure 5 was inspected directly from the local original,
excluding the leaders, group brackets and axis. The rear relay was undersized relative to the front collector:
L62's optical half-span is approximately 142 versus 201 pixels for L41 on the inspected crop. Holding the existing
26 mm front reference gives about 18.4 mm for L62. Its two continuous caps now share that value; the biconcave L61
uses 13.8 mm on both faces, following its approximately 107-pixel half-span. Raster uncertainty is about two pixels
per endpoint, and the absolute scale remains an inference. These are display/aperture estimates, not source dimensions.

Source-continuous paired heights also remove the old tapered front menisci and L47, with the D1 shared interface
kept connected. A separate outer land on concave S8 preserves its 16.5 mm clear cap and extends the drawn body to
17.5 mm. The equivalent numeric cut was independently checked over the full wide-open field/focus sweep: beam reach
15.6657 mm plus the required 0.05 mm margin stays inside 16.5 mm. No source-depth exemption is used.
Other proposed lands on S2/S4/S6/S15 were rejected where their former caps failed that same clearance rule; those
faces instead use the source-continuous paired height. No opaque land is placed at the cemented interface.

The modeled centered full-frame chief ray and on-axis f/2.53 beam remain clear. A finite stop-lattice comparison
retained 6,930 sampled rays, lost none and gained 1,356 after the source-supported cap increases. These counts are
sampling results rather than flux or a proof of an unvignetted three-dimensional pupil. Actual production SVG paths
were checked across nine focus/zoom states with no hidden geometry trim. Source radii, thicknesses, focus law, iris
schedule and geometric tolerances were retained.
