## Optical Architecture

Eight elements form six groups with two cemented doublets. L1, the L2/L3 pair and weak L4 precede the printed diaphragm. L5, the L6/L7 pair and L8 follow it. L5 has a finite front radius−2082.087mm; it is not replaced by a plane. The source stop is positional S8, with3.86mm before and1.79mm after it. No extra sensor plate or aspheric coefficients are supplied or invented.

The architecture statements describe the selected source arrangement. A probable product match does not supply unprinted factory clear apertures, iris mechanics or spectral coefficients.
