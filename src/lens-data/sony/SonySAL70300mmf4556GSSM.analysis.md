## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** JP 2009-168934 A  
**Filed:** 11 January 2008  
**Published:** 30 July 2009  
**Inventors:** Hisayuki Yamanaka; Dayong Li  
**Applicant:** Tamron Co., Ltd.  
**Embodiment analyzed:** Third embodiment, Tables 3 and 3-1, Figure 3.

This is a probable reconstruction of the original Sony SAL70300G, not a manufacturer-confirmed factory prescription. Its 16 elements in 11 air-separated components, ordered cemented pairs and triplet, single front ED position and fifth-group rear focusing agree with Sony's original construction diagram. The later SAL70300G2 and E-mount SEL70300G are not identified by this model. Patent applicant identity does not prove a supply relationship.

The three native focal lengths are 71.7730, 140.0942 and 291.9632 mm, with source apertures f/4.59, f/5.60 and f/5.79. Marketing 70–300 mm f/4.5–5.6 is retained only as catalog metadata. Source image-plane label34 follows surface28; no missing glass surfaces are invented.

## Optical Architecture

Five optical units have positive, negative, positive, positive and negative first-order powers. G1 has three elements; G2 three; G3 two; G4 five; G5 three. Four cemented assemblies include the distinctive negative-positive-negative triplet in G4. The source stop is row11, 2 mm before G3's first glass surface. No rear cover plates are listed or added.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The source gives d-line index and Abbe number only. Named Hoya glasses are coordinate-compatible public catalog equivalents used for dispersion, not recovered supplier or melt identities. Other vendors have equivalent coordinates. Native nd/vd values remain unchanged. No anomalous-dispersion or APO claim is inferred from Abbe number alone. The source contains no aspherical surfaces for this example.

## Zoom and Focus

All three published infinity rows and all three 1.6 m rows are preserved exactly. The 1.6 m object distances are 1424.07, 1393.16 and 1364.12 mm from the first vertex, as printed. They are not Sony's 1.2 m minimum-focus specification. Only these three near states are marked source-published finite conjugates.

Naive straight interpolation of the three zoom gap sets creates approximately 13.3 mm of intermediate paraxial defocus. This model therefore adds calculated intermediate cam stations. G2 and G4 positions are solved jointly to reproduce the interpolated focal length and image plane, while G1, G3, G5 and total track follow their source-station interpolation. The maximum G2 correction is approximately 3.208 mm. This is a constrained reconstruction, not documented factory motion. All source stations remain unchanged.

Fifth-group focus movement is calculated at intermediate focus settings, keeping d23+BF constant except the source’s retained endpoint rounding. The model ends at 1.6 m. Dense cam and focus points reduce interpolation error; they do not establish full optical performance or genuine production mechanics. The exact source image plane is retained rather than optimized for best MTF.

## Aperture, Clear Apertures and Validation

Stop radii are calculated from the source design f-numbers at each zoom station. Clear semi-diameters are inferred from Figure 3 and constrained by positive edge thickness and nonoverlapping geometry: front singlet 29 mm, front doublet 26 mm, G2 12 mm, G3 11.5 mm, G4 15/13/14 mm and G5 10.5 mm. These are modeling estimates rather than published dimensions. No gap-sag override is used; the normal 0.90 limit remains.

The displayed off-axis fan uses 0.3 of the engine’s computed field. This is a central-field visualization choice, not a claim of unvignetted full-frame illumination. Wider-pupil/full-field clipping must remain visible in validation evidence. Surface radii, axial glass thicknesses and source gaps have not been changed to improve tracing.
