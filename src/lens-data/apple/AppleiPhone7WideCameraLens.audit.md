### Prescription and source finding

Visually checked every Table 11A radius and axial distance, all six plastics' nd/vd coordinates and the rear filter,
and every K and A–G entry of Table 11B, including the continued D–G rows on PDF p.85. The stored values agree with
the print: A–G multiply h⁴…h¹⁶; G is nonzero on 10A/11A; no odd or higher-order printed terms are missing.
The source denominator contains (1+K), K maps directly, dimensions are millimeters, and no scaling is applied.
The separate c column has higher precision than rounded Table 11A radii; it is redundant curvature, not an extra
polynomial term. No transcription error or supported source erratum was identified; no source.errata entry added.

The baseline lower-rim ray at 14.42° and normalized pupil y=−1 meets 7A at about 1.16 mm and misses the chief-ray
image by +0.6857 mm. The stated on-axis f/1.8 marginal ray instead meets 6A at 1.015756636 mm and 7A at
0.933289729 mm, so the shared 1.04 mm cap clears it. The 7A cap is selected from the slope-root geometry, not by
fitting RI. The front face follows the square-rim rule. The uncapped rear gull-wing profiles (9A, 10A, 12A, 13A,
15A) remain intact. Before/after silhouette review retains their family shape; Fig.10 is only a related example,
so it cannot certify absolute Example 11 rim heights.

### Aberration and localization outcome

LSA remains +0.003 / −0.006 / −0.005 / −0.003 mm at pupil fractions 1 / 0.85 / 0.7 / 0.5. Close-ray astigmatism
and chief-ray distortion are unchanged by the rim edits. Fig.26 has ±0.050 mm focus axes and ±1% distortion axes;
the modeled LSA is on the same small scale, and distortion has the same sub-percent, non-monotonic shape. At the
source's 37.0° field, the model gives T/S −0.035 / −0.033 mm and distortion +0.053%. Astigmatism is not a quantitative
reproduction of the source's polychromatic curves. No source-errata correction is justified from that comparison.

Rimmer RI is now finite throughout the sampled sweep; peak referred deviation decreases from 0.7957 to 0.00191.
Outer-field loss is expected from the inferred cap. At the runtime 37.4633° edge, the transmitted pupil fraction is
0.495663 and RI is 0.154299. The stated f/1.8 axial beam remains iris-limited and the capped surfaces admit it.
The canonical surface check accepts the model. Image-circle certification is unavailable without a declared image
format; none is inferred from the product correlation.
