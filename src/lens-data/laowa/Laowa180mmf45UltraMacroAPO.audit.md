# Laowa 180mm f4.5 source and construction audit

## Disposition

The source dossier earns READY_FOR_DATA and the revised construction earns READY_FOR_ANALYSIS. Source f/4.6 and all prescription rows are retained with a disclosed gap 6→7 allowance of 0.922. Independent exact-byte MF/AF/analysis review is complete. The dossier earns READY_FOR_BATCH only with the accompanying successful final clean replay; integration remains pending.

Every other gap is independently required to pass 0.90.

## Exact aperture conflict

The infinity marginal ray launched at EFL/(2 × 4.6) has entrance height 19.013376598024 mm. Native project tracing and a separate exact sphere/vector-Snell implementation agree: surface 6 height is 14.1308321797 mm, surface 7 height is 14.0929012565 mm and the stop hit is 9.1475729303 mm. That stop radius is an inferred f-number calibration, not a published physical diaphragm dimension.

For R6 = 662.7343 mm, R7 = −139.4136 mm and d6 = 0.9382 mm, the default rule allows 0.84438 mm intrusion and at most 13.932374832762 mm shared radius. Passing the complete source f/4.6 cone requires a shared radius of at least 14.0929012565 mm. Its intrusion is 0.863991718238 mm, so the theoretical minimum gapSagFrac is 0.920903558130. Equal-envelope apertures have no extra ray-clearance allowance. The actual ideal-surface common-radius axial clearance is 0.074208281762 mm; the ray advances 0.073400420265 mm axially over a 0.082621889531 mm segment. The surfaces meet at radius 14.684303791217 mm, 0.591402534697 mm beyond the required shared radius.

Half-last-digit perturbations give a local linear sensitivity of ±0.003078306 in the required fraction and ±0.002892021 mm in common-radius axial clearance. Selected coordinated extreme corners give 0.917833355–0.923990005 and 0.071308777–0.077092855 mm. These are numerical precision diagnostics, not a rigorous global bound or manufacturing tolerance budget. Centering, wedge, thermal expansion, polishing, coating, mounting and assembly margins are absent from the source. Positive ideal clearance does not certify manufacturability.

With the same inferred physical iris held constant, exact on-axis traces at all five published conjugates have positive clearance. Required fractions are 0.920904 at infinity, 0.648741 at MF −1x, 0.594971 at MF −1.5x, 0.910901 at AF8750 and 0.860110 at AF1350. This conditional iris diagnostic does not invent a published macro iris schedule. It also does not prove full-field/pupil clearance over a continuum.

A separate source-precision nuance is the one-decimal FNO. An unreported actual value near f/4.6491 could fit the on-axis default reserve if 4.6 were rounded, but no physical iris or unrounded value is given. This is a sensitivity hypothesis, not authority to replace the literal target. Only about 4.20% additional common radius separates the exact envelope from profile contact; a 5% radius allowance would instead overlap by 0.014561 mm.
