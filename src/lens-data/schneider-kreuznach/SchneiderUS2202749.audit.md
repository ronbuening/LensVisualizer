# SCHNEIDER-KREUZNACH 100mm f/3.5 (US 2,202,749 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

[US 2,202,749 A, “Asymmetric Objective”](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2202749A/en)
names Albrecht Wilhelm Tronnier and the assignee Jos. Schneider & Co.,
Optotechnische Gesellschaft. The US application was filed on 9 August 1938,
with German priority dated 4 October 1935; publication was on 28 May 1940.

The [patent facsimile](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/2202749)
prints Example II in the lower table on PDF page 2, at f = 100 mm and f/3.5.
The drawing sheet is PDF page 1. Figure 2 shows the f/5 Example I form;
Example II is described as analogous to it, with planar R2 and R5.

## Transcription Check

All seven radii, five element thicknesses, the 10.9 mm diaphragm space,
five refractive indices and five Abbe numbers agree with the page images.
Every finite radius has its sign printed; R2 and R5 are planes. R2, R3 and
R6 are cemented interfaces. No numerical transcription error was found,
and the dimensions retain the printed millimetre scale.

Example I uses violet-ray indices; Example II uses yellow-ray indices.
The patent does not identify a wavelength or spectral line for either.
The Example II indices give ΔN = 0.1231 in the patent's expression,
within its stated range greater than 0.005 and at most 0.185.

## First-Order Check

The calculated EFL is 99.936 mm, 0.064 mm below the printed 100 mm.
The paraxial aperture is f/3.50, agreeing with the printed f/3.5.
Calculated back focus is 77.95 mm and the first-vertex-to-image distance is
120.25 mm. The optical vertex track is 42.3 mm. No back focus is printed;
the final air gap places the image at the calculated paraxial plane.

The inferred stop radius is 11.183 mm. Exact rays give an entrance-pupil
radius of 14.196 mm, equivalent to f/3.52. A nominal f/3.5 entrance marginal
at 14.277 mm radius reaches the stop at 11.248 mm, slightly beyond the
opening. The optical rims admit that marginal; the iris accounts for the
small difference between the paraxial and finite-aperture f-numbers.

The surfaces stay clear of each other. The 12.0° chief ray reaches
21.09 mm image height without blockage. Wide-open geometric transmission
is about 99.8% there. Chief rays also reach 20°, with increasing vignetting
at the front and rear rims.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The 10.9 mm diaphragm space is divided into 7.6 mm after R4 and 3.3 mm
before R5, placing the stop 23.1 mm behind the first vertex. This station,
the iris diameter and every clear semi-diameter are inferred. The retained
clear radii at R1–R7 are 17.5, 17.5, 18, 14, 14.5, 14.5 and 14.5 mm.
Minimum air clearance between the groups is about 6.04 mm; R4's rim clears
the stop plane by about 2.74 mm. Figure 2 supplies relative rim proportions;
its different R2/R5 curvatures do not establish Example II's absolute rim sizes.

The ±12.0° field is illustrative. The text's useful angle of about 60°
describes the design class without specifying Example II's useful field.
No image-circle diameter or image format is established for this example.

`focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. The patent gives
no close-focus spacings, travel or minimum focus distance. The selectable
f-stops extend from f/3.5 to f/22, but the source supplies no minimum iris
opening or production stop range.

Helium d at 587.6 nm is inferred from the catalogue agreement of four
element index/Abbe pairs; none of those pairs agrees at sodium D, 589.3 nm.
The patent itself says only “yellow ray.” The named glasses supply
approximate dispersion curves without establishing a historical supplier
or melt. L2 uses S-FSL5 as a spectral proxy, retaining the printed index
1.4875 and Abbe number 70.0. All five elements use catalogue curves.
No measured partial dispersion or MTF curves are supplied.

## Product Identification

No production lens is documented for this five-element, two-group
asymmetric prescription. The patent establishes the design and Schneider
assignment without a commercial model name. Marketing specifications,
mounts, a production image format and diaphragm blade details are not
assigned.
