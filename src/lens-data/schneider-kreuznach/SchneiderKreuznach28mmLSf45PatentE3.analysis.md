## Optical Architecture

The14 powered elements form10 air-separated groups: four negative singlets, a positive/negative cemented pair, a smaller positive/negative pair before the stop, an isolated positive, a negative/positive pair, a positive meniscus, and a final positive/negative pair. Four front negative menisci distribute the retrofocus divergence; the rear positive assembly provides a long back focus. GR1 contains the first six elements; GR2 contains the remaining eight and the stop. The actual source stop is surface15, with d14=8.5441mm and d15=2.2231mm.

The maker section includes an additional terminal plate-like body absent from the patent. Its identity, power, glass, thickness and placement are unprescribed. No plate is invented or treated as optically neutral in this patent model. The maker's blue section stroke has no acquired legend, so it does not independently establish production asphere manufacture.
