# SCHNEIDER-KREUZNACH XENAR 100mm f/3.5 (US 2,812,689 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

[US 2,812,689 A, _Unsymmetrical Photographic Objective with Three Air-Spaced Members_](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2812689A/en), by Wolfram Wilhelm Albrecht, assigned to Jos. Schneider & Co., Kreuznach. The drawing names the inventor Wolfram W. Albrecht; the company is listed here as Jos. Schneider & Co., Optische Werke. German priority is 13 March 1954, US filing 10 March 1955, and publication 12 November 1957.

The sole numerical example appears in column 2 on PDF p. 2 and is repeated in the claim, column 3 on PDF p. 3. The sole figure is on PDF p. 1. Related publications are GB 769,241 A and FR 1,120,790 A.

Franke & Heidecke's [Rolleicord V instruction manual](https://www.cameramanuals.org/rolleiflex/rolleicord_v.pdf), printed p. 3, documents the Xenar f/3.5 product name. The [Tessar family account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessar#Tessar-derived_lenses) lists the Schneider Xenar among Tessar-derived lenses. [Schott F2](https://us.shop.schott.com/advanced-optics/en/Optical-Glass/F2/c/glass-F2) and [CDGM H-LaK6A](https://www.cdgmgd.com/database/toWebDatabase.htm?k=Products_Data&pageIndex=9&url=database) supply the glass comparisons below.

## Transcription Check

All seven signed radii, six thicknesses and air spaces, four nd/νd pairs and the 33.83 total optical track agree with both tables re-read against the page images. Both print the front index 1.69347. The cemented junction is R6, and the 7.06 diaphragm space lies between R4 and R5. No transcription mismatch was found. The text explicitly identifies the yellow helium line, supporting the retained d-line reference.

The source gives focal length 100 without a physical length unit. Its dimensions are represented in millimetres at the printed scale. All refracting surfaces are spherical, and no variable spacing is specified. The drawing identifies the front, central and rear members as I, II and III.

## First-Order Check

The calculated paraxial EFL is 100.13 mm against the printed 100, a difference of +0.1%. The paraxial f-number is 3.50, matching the printed f/3.5, with stop radius 11.51 mm. BFD is 83.21 mm, placing the image plane at paraxial infinity focus, 117.04 mm behind the first vertex. The optical track is 33.83 mm; the patent prints no back focus.

Finite-aperture estimates are f/3.55 from the entrance pupil and f/3.56 from the image-side sine angle. An f/3.5 axial marginal ray requires about 11.69 mm at the stop. The retained aperture differs from nominal by less than 3%, and the iris sets the full aperture without clipping at a lens rim. Rays admitted by the iris reach the image through the illustrative ±5.0° field.

The minimum glass edge is 0.97 mm in the rear doublet. The R4–R5 common-radius gap clearance is 3.11 mm; front and rear stop-side rim clearances are 0.82 and 2.25 mm. The maximum rim angle is 28.9°. The surfaces stay clear of each other without a gap-clearance exception.

The first air space is 67.6% of the diaphragm space, the front thickness is 9.8% of nominal focal length, and R6 is 99.0% of the optical track, satisfying the patent's dimensional conditions. The printed values retain their small focal-length residual.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The stop position divides the printed 7.06 mm diaphragm space into 4.50 mm after R4 and 2.56 mm before R5, placing it 21.90 mm behind the first vertex. The figure draws no diaphragm. The stop opening follows the paraxial f-number; both its exact position and diameter are inferred.

The estimated clear semi-diameters are 18 / 18 / 16 / 15 / 16 / 15.5 / 15.5 mm. The drawing suggests approximately 17.1 / 17.1 / 12.5 / 11.7 / 12.6 / 13.7 / 13.7 mm, especially narrower at L2 and the rear doublet. The model's L4 edge is about 0.97 mm thick, compared with about 2.4 mm in the drawing. The retained rims are not drawing measurements or measured barrel and baffle dimensions.

The image plane is calculated from the prescription. The ±5.0° field is illustrative; the source prints no field angle, image circle or image format. No image-circle diameter is assigned. No filter or sensor plate is specified or included.

`focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. Both focus endpoints have the same geometry; no close-focus distance or group movement law is given. The selectable f-stops span f/3.5 to f/32, but the patent documents only the full aperture. The f/32 endpoint is not a published minimum opening.

The patent supplies no glass trade names, separate C-, F- or g-line indices, or partial dispersions. F2 supplies an approximate dispersion curve for L2; the other three glasses use Abbe-based estimates. Historical suppliers, melts, measured broadband performance and apochromatic correction are not established. No special-dispersion designation is assigned.

## Product Identification

The four-element, three-group architecture is Tessar-type, and the patent cites Rudolph's US 721,240. The Schneider attribution and the documented Xenar family support the name's family association. No source ties this prescription to the Rolleicord V or to a specific production Xenar. Tronnier's US 2,084,714 and DE 753,329 describe different prescriptions.

No marketed focal length, marketed f-number, marketed minimum focus distance, mount, production image format, diaphragm blade count or blade shape is assigned. The title retains the printed 100 mm design scale.

## Corrections, 2026-11-21

- The key and title changed to identify Schneider-Kreuznach, the Xenar family and US 2,812,689. The maker changed from unspecified to Schneider-Kreuznach, and the lens became publicly visible. The Xenar name denotes the family association; no production identification is claimed.
- The patent designation gained the A publication code. The inventor is recorded as Wolfram W. Albrecht, as on the drawing; the assignee is recorded as Jos. Schneider & Co., Optische Werke. The 1957 publication year, sole example, helium-d index reference and estimated-rim origin are retained.
- The subtitle reads “US 2,812,689 A, sole example — Schneider / Albrecht.” The specifications are “4 ELEMENTS / 3 GROUPS”, “ALL SPHERICAL”, “DESIGN f = 100 mm” and “DESIGN F/3.5”, distinguishing the printed scale from a marketed lens specification.
- Generic element descriptions became Element 1 through Element 4, with shapes Positive Meniscus, Biconcave Negative, Biconcave Negative and Biconvex Positive. Optical roles and standalone in-air focal lengths of +55.12, −35.44, −49.62 and +28.09 mm were added. The rear cemented pair has calculated focal length +59.74 mm.
- L1 is labeled `Unmatched (nd 1.69347 / ν 53.5)`. Its tentative H-LaK6A identification was removed because that catalog glass has nd 1.69350 / νd 53.38, differences of +0.00003 / −0.12, outside the printed precision. L2 is labeled `F2 (Schott)`: nd 1.62004 / νd 36.37, differences of 0 at the printed index precision and +0.07 in Abbe number. F2 approximates dispersion without identifying a historical supplier. L3 and L4 are labeled `Unmatched (nd 1.62364 / ν 36.7)` and `Unmatched (nd 1.74472 / ν 44.7)`. L3 has no catalog match within 0.0001 in index and 0.15 in Abbe number; L4 differs by about 0.00072 in index from nearby LaF2-family rows.
- C1 identifies both members of the rear cemented doublet and spans R5–R7, replacing the earlier group identifier on those elements. Group brackets identify the patent's members I, II and III.
- The catalog design focal length changed from 100 to 100.13 mm to state the calculated EFL. The printed f/3.5, four elements and three groups remain. Production-only specifications and an unsupported image-circle diameter remain omitted.
- The focus description states infinity focus and the absence of close-focus data. The infinity distance, all printed radii, thicknesses, spaces, indices and Abbe numbers, the inferred stop, estimated rims, field and image plane are retained.

The resulting first-order values are EFL 100.13 mm, paraxial f/3.50, BFD 83.21 mm, stop radius 11.51 mm and half-field 5.0°.

## Remaining Questions

- The narrower drawn rims and the difference in L4 edge thickness remain unexplained; the exact physical clear apertures, barrel and baffle dimensions are unknown.
- L1, L3 and L4 lack matching catalog curves at the stated precision. Historical glass suppliers and melt identities remain unknown for all elements.
- No source identifies a production lens, mount, image format, field angle, image circle, blade count or shape, minimum iris opening, marketed specifications or close-focus mechanism for this prescription. The exact diaphragm position is also unprinted.
- The numerical contents of GB 769,241 A and FR 1,120,790 A remain unconfirmed against the US tables.
