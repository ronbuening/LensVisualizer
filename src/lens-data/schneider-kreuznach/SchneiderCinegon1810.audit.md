Automatic ENV/RIM readings are contaminated by leaders and cross-hatching. Manual high-resolution review
identifies the front stepped optical boundaries, second-doublet bevel, and common rear half-height of roughly
240 pixels (7.2 mm). Flange and leader extents are excluded. The stop is unchanged. The revised silhouette has
larger front doublets and a substantially narrower rear component, as drawn. Earlier pupil-clearance claims
were removed because they described the larger rear rims.

Display prefix standardized from SCHNEIDER to SCHNEIDER-KREUZNACH. The patent's nine nd/vd pairs are retained.
Removed all eight sets of catalog-derived nC/nF/ng: these were proxy values, not patent measurements, and bypassed
the runtime resolver. Eight elements resolve to compatible catalog curves. L4 (1.5128 / 57.2) remains unmatched;
no new validated coefficient source was found. Supplier identities remain unproven.
