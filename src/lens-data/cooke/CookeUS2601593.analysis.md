## Optical Architecture

Six elements form four air groups, arranged 1+2 | 3 | 4 | 5+6. The front and rear groups are divergent cemented menisci, with bonds at R2 and R9. The inner groups are positive crystalline-calcium-fluoride menisci. The source is all-spherical; no asphere, plate, baffle or extra element is added.

The exact normalized radii and axial intervals are multiplied by 100 without focal rescaling. Powered track is 38.3 mm at this scale. A flat bookkeeping stop is distinct from the ten optical boundaries. Its physical diameter and the optical clear radii are not printed source measurements.
