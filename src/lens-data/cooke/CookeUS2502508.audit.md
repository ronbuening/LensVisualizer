# COOKE 100mm f/2.67 (US 2,502,508 Design) — Audit

## Source

US 2,502,508 A, Example I and Figure 1. Gordon Henry Cook is the inventor; Taylor, Taylor & Hobson Limited is the assignee. The application was filed on 29 November 1948, claiming British priority of 18 October 1948, and published on 4 April 1950. Two numerical examples are printed; Example II changes only the rear doublet.

## Transcription

Two independent readings of the patent page images agree on all seven signed radii, six glass thicknesses and air separations, four D-line indices and four Abbe numbers. No transcription mismatch was found. The unit-focal-length dimensions are multiplied by 100 to express the model in millimetres. The 0.114F rear air separation remains 11.4 mm, split 7.2 + 4.2 mm around the inferred stop. Figure 1 confirms the cemented R6 interface and the biconvex, biconcave, biconvex and negative-meniscus element shapes.

## First-Order Properties

The computed EFL is 99.9675298253 mm against nominal 100 mm, with paraxial f/2.670 and back focus 76.2972883785 mm. The stored image gap equals that paraxial back focus. The 46.2 mm front-to-rear track reproduces the printed 0.462F. Normalized component powers +1.744, −3.230 and +1.924 reproduce the printed approximate +1.75, −3.23 and +1.92.

At the modeled opening, real marginal rays give entrance and image-space sine equivalents of f/2.72952 and f/2.73764. The separately computed stop radius 14.24704 mm for a ray entering at the paraxial f/2.67 pupil edge is not applied; the model retains the paraxially derived 13.91783 mm radius.

## Quantities Not Printed

The stop at z = 30 mm, its bore and all individual glass rims are inferred. The R1 radius 18.75 mm uses the printed effective front diameter 0.375F; that diameter is not a measurement of the physical rim. The final image gap is computed. The ±5° field is illustrative, and the focus distance 1e15 denotes infinity. The patent supplies no close-focus data, exact angular field, image format, supplier identities or full spectral curves.

## Product Evidence

No documented commercial embodiment is established. Period Taylor Hobson Ortal and Adotal lenses are not tied to this prescription. The separate six-element US 2,502,509 design does not identify this four-element objective.

## Corrections

The earlier account of the R6 contact is confirmed by the page images. The earlier description of a millimetre source prescription is corrected: the patent uses a unit focal length without a physical length unit. The published dimensions, indices and dispersions need no correction.

## Remaining Questions

Open: Figure 1 draws larger rear rims and a near-zero edge on Element 3 at R5/R6. Individual apertures are undimensioned; the delivered R5/R6/R7 radii of 17/16/17 mm remain estimates. A later comparison of the optical rims must respect Element 3's closing edge near 17.1 mm. The inferred stop, historical glass identities, commercial embodiment and usable image field remain unresolved.
