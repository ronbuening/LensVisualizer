# COOKE 100mm f/4 (US 2,601,593 Design) — Audit

## Source

US 2,601,593 A, Example VI, Figure 6. Gordon Henry Cook; Taylor, Taylor & Hobson Limited. British priority 15 February 1951; US filing 8 March 1951; published 24 June 1952. The patent gives nine examples. Figure 6 is on PDF p. 2, the table on p. 6, column 6, and the field, diaphragm and material discussion on p. 7, column 7.

## Transcription

Two independent readings of the page images agree on all ten radii, nine physical thicknesses and air separations, six sodium-D refractive indices and six Abbe numbers. The normalized dimensional values use 100 mm per source unit. The central separation of 0.040 becomes 4.0 mm, split 2.0/2.0 mm around the diaphragm. The cemented interfaces are R2 and R9.

The table and Figure 6 both print R9 = −0.6897; the model's −68.97 mm follows them. The reciprocal magnitude is 1.4499, agreeing with the prose's approximately 1.45. All prescription radii, physical spacings and material coordinates are retained.

## First-Order

The model computes EFL 99.989725480 mm against nominal 100 mm, a difference of −0.0103%; paraxial aperture f/4; rear-vertex BFD 80.713624629 mm; optical vertex distance 38.3 mm; and first vertex to image plane 119.013624629 mm. The stop radius is 9.766264130 mm. The real axial beam clears the iris and all glass rims; its finite entrance-equivalent aperture is approximately f/3.9784.

Earlier spherical-ray comparisons agreed with an independent calculation and found field-edge vignetting. Earlier sodium-D geometric MTF calculations were stable at the center and middle field; the edge remained unconverged, with an approximately 0.0018 change under denser sampling. These results depend on estimated clear radii.

## Not Printed

The text places the diaphragm approximately midway between R5 and R6, but gives no exact station or opening. The exact midpoint, iris diameter, individual clear radii, paraxial image gap and derived image-circle diameter are model choices. The patent recommends effective diameters at selected surfaces and chamfers, but these are not a complete table of clear radii. It gives no close-focus prescription or measured MTF chart.

## Product

No manufactured product is documented for this prescription. The patent's six-element, four-group fluorite design remains identified by its patent and example.
