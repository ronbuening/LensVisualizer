# COOKE 100mm f/1.5 (US 2,398,680 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

Arthur Warmisham, [US 2,398,680 A, “Optical Objective”](https://patents.google.com/patent/US2398680A/en), assigned to Taylor, Taylor & Hobson Limited. British priority is 6 July 1943; the US application was filed on 21 September 1944 and the patent published on 16 April 1946. The patent gives five examples, I–V. This model follows Example I and Figure 1: drawing on PDF page 1, prescription and sodium-D definition on page 4, back focal lengths and material choices on page 5, and the repeated prescription in claim 15 on page 6. The [original scan](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/2398680) supplies the numerical values.

[Taylor Hobson's company history](https://www.taylor-hobson.com/aboutus/history) documents its manufacture of Cooke lenses. It does not identify a product using this prescription.

## First-Order Check

The paraxial EFL is 100.002 mm against the printed 100 mm at this scale; the paraxial f-number is 1.50 against f/1.5. BFD is 57.322 mm against the printed 57.31 mm. The image plane is at calculated paraxial focus, 0.012 mm beyond the printed back focus. The first-to-last optical vertex distance is 80.52 mm and the first-vertex-to-image distance is 137.84 mm.

The inferred stop radius is 20.724 mm, giving a diameter of 41.448 mm. An exact axial ray entering at the nominal marginal height reaches 20.651 mm at the stop. The magnitudes of R5 and R6 sum to 0.6304 of the nominal focal length, within the patent's 0.63–0.64 interval. The last singlet supplies about 42.1% of the whole objective's power by thick-lens calculation, or 42.2% by thin-lens calculation, against the printed 42.3%.

The surfaces stay clear of each other and the iris sets the axial aperture. The minimum shared-radius glass thickness is 1.887 mm; minimum glass thickness at the individual rim endpoints is 0.463 mm. Central air clearance is 3.210 mm at the common radius and 2.527 mm at the individual rim endpoints. Sag occupies about 84.0% of the central axial gap; maximum rim angle is 56.2°. Straight tapered sidewalls between the estimated rims leave a minimum radial ray clearance of about 0.0066 mm, and the rim remains at least 1.16 mm clear of the stop plane. These values describe estimated glass blanks, without establishing manufacturing tolerances.

The 10.0° chief ray reaches an image height of about 17.62 mm, and chief rays reach the image throughout the illustrative field. Marginal rays are vignetted off axis: the one-sided clipping estimates at 10.0° are about 12.0% at R7 and 9.0% at R10–R12. The lower marginal ray at 10.0° is unresolved, so a complete edge bundle is not established. A 2.0% increase in iris radius causes axial clipping; moving the assumed iris by 0.5 mm in either direction changes off-axis illumination.

Numerical sensitivity of the monochromatic sodium-D geometric MTF over 0–40 cycles/mm at paraxial focus is about 0.00006 on axis, 0.0011 at 5.0° and 0.0010 at 10.0°. These values do not establish measured brightness or broadband image quality.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The patent gives no iris position or diameter, clear semi-diameters, numerical field, image format, image-circle diameter or close-focus data. The inferred stop lies 42.22 mm from the first vertex, 11.10 mm after R5 and 8.96 mm before R6. Its radius follows paraxial f/1.5; the final image spacing follows the calculated paraxial BFD. The f/1.5–f/22 aperture range has no published minimum opening.

All clear radii are estimates. Figure 1 is approximately to scale: its optical vertex positions agree within about 0.5 mm, and the R5/R6 curves give about 10.0–10.1 pixels/mm along and across the axis. Several estimated rims are larger than the drawing:

No rim is trimmed to the drawing and no land is added.

The 10.0° half-field and 20.0° full field are illustrative. The calculated chief-ray image height does not establish an image circle, and no diameter is assigned. `focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. No finite-focus movement is specified.

The sodium-D reference is explicit. No catalog row matches within 0.0001 in D-line index and 0.15 in Abbe number; all seven labels remain `Unmatched (…)`. The patent gives no individual C-, F- or g-line indices or partial dispersions. Abbe-based colour estimates do not establish measured broadband performance, historical glass suppliers or melt identities.

## Product Identification

No commercial product is documented for this prescription. The patent describes photographic, projection or similar uses without naming a marketed lens. Comparisons with other Cooke designs establish no matching prescription; this does not establish an exhaustive production history. The Cooke name follows the assignee's manufacture of Cooke lenses, and the title retains the 100 mm design scale.

No marketed focal length, marketed f-number, mount, production image format, diaphragm blade count or shape, marketed minimum focus distance or production aperture range is assigned.

## Corrections, 2026-11-21

- The identity changed from inventor-and-example wording to the Cooke maker-and-patent form: maker Cooke and title “COOKE 100mm f/1.5 (US 2,398,680 Design)”. The key follows the same identity. The patent designation gained its A suffix and the assignee's legal name gained “Limited”; Example I, Arthur Warmisham and 1946 are retained.
- Generic element descriptions became Element 1 through Element 7, with shape descriptions matching the radius signs: positive menisci for L1/L2, a negative meniscus for L3, biconcave negative for L4 and biconvex positive for L5–L7. Optical roles and in-air focal lengths were added: +150.49, +66.70, −43.24, −44.37, +52.23, +196.87 and +237.57 mm.
- C1 identifies the cemented L2/L3 pair with a bracket spanning R3–R5; C2 identifies L4/L5 with a bracket spanning R6–R8. The five component brackets follow the seven-element, five-group arrangement.
- General glass descriptions became explicit unmatched labels containing the printed pairs in the table above. Every label change has Δn = 0 and Δν = 0. The sodium-D index reference is retained; no catalog glass or historical supplier is assigned.
- The inferred 35.23 mm image-circle diameter was removed because the illustrative field supplies no published coverage. No mount or image format is assigned.
- The subtitle reads “US 2,398,680 A, Example I — Taylor Hobson / Warmisham”. The specification chips are “7 ELEMENTS / 5 GROUPS”, “ALL SPHERICAL”, “DESIGN f = 100 mm” and “DESIGN F/1.5”; 100 mm is the chosen scale of the patent's normalized prescription. The focus description states infinity focus and the absence of close-focus data.

After these changes, EFL is 100.002 mm, the paraxial f-number is 1.50, BFD is 57.322 mm, stop radius is 20.724 mm and half-field is 10.0°, identical to their starting values. No printed radius, thickness, airspace, glass coordinate, index line, stop station, rim, field angle, image spacing or focus distance has changed.

## Remaining Questions

- Historical glass suppliers and melts are unknown; measured spectral indices and partial dispersions are unavailable.
- Actual iris geometry, clear radii, lands and edge shapes remain unknown. Several estimated rims exceed Figure 1, and illumination at the edge of the illustrative field depends on those estimates.
- No numerical field, image circle or production format is documented. A complete unvignetted marginal bundle at 10.0° is not established.
- The small difference between printed and calculated back focus, and the difference from the printed rear-component power percentage, remain unexplained.
- No source identifies a production lens, mount, diaphragm blade count or shape, minimum iris opening, marketed specifications, close-focus distance or focusing mechanism.
