# COOKE 100mm f/2.5 (US 2,298,993 Design) — Audit

## Source

US 2,298,993 A, “Optical Objective,” names Arthur Warmisham as inventor and Taylor, Taylor & Hobson Limited as assignee. The US application was filed on 6 December 1940, with British priority dated 12 December 1939, and published on 13 October 1942. The modeled prescription is Example IV / Figure 4, one of five examples.

## Transcription

Two independent readings of the patent page images agree on all ten signed radii, five glass thicknesses, four air separations, five D-line indices and five Abbe numbers. Figure 4 repeats the Example IV radii and thickness/separation labels. No aspherical surfaces or cemented interfaces are present. The printed S3 separation remains 4.0 mm, split 3.51 + 0.49 mm around the inferred stop. The front air space remains 0.1 mm.

## First-Order Properties

The printed prescription gives EFL 99.619436 mm against nominal 100 mm (−0.38%), paraxial f/2.500 and paraxial BFD 76.966226 mm. The vertex span is 39.80 mm and the total track is 116.766226 mm. The normalized examples calculate across approximately 0.990–1.008; the printed three-decimal index precision alone permits about ±0.7% worst-case variation. The computed longitudinal spherical aberration of approximately +0.005 mm at the f/2.5 margin supports retaining the table. No particular numerical correction is supported.

The inferred paraxial iris radius is 15.032354 mm. Real-ray entrance-pupil evaluation at that opening gives about f/2.619, and the image-space sine measure gives f/2.616. A radius near 15.825585 mm would admit the real-ray f/2.5 marginal ray, but the saved paraxial aperture convention is retained.

## Not Printed

The stop station and bore, clear semi-diameters, final image distance and Example IV field are not printed. The stop lies in S3 at z = 23.31 mm; a real-ray coma comparison supports this space. Its plane clears the R6 and R7 rims. The image plane is the paraxial focus. The ±10° field is illustrative, and the 25° semi-angle statement belongs to Example V's glass arrangement. No image format or image circle is assigned. No close-focus data are provided.

## Product

No production lens is documented as using this five-singlet prescription.

## Corrections

The numerical prescription requires no correction. Catalog review leaves all three glass pairs Unmatched, with the printed D-line indices and Abbe numbers retained. The source's capitalization is preserved as D, taken as sodium at 589.3 nm.
