# COOKE 100mm f/6.3 (US 2,354,503 Design) — Audit Log

## Source

[US 2,354,503 A, _Optical Objective of the Telephoto Type_](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/2354503), by Arthur Cox of Leicester, England, assigned to Taylor, Taylor & Hobson Limited. The US application was filed on 12 December 1942, claiming British priority of 1 December 1941, and published on 25 July 1944. The sole numerical example appears on PDF page 2 and is repeated in claim 9 on PDF page 3. The drawing is on PDF page 1.

The example states equivalent focal length 1.000, relative aperture f/6.3 and a rear/front clear-aperture ratio of 1.20. The description explicitly identifies D-line indices and Abbe numbers. No source establishes a production product using this prescription.

## Transcription Check

All seven signed radii, four glass thicknesses, two air separations and four D-line index/Abbe pairs agree with the page images. The drawing, description table and claim 9 table agree. All printed dimensions are multiplied by 100 for the nominal 100 mm scale. No transcription mismatch or supported digit correction was found.

The four elements form three air-separated glass groups. R2 is the front cemented interface; Elements III and IV form the patent's divergent, air-spaced rear doublet member. The printed conditions agree with the prescription: the cemented index step is 0.063; the rear air-lens curvature difference is about 0.22 of the objective power; the inter-member space is 0.235 of the nominal focal length; and the rear-element thicknesses are 0.014 and 0.027 of that length. All seven refracting boundaries are spherical.

## First-Order Check

At the nominal 100 mm scale, the printed prescription gives EFL 99.49 mm, 0.5% below the stated focal length. Rounding may explain the difference, but remains unproven; no misprint has been identified. Paraxial back focus is 50.28 mm, the glass-to-glass vertex span is 32.80 mm and the first-vertex-to-image distance is 83.08 mm. The stored image plane coincides with the computed paraxial plane behind R7; the patent prints no back focus.

The paraxial aperture is f/6.30, agreeing with the printed f/6.3, with an iris radius of 6.895 mm. The real marginal ray gives about f/6.48: the front member's spherical aberration carries the real f/6.3 ray to 7.11 mm at the stop. No rim narrows the axial beam, and no glass crosses the diaphragm plane. The surfaces stay clear of each other.

Chief rays at ±12.0° clear every rim and reach an image semi-height of 21.60 mm at the paraxial plane. Off-axis bundles remain partly vignetted at the internal rear rims; chief-ray clearance does not establish full-pupil illumination.

## Values Not Printed in the Patent

The absolute focal-length scale, clear radii, diaphragm position and bore, back focus, image plane, image format and close-focus behavior are not specified. The exact field of the numerical example is also unstated. Its printed aperture ratio and inequalities, 3.825 tan θ to 6.50 tan θ, imply a half-field of about 10.5–17.4°. The retained ±12.0° field lies within that interval.

The inferred stop lies 1.5 mm behind R3 and 22 mm before R4, preserving the printed 23.5 mm inter-member gap. It is 6.6 mm behind the first vertex, with 0.748 mm clearance behind R3's outer rim. At this station, the rear/front clear-aperture ratio needed by the external faces is about 1.09 at 10.0° and 1.18 at 12.0°, close to the printed 1.20.

Clear radii are 8.2 mm at R1–R2, 8.3 mm at R3–R6 and 9.84 mm at R7, preserving the external ratio 9.84/8.2 = 1.20. The drawing suggests front/rear outlines near 10.3/10.7 mm at the nominal 100 mm scale, with the R3/R4 curves ending near 9.4/9.0 mm. Those outlines cannot be applied throughout the rear member: the R5–R6 space closes near 8.88 mm height. At the retained 8.3 mm internal rims, the 0.1 mm axial air gap retains 0.014 mm edge clearance, consuming 85.8% of the gap. Minimum glass edge thickness is 1.031 mm.

`focus.closeFocusM` is 10¹⁵ m, which stands for infinity. No close-focus motion is described. The selectable aperture range is f/6.3 to f/32; the patent supplies no minimum iris opening. No image circle is assigned because the exact example field and film format are unstated. The traced image height alone does not establish either value.

All four elements retain the printed glass coordinates. Elements 1 and 3 use the BACD8 catalog dispersion shape as a spectral proxy; Elements 2 and 4 retain an Abbe-based dispersion estimate. The source provides no glass names, suppliers, C-, F- or g-line indices, partial dispersions or transmission data. Secondary-spectrum, apochromatic and measured production performance remain unknown.

## Product Identification

No documented commercial embodiment links this numerical prescription to a named lens. The Cooke title places the Taylor Hobson patent among Cooke designs without assigning a production model. No mount, image format, marketed focal length or aperture, minimum focus distance, diaphragm blade count or blade shape is assigned.
