# COOKE 100mm f/2.5 (US 2,366,597 Design) — Audit

## Source

Arthur Cox, US 2,366,597 A, “Optical Objective,” assigned to Taylor, Taylor & Hobson Limited. Filed November 18, 1942; published January 2, 1945; British priority September 17, 1941. The prescription is Example I on PDF p. 4, illustrated by Figure 1 on PDF p. 1. The patent has seven examples. Claims 35–37 reproduce Examples II, III and VII, not Example I.

## Transcription

The nine signed radii, six element thicknesses, two air spaces and six D-line index/Abbe pairs agree with the Example I page image. All radius signs are printed. The three cemented interfaces are R2, R5 and R8; the two front doublets converge and the rear doublet diverges. No transcription mismatch or source correction is identified.

## First-order

At the chosen focal scale, the nominal focal length is 100 mm and the computed EFL is 99.867750695 mm, a difference of −0.13225%. The paraxial aperture is f/2.5 and the calculated BFD is 48.908493251 mm. Total length is 87.008493251 mm, or 0.871237 of EFL, satisfying the stated limit below 7/8. Axial D-line spherical aberration is −0.016 mm at the full nominal entrance radius and −0.126 mm at 70%, consistent with the patent's discussion of zonal correction.

The saved iris radius is 14.387904444 mm. The corresponding real-ray entrance and image-sine equivalents are approximately f/2.65708 and f/2.65707; this is f/2.66 at the paraxial iris. A radius near 15.42828 mm would pass the nominal f/2.5 real marginal ray. The saved iris retains the paraxial setting.

## Not Printed

The patent states unit focal length without physical units. It supplies no stop station, stop diameter, clear radii, back focus, numerical field, image format, image circle or close-focus schedule. The stop at z = 25.3 mm splits the preserved 6.5 mm air space into 3 and 3.5 mm. The last gap is the computed paraxial BFD; the ±5° field and all clear radii are inferred.

The first model retained positive surface clearances, with a minimum glass clearance of 1.46345 mm over the shared clear radius, minimum air clearance of 1.4 mm and minimum glass thickness at an individual rim of 0.51215 mm. No increased gap allowance was needed. Geometric image-quality calculations at the D line did not establish measured or broadband performance.

## Product

No production lens is documented as using Example I. The Cooke Telepanchro line has no established prescription link to this patent.

## Corrections

The numerical prescription needs no correction. Statements that the page images had not been checked and that 100 mm was a printed physical scale are superseded by the independent readings and the source's unit-focal-length table. The Cooke identity and public descriptions follow the assigned name and source attribution; no mount or image format is inferred from them.

## Remaining Questions

Open: Figure 1 suggests clear radii near 23.5 mm for the first two doublets and 16 mm for the rear doublet, with about 13 mm at R9. The model's R4–R6 radii are 20 / 17.4 / 17 mm and R7–R9 are 14 / 14 / 11.5 mm. Any enlargement must retain positive edge thickness; Element 3 approaches a knife edge near 22.2 mm and Element 5 near 16.4 mm. The stop location, historical glass melts and any production-lens association remain unknown. The patent supplies no wider-field specification.
