# COOKE 27mm f/1.5 (US 1,955,617 Design) — audit

## Source

Arthur Warmisham, US 1,955,617 A, _Photographic and Projection Lens_, assigned to Kapella Limited. Filed 27 January 1931, with British priority 28 January 1930; published 17 April 1934. Figure 1 is on PDF p. 1 and its numerical table is on PDF p. 2. Figures 2 and 3 are separate f/1.4 examples. The illumination arrows establish left-to-right propagation.

## Transcription

Two independent readings of the page images agree on every Figure 1 radius and sign, all six glass thicknesses, three air separations, six nD/Abbe pairs, four Chance numbers and two blank code cells. The dimensions are converted from inches to millimetres by a factor of 25.4. The first air interval remains .002 in = 0.0508 mm. The central .130 in air interval remains 3.302 mm across the inserted stop. R4 is cemented and R7 is a plane cemented interface. No transcription mismatch, asphere or variable spacing was found.

## First-order

The patent prints focal length 1.059 in = 26.8986 mm and f/1.5. The prescription computes to EFL 26.929068572551163 mm, 0.11327% longer, at paraxial f/1.500. Computed BFD and the stored image gap are 11.936636404836825 mm. The refracting-vertex track is 24.892 mm and total distance to the image is 36.82863640483682 mm. The Petzval sum is 0.025832066633449467 mm⁻¹; multiplied by EFL it gives 0.6956, reproducing the printed .696. The patent's earlier-design comparison is .809.

The 6.814004908152711 mm stop radius admits an axial input radius of 8.4378704141 mm, giving real-ray f/1.5957266023, about f/1.60 against the paraxial f/1.5. An input radius of 8.9763561909 mm for real-ray f/1.5 would require stop radius 7.3235616592 mm; that ray clears the existing element rims. The stop opening remains unchanged.

## Not printed

The patent supplies no diaphragm position or diameter, clear radii, back focus, field angle, format, finite projection conjugate or close-focus data. The model places the stop midway between R5 and R6 at z = 10.795 mm, infers the stop opening from paraxial f/1.5, estimates all clear radii, and uses the computed Gaussian infinity plane. The ±3° field is illustrative. Its chief-ray image height of 1.4118353741 mm does not establish an image circle.

Earlier calculations found positive glass thickness and open air spaces without trimming or altered geometry limits: minimum glass thickness 0.508 mm, first-air clearance 0.0508 mm, R8–R9 clearance 1.2030198825 mm, and stop-opening clearance at least 1.133101792 mm in front and 1.651 mm behind. No glass crosses the stop plane. Rays admitted by the modeled stop reached the image throughout the illustrative field; clipping of a larger launch pupil occurred at the iris. Geometric D-line MTF changed by at most 0.00114969 on the final numerical refinement through 40 lp/mm. These are calculated properties, not measured performance.

The earlier sensitivity work retained the prescription while varying estimated apertures. Common clear-radius changes of ±0.1 mm retained valid geometry; stop-radius changes of ±2% gave f/1.624085 and f/1.568592. Stop positions z = 10.545, 10.795 and 11.045 mm gave f/1.595309, f/1.595727 and f/1.596150 while preserving the full 3.302 mm gap and focal length. These are assumption sensitivities, not manufacturing tolerances.

## Product

No product is documented as using this prescription. An unverified forum description suggested a Kinic connection, but supplied no confirmed construction diagram matching Figure 1. The Cooke name identifies the catalog family; it does not establish a production model.

## Corrections

No printed radius, thickness, separation or glass constant has been corrected. The Petzval value, previously described only as context, is reproduced by the model. The former catalog image circle came solely from the selected field and is removed. The source's four Chance codes remain attached to their printed elements; the blank code cells for Elements 4 and 6 remain unresolved.

## Remaining questions

The historical diaphragm dimensions, actual clear apertures, field coverage, full spectral behavior and production identity remain undocumented. The rear clear radii differ from Figure 1 and await a separate semi-diameter review.
