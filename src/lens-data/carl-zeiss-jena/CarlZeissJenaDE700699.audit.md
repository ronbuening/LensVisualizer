# Audit Log — CARL ZEISS JENA 100mm f/2 (DE 700,699 Design)

Patent: DE 700,699 C, sole example, printed F = 100 mm and 1:2.

## 2026-11-20 — Deployment validation and cleanup

The independent validator reread the page images at 300/600 dpi and checked all ten radii, seven glass thicknesses, seven nd/νd pairs, the 0.38 mm first air space and the 9.44 mm second air space with its 7.50/1.94 mm stop split; no transcription mismatch was found. The verdict was valid with notes: the historical Sonnar account identifies an improved-distortion Sonnar 2/5 cm that did not enter series production, so no production-product identity is assigned.

### Source and field decisions

The front page, table heading, drawing crop and historical distortion plot were also inspected during this pass. The front page is the Patentschrift issued 3 January 1941, naming Ludwig Bertele and Zeiss Ikon Akt.-Ges.; the table prints lowercase nd. The stop split is printed in the table, while the stop diameter and image plane are inferred. The drawing supports approximately 28 mm at R1/R2 and 23.7 mm at R3–R5 and R8–R10, measured by the validator at about 12.1 pixels/mm from vertex spacings.

The [historical Sonnar account](https://www.zeissikonveb.de/start/objektive/normalobjektive/sonnar.html), section 5.2, supplies the link to the unproduced improved-distortion design. Its plot labeled “nach DRP 700.699” reaches 22° and +0.98% distortion. The real chief-ray height is 40.805 mm at 22°, so 81.61 mm is used as the inferred image-circle diameter at the patent's 100 mm scale. No mount, production image-format id, marketed focal length or marketed minimum focus distance is inferred.
