## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** US 2,793,565 A
**Filed:** 26 April 1955
**Granted:** 28 May 1957
**Inventors:** Harry Zöllner; Rudolf Solisch
**Assignee:** VEB Carl Zeiss Jena
**Example details:** Whole first embodiment, Figure 1; all lengths multiplied by 0.35.

The 1957 maker catalog identifies a 35 mm f/2.8 Flektogon and a six-element/five-group section with a front negative meniscus, long gap and plano-convex second element. This supports a probable design-family association, not an exact manufactured prescription. The same maker page also covers 12.5 mm and 65 mm versions, so the section is not serial-specific proof. The 35 mm scaling, f/2.8 and full 62° field are retail-envelope hypotheses, not values supplied by the patent. The literal computed BFL is slightly shorter than focal length, whereas the maker says slightly longer. This discrepancy is retained without retuning. The later 0.18 m product is not substituted for the ASB Exakta/Exa version with 0.36 m minimum focus.

## Optical Architecture

The complete source contains 6 glass elements in five air-separated groups, 11 optical interfaces and one explicit stop, giving 12 schema records. All surfaces are spherical or planar. The model is static at infinity.

The explicit source b1 = 7.55 and b2 = 8.09 divide the R7–R8 air gap. No midpoint inference is used. The stop is at 39.7425000000 mm. Its physical diameter is derived by the runtime from the stated model f-number, not recovered from the source.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The source explicitly identifies the d-line. Literal indices and Abbe values are retained. No modern glass is substituted. Abbe-only dispersion cannot establish measured broadband or anomalous-partial-dispersion fidelity.

## Focus and Operating Envelope

The unchanged source, evaluated at collimated infinity, gives EFL 34.965364713964 mm and BFL 34.559067200810 mm. The image plane is 81.620067200810 mm, derived rather than published. No finite-focus motion law is recovered. The required focus.closeFocusM = 1e9 m is an unvalidated schema sentinel only; maker minimum focus 0.36 m remains separate catalog information. Generic focus slider or fallback text cannot establish a validated optical endpoint. No variable gaps are authored.

## Model Qualifications and Validation

Native geometry and reference diagnostics are complete. These frozen files are staged for combined integration with four other models; no independently tested paired installation is claimed. Clear semi-diameters are explicit engineering hypotheses, not measured factory rims. Literal rows, source stop splits and derived image planes are unchanged. Native rendering has zero trim and both iris planes have positive clearance. The standard aperture census is within its 3% tolerance for both models, but its pass must not be confused with mathematically exact iris-limited behavior.

The physically clear rear rim of surface 7 is 5.79 mm. Keeping the exact source stop and the initially required 5.803010 mm beam rim would put the iris plane 0.01344 mm inside the glass surface at the edge. The chosen rim leaves 0.0021003 mm axial clearance but produces a small, explicitly retained axial rim loss: the traced iris-relative equivalent is f/2.807153 rather than exactly f/2.8 (0.255478% penalty). The model is therefore not claimed to be perfectly iris-limited. This is a geometric reconstruction, not manufacturing-clearance certification. No gap-sag or rim-angle override is authored.

Reference-wavelength geometric and diffraction MTF were evaluated at maker-angle hypotheses 0°, 15.5° and 31°, plus the independently solved actual full-frame corner 32.208391206°. Neither patent gives a numerical field. Exact maker angles use chief-height mapping; the temporary 100 mm image-circle normalization exists only in the diagnostic request, not the authored model. Grid-128 nonconvergence is preserved. The supported adaptive cap of 256 converges through sampled 40 lp/mm at all maker angles. A requested 512 grid was rejected by the support guard and is not used. The derived-plane and best-axial results are separate: best-axial shift +0.027699359223 mm is diagnostic only and never authored.

At the actual full-frame corner both methods and both focus modes converge through sampled 40 lp/mm. Broadband accuracy, finite focus, manufactured rim sizes and exact factory performance are not established.
