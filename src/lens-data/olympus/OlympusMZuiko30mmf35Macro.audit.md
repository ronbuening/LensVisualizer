## Transcription

All 16 surface rows, six sets of aspheric coefficients and both variable gaps at all three printed positions
agree with the patent. Dimensions are in millimetres at scale 1. The d-line indices and Abbe numbers are
reproduced as printed. The sag convention uses the printed $k$ directly. Example 6 gives terms through
$A_{10}$. The stop is surface 5, and the cemented interface between L3 and L4 is surface 7.

The camera-side plate at surfaces 15–16 is 6.08 mm thick with $n_d$ 1.51633 and $\nu_d$ 64.14. The 16.04 mm
air gap before it and the 0.75 mm air gap behind it place the image plane 73.69 mm behind the front vertex.

intermediate positions keep the gap sum at 21.72 mm and bring the largest remaining segment-midpoint residual
to 0.0175 mm. Residuals at the calculated positions are within 0.0002 mm after rounding their gaps.

## Product Identification

The Olympus drawing agrees with the seven-element, six-component section, including the L3–L4 cemented pair,
the stop gap and the shallow front of L7. The DSA, EDA and aspherical positions correspond to L1, L5 and L6,
which have aspheres on both faces. The location of L6 agrees with the moving G3 in Fig. 6A–C. Olympus describes
the production mechanism as internal focusing. Examples 2–4 differ in element count or length, and Example 1
has a differently bent rear element and a 41.56° field. The drawing fits Example 5 or 6. Example 6 is closer to
the maker's nominal 40° field by 0.24°. [Olympus construction drawing](https://jp.omsystem.com/content/000083238.jpg)
[Olympus specifications](https://jp.omsystem.com/product/lens/macro/30_35macro/spec.html)

## Corrections

### 2026-11-24

The surface table is reproduced as printed. The small differences between the reported first-order values and
those calculated from the prescription are retained. The glass catalog matches supply dispersion curves and
leave the patented d-line coordinates intact. Olympus's EDA placement and the printed Abbe number support the
inferred designation of L5.

The production metadata describes a 30 mm f/3.5 lens with aperture settings through f/22, a seven-blade circular
diaphragm and close focus at 0.095 m and 1.25×. The modeled aperture is f/3.56 at infinity and the model ends at the
patent's 1× spacing, which focuses at about 0.09761 m. The patent's tabulated 11.15 mm image height exceeds the
10.82 mm Four Thirds corner, so the Four Thirds format sets the analysis field.
[Olympus specifications](https://jp.omsystem.com/product/lens/macro/30_35macro/spec.html)

## Remaining Questions

- The Example 6 table gives IH 11.15 mm, while Fig. 12 labels the aberration plots FIY 11.45 mm.
- The drawing provides estimated rim sizes. The exact clear diameters and the production lens's close-range
  iris behavior remain unconfirmed.
- The production spacing at 1.25× and the distinction between Examples 5 and 6 remain unresolved by
  the published construction drawing. The production glass suppliers and melts are unspecified.
