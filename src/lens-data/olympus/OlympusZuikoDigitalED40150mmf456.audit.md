## 2026-10-28 — Deployment validation and wording pass

Source re-read from the A1 page images (PDF pp. 23–24). It covered 25 surface rows (R, d, nd, νd; the stop at r11; the filter block at r23–r24; the image at r25), 13 material entries and the 3 variable gaps (D3, D10, D22) at WE, ST and TE for infinity and OD = 900 mm, 18 printed gap values in all. There are no asphere coefficients. No mismatches were found, and the file's gaps at the six printed states equal the printed values exactly.

Patent vs model, first-order:
- EFL: 40.926 / 77.514 / 146.715 mm vs 40.9 / 77.5 / 146.7 mm.
- Iris: set to the plot f-numbers 4.080 / 4.800 / 5.811; the table prints 4.1 / 4.8 / 5.8.
- Back focus after the plate: 2.525–2.532 mm paraxial vs the printed 2.50 mm, a native offset.
- Total track: 110.92 / 144.92 / 167.69 mm, the table sums.
- WE half-field: 15.25° in the patent; the model reaches the 10.82 mm half-diagonal at 15.2°.
- Close focus: with the image held at the infinity offset, the paraxial object-to-image distance at OD = 900 mm is 900–904 mm at all 65 stations. TE magnification is 0.143× vs Olympus's 0.14×.

Zoom path:
- Every variable gap is monotonic across the 65 stations.
- G2 reverses once, at ST, as the printed rows require.
- The largest infinity image-plane offset at an inferred station is 0.032 mm.

Validators: audit:surface reports no errors; audit:image-circle reports 0 undersized; audit:field-coverage shows 65 of 65 stations at 100 %. A clearance trace at the 11.60 mm plotted image height, at infinity and at close focus, shows no axial clipping and no blocked chief ray.

Maker diagram: the September 2010 Four Thirds catalogue section shows:
- a cemented front doublet;
- a four-element G2 (meniscus, biconcave–positive-meniscus doublet, meniscus);
- a biconvex singlet and a doublet whose biconvex front element is coloured as the ED element;
- three separated rear elements.

That matches Examples 1 and 3, with L8 (1.4970 / 81.5) as the ED element. Example 2 puts νd 81.5 in the singlet L7. Examples 4 and 5 have an air-spaced front pair (12/10). Examples 1 and 3 use the same glasses, and their radii agree within about 1 %; the largest difference is r10, −229.620 vs −231.956. Only Example 1 prints the OD = 900 mm rows. Olympus's announcement is dated 13 November 2006.
