# Audit Log — OLYMPUS H.ZUIKO AUTO-W 24mm f/2.8

Patent: US 3,994,574, Embodiment 2 (PDF p. 6; repeated in claim 3, PDF pp. 7–8)

## 2026-09-24 — Semi-diameters raised to the traced format corner

Embodiment 2 is normalized to f = 1.0 (scaled ×24 here). FIGS. 4A–4D (Sheet 3, PDF page 4) plot it at 1:2.8 to
ω = 42.5°, where the traced chief ray lands at 21.29 mm (98.3% of the full-frame corner, 21.65 mm); the corner itself is
reached at 42.95°. The estimated front rims (surfaces 1–3 at 10.8 / 10.2 / 8.7 mm) clipped the real chief ray (solved
through the stop centre) from 39.2°, leaving the analysis field at 87% of the corner (18.91 mm). The corner chief ray
needs surface 1 ≥ 12.48, surface 2 ≥ 11.42 and surface 3 ≥ 9.30 mm (12.26 / 11.19 / 9.17 mm at the patent's 42.5°);
surfaces 4 and 5 clear it by 0.12 and 0.21 mm and are unchanged. Surface 1 takes floor + ~0.5 mm and surface 2 is scaled
with it (L1 is a weak meniscus, R 49.16 / 147.28); surface 3 takes floor + ~0.5 mm, and its partner surface 4 (R 9.01;
L2 is a strong meniscus) was not scaled. FIG. 2 (Sheet 1) was read by eye at 300 dpi (≈ 18.3 px/mm from the
prescription's 36.9 mm first-to-last vertex length as drawn; L4's drawn thickness agrees): it draws L1 ≈ 14.9 mm,
L2 ≈ 11.9 mm, L3 ≈ 10.0 mm and L4 ≈ 7.8 mm. The new L1 and L2 values sit 15–20% below the drawing, short of the ~25% the
procedure asks before a figure reading moves a value, so the figure was not used; L1/L4 is now 1.78 against the
drawing's ≈ 1.9 (it was 1.48).

The validator accepts the new values (all surfaces spherical). The traced field now reaches 42.95° and 21.65 mm, 100% of
the corner (it was 39.23° / 18.91 mm, 87%), with every rim clear (surface 4 by 0.12 mm). The image-circle floor still
reports nothing undersized. The analysis quotes no semi-diameter values and is unchanged.
