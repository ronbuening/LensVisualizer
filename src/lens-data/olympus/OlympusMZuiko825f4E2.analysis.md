## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** [WO 2022/137385 A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2022137385A1/en)  
**Application Number:** PCT/JP2020/048155  
**Filed:** 23 December 2020  
**Published:** 30 June 2022  
**Inventors:** Yuki Zen; Koji Nakagawa; Yasuharu Yamada  
**Applicant:** Olympus Corporation  
**Title:** Zoom lens and image-capturing device provided with same  
**Example details:** Numerical Example 2 (数値実施例2), surface table, aspheric data and zoom data on printed pp. 16–19; lens sections in Fig. 3; aberration curves in Fig. 6. Millimetres, used without scaling.

The patent describes a wide-angle zoom with a negative first group, a weak positive second group that carries the aperture stop, and a positive rear group. It gives three numerical examples. Example 2 has a focal length of 8.16–24.51 mm, f/4.08 at every zoom position, a full field of 112.09° to 46.44° and an image height of 10.815 mm, which is the Four Thirds half-diagonal. [1]

The example matches the M.Zuiko Digital ED 8-25mm F4.0 PRO on these points:

1. **Maker and date.** The applicant is Olympus, and the application was filed about six months before the lens was announced on 9 June 2021. [1, 4]
2. **Focal length, aperture and format.** The lens is sold as 8–25 mm with a constant f/4 for Micro Four Thirds; the example is 8.16–24.51 mm at f/4.08 on a 21.63 mm image circle. [1, 2]
3. **Construction.** The maker states 16 elements in 10 groups. The example has 16 elements in 10 groups. [1, 2]
4. **Special elements, position by position.** The maker's construction diagram colours a dual-sided aspherical second element, a Super ED third element, HR fourth and tenth elements, aspherical ED eleventh and fourteenth elements, an HD twelfth element, a Super HR fifteenth element and an ED last element. The example has aspheric surfaces on both sides of the second element, on the rear of the eleventh and on both sides of the fourteenth, and its index and Abbe values fall in the matching class at each of those positions. [1, 3]
5. **Cemented pairs and stop.** The diagram and Fig. 3 show the same six cemented pairs and the stop directly behind the second group. [1, 3]

The link to the product is strong, but it does not single out one example. All three numerical examples share this layout and differ only slightly in their radii and spacings, so Example 2 is one of three equally good candidates, and no factory prescription is available to say which, if any, is the production formula.

## Optical Architecture

The lens is a five-group zoom with a negative-positive-positive-negative-positive power arrangement. The patent prints the group focal lengths as f1 = −15.15, f2 = 270.01, f3 = 27.41, f4 = −40.81 and f5 = 41.62 mm. [1]

- **G1 (negative):** L11, L12 and the cemented pair L13–L14. This strongly negative front group gives the wide field and the long back focus relative to the 8 mm focal length.
- **G2 (weakly positive):** the cemented pair L21–L22, with the aperture stop 1.20 mm behind it. The stop moves with this group.
- **G3 (positive):** the cemented pair L31–L32, the single element L33 and the cemented pair L34–L35. This is the main positive group and does most of the zooming.
- **G4 (negative):** the cemented pair L41–L42, the focusing group.
- **G5 (positive):** L51 and the cemented pair L52–L53, which stays fixed relative to the image.

From wide to tele the first group moves toward the image and then back toward the object, while G2 and G3 move toward the object. In the printed positions the total length is 131.41 mm at wide, 122.63 mm in the middle and 124.79 mm at tele, so the lens is longest at 8 mm. The gap between G1 and G2 closes from 24.10 to 1.60 mm, and the gap between G3 and G4 opens from 2.85 to 26.52 mm. [1]

The patent's condition (1) asks for the ratio of the second group's focal length to the wide-end focal length to lie between 15 and 50; Example 2 gives 33.08, and the model reproduces it (269.98 / 8.1617). [1]

## Element-by-Element Analysis

Focal lengths are thick-lens values in air computed from the data file at the d-line. Index and Abbe values are as printed.

### G1: L11 to L14

- **L11** — negative meniscus, convex to the object. nd 1.87070, νd 40.73, f = −34.9 mm. The front element; the patent prints its effective diameter as 39.92 mm.
- **L12** — negative meniscus, convex to the object, aspheric on both surfaces. nd 1.58253, νd 59.32, f = −33.0 mm. This is the element the maker labels DSA.
- **L13** — biconcave. nd 1.43875, νd 94.66, f = −31.4 mm. The Super ED element, cemented to L14.
- **L14** — positive meniscus, convex to the object. nd 1.95375, νd 32.32, f = +31.8 mm. The only positive element in the group. The cemented pair L13–L14 has almost no net power (f ≈ −1635 mm); it is there for colour correction.

### G2: L21 and L22

- **L21** — biconvex. nd 1.65412, νd 39.68, f = +23.5 mm.
- **L22** — negative meniscus, convex to the image. nd 1.87070, νd 40.73, f = −25.5 mm. The cemented pair has f = +270.0 mm, against the patent's 270.01 mm.

### G3: L31 to L35

- **L31** — biconvex. nd 1.51633, νd 64.14, f = +21.5 mm.
- **L32** — negative meniscus, convex to the image. nd 1.87070, νd 40.73, f = −18.1 mm. Cemented to L31; the pair is weakly negative (f ≈ −158 mm).
- **L33** — symmetric biconvex (radii ±46.749 mm). nd 1.85150, νd 40.78, f = +28.0 mm.
- **L34** — negative meniscus, convex to the object. nd 1.91082, νd 35.25, f = −25.8 mm.
- **L35** — biconvex, aspheric on the image side. nd 1.49650, νd 81.53, f = +20.7 mm. Cemented to L34; the pair has f = +79.0 mm.

### G4: L41 and L42

- **L41** — biconvex. nd 1.94595, νd 17.98, f = +43.9 mm. The high-dispersion element.
- **L42** — biconcave. nd 1.72916, νd 54.09, f = −20.8 mm. The cemented pair has f = −40.8 mm, the patent's f4.

### G5: L51 to L53

- **L51** — biconvex, aspheric on both surfaces. nd 1.49650, νd 81.53, f = +25.6 mm.
- **L52** — negative meniscus with a nearly flat front (radius 600 mm). nd 2.00100, νd 29.13, f = −19.9 mm.
- **L53** — biconvex. nd 1.49700, νd 81.54, f = +31.2 mm. Cemented to L52; the pair has f = −59.3 mm.

## Glass Identification

The patent prints only nd and νd and names no glass or maker. The data file carries those printed values and no glass names.

Twelve of the sixteen elements have printed values that coincide with a current catalogue glass at the d-line: Ohara S-FPL55 (L13), S-NBH5 (L21), S-BSL7 (L31), S-LAH89 (L33) and S-FPL51 (L53); and Hoya TAFD32 (L11, L22, L32), TAFD45 (L14), TAFD35 (L34), FDS18 (L41) and TAFD55 (L52). These are numerical matches, not statements by the maker. L12, L35, L42 and L51 were not matched to a catalogue glass.

All index values are for the helium d-line (587.56 nm), as the patent states.

## Focus Mechanism

The patent says that the fourth group moves toward the image when focusing from infinity to a near object. It tabulates no close-focus spacings, so the model is at infinity focus only. The maker gives a minimum focus distance of 0.23 m across the zoom range; that figure is recorded in the catalogue data and is not modelled. [1, 2]

## Aspherical Surfaces

Five surfaces are aspheric: both sides of L12 (surfaces 3 and 4), the image side of L35 (surface 19) and both sides of L51 (surfaces 23 and 24). The patent's sag equation uses the term (1 + k), so its k is the conic constant K used in the data file.

| Surface | Element  | K      | Terms   |
| ------- | -------- | ------ | ------- |
| 3       | L12 front | −0.764 | A4–A12  |
| 4       | L12 rear  | −2.000 | A4–A12  |
| 19      | L35 rear  | 0      | A4–A10  |
| 23      | L51 front | 0      | A4–A10  |
| 24      | L51 rear  | 0      | A4–A10  |

Surface 4 is a deep concave surface (radius 9.332 mm) with a hyperbolic conic. The traced full field at the wide end, 112.2°, agrees with the printed 112.09°, which checks the conic and coefficient convention on the two front aspheres, since they control the wide-end distortion.

## Zoom Stations: Printed and Calculated

The patent prints three zoom positions. The data file holds them exactly, at stations 0, 64 and 128 of 129.

| Position | Focal length (patent / model) | Back focus (patent / paraxial) | Full field (patent / traced) |
| -------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Wide     | 8.16 / 8.162 mm               | 14.88 / 14.888 mm              | 112.09° / 112.2°             |
| Middle   | 14.14 / 14.143 mm             | 14.90 / 14.902 mm              | 76.17° / 76.2°               |
| Tele     | 24.51 / 24.513 mm             | 14.90 / 14.897 mm              | 46.44° / 46.4°               |

The 126 stations in between are calculated, not printed. The gaps ahead of G2, behind the stop and behind G5 are interpolated in straight lines between the printed positions, and the focusing group G4 is shifted to hold paraxial focus. Across all 129 stations the paraxial focus error stays between −0.008 and +0.003 mm and the focal length rises steadily.

This calculated path is not the real zoom cam. Because the other groups are interpolated in straight lines, G4 has to overshoot by 2.57 mm between the middle and tele positions: the gap behind it peaks at 12.13 mm, against printed values of 9.72 and 9.44 mm.

The printed gaps add up to 131.40, 122.62 and 124.79 mm, while the patent's totals are 131.41, 122.63 and 124.79 mm. The 0.01 mm differences are rounding: the totals use the unrounded back focus.

## Stop and Clear Apertures

The stop position is printed (surface 11). Its semi-diameter is not; the model sets it at each station from the printed f/4.08, so it opens from 3.72 mm at wide to 6.78 mm at tele.

The patent prints one clear aperture, the 39.92 mm effective diameter of the front element, and the data file uses it. Every other semi-diameter is inferred. With these rims the on-axis beam is clear at every station and the chief ray reaches the 10.815 mm image corner at every station.

At the wide end the inferred rims cut the corner beam heavily: geometric transmission is 100 % out to 44°, 76 % at 53° and 42 % at the 56.1° corner. The loss comes mainly from the inferred rims of G4 and of the rear surface of L12, not from the prescription. At the middle and tele positions transmission stays at 98 % or better across the field.

## Verification Summary

- Every surface, aspheric coefficient and printed variable gap of Example 2 was compared with the patent page images; no difference was found.
- The model reproduces the printed focal lengths, back focus, total length and field angles at the three printed positions, the printed f2 and f4, and the three conditional values (33.08, 2.26, 2.76).
- Longitudinal spherical aberration at full aperture is at most 0.05 mm at the stations checked.
- Not covered: close focus, the real zoom cam, and the true clear apertures.
