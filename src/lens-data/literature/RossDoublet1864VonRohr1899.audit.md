# Ross Doublet f/19.4 (von Rohr 1899, Fig. 76) — audit log

## 2026-11-18 — Literature reconstruction audit

### Pages and figure used

Von Rohr (1899), printed pp. 176–178: the table, its notes and Fig. 76 on p. 177, the text on Ross's doublet on
p. 177, Boyle's ratio lens on pp. 176–177 and Dallmeyer's landscape lens on p. 178. These pages lie outside the
pp. 245–398 chapter PDF and were read from a three-page extract of the scan (native 160 ppi). Further sections of the
same e-rara record: the Litteraturverzeichniss pp. 423–424 (A. Ross, Th. Ross, Ross & Co.) and 426 (H. Schroeder)
in PDF 4637806, pages 26, 27 and 29; the Bemerkungen zu den Figurentafeln (PDF 4637812, page 2), which state that
the plate drawings are at natural size for f = 100, give the hatching legend and the curve scales, and say the plates
give the extreme h and ω more exactly than the text; and Tafel III (PDF 4637814, page 4, native 162 ppi), which
redraws the lens as No. 76 with curves 76a and 76b. Every value was read on page renders at 400–800 dpi. The page
prints no ν̄, so the glass-table definition on p. 339 is not needed.

### Signs and their basis

Signs follow Fig. 76 and Tafel III. The front cell is a meniscus convex toward the object: r1 and r3 have their
centres behind the vertices, and the cemented face r2 bows toward the image in both drawings. On Tafel III at
600 dpi the r2 line lies 8.5 px (0.36 mm) further toward the image on the axis than at the rim; |r2| = 241 gives
0.34 mm at the drawn rim, and the sag of r1 (2.36 mm drawn, 2.41 computed) confirms the scale. The rear cell is a
meniscus concave toward the object, all three centres in front of the vertices; r5 runs into the corner where r4
meets the rim line, as the printed radii require (L3 reaches a knife edge at 12.95 mm). With r2 = +241 the EFL would
be 96.25 mm; with −241 it is 99.94 mm. The reproduction of curve 76b below, which depends on the signs and on the
stop position, also confirms them.

Scale: Tafel III at 600 dpi gives 652 px for the 27.4 mm vertex length (23.80 px/mm, nominal 23.62 for natural
size); Fig. 76 gives 644 px (23.50 px/mm). Drawn spacings, Tafel III / Fig. 76 against the print: d1 3.59 / 3.57
(3.6), d2 1.53 / 1.53 (1.4), front cell to stop 5.69 / 5.68 (5.9), stop to rear cell 10.25 / 10.32 (10.1), d3 4.81 /
4.74 (4.8), d4 1.51 / 1.55 (1.6).

Hatching, checked at 1200 dpi in both drawings: L1 and L3 descending to the right (the plate legend's "altes
(niedrig brechendes) Crownglas"), L2 and L4 rising to the right (the legend's "Flintglas").

### Rim decisions

Exact meridional trace at the printed ω = 36° and f/19.4 with the staged 10 mm rims: no on-axis clip, but the chief
ray crosses r6 at 10.689 mm and is blocked there from 33.9°. The engine's chief-ray solve converges 0.213 mm from
the stop centre and crosses r6 at 10.843 mm; it reports the 36° chief ray clipped with 10.7 and 10.8 mm and clear
with 10.9 mm, and with 10 mm rims it clips from 34°. The package's claim that 0–36° passed without clipping is
therefore wrong. Only r6 blocks. Both drawings show the rear cell with one flat rim line across its three faces, so
r4, r5 and r6 were raised together to 10.9 mm; the front cell, which the chief ray crosses at 7.09 mm at most,
keeps 10 mm. After the change the trace at 36° shows no axial clip and no blocked chief ray. L3 keeps a 1.884 mm
edge and L4 3.737 mm at 10.9 mm; L1 and L2 keep 1.967 and 2.413 mm at 10 mm.

### Patent check (D6)

Von Rohr names no patent for the doublet. Page 177 cites only Schroeder's book for the data. The bibliography's
Th. Ross entries (pp. 423–424) list six publications; the only patent is E.P. 174 of 23 January 1861, on achromatic
objectives made of crown glass alone or flint glass alone, indexed at p. 211, three years before this design. The
index entry for the doublet ("Umarbeitung der Collen lens in sein Weitwinkeldoublet. 177. 193.") carries no patent.
Result: **no patent named**, so no table to compare and no patent office searched. The file was not converted to a
patent lens.

### Open limitations

- All thicknesses and distances were taken from a drawing and both indices estimated from specific gravity, by von
  Rohr's own note; the model reproduces his computation, not measured maker data.
- The model keeps the rounded book table; its EFL is 0.06 % short.
- The front cell keeps the staged 10 mm envelope and the rear cell 10.9 mm, both well inside the drawn 12.9 mm. The
  drawn lens would pass the whole beam at 36°; the model passes 56 % of the meridional stop diameter there.
- Curve 76b's meridional end point at 36° reads +0.47 mm against the model's +0.65 mm, where the dashed curve is
  steep; the other points agree within 0.05 mm.
- `url` left out. The package names e-rara PDF 4637858, page 36, for p. 177 (and page 37 for p. 178 in the Dallmeyer
  file); e-rara answered with a browser-verification page, so the anchor is unconfirmed.
- nD only; von Rohr prints no dispersion, so no chromatic behaviour is modelled.
- `maker` stays null: von Rohr credits Th. Ross, who is not a catalog maker.
