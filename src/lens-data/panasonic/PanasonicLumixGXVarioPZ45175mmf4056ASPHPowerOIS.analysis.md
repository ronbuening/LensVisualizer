**Inventors:** Isamu Izuhara; Kyoichi Miyazaki
**Filed:** 2012-08-10 (PCT); priority 2011-08-25
**Published:** 2015-01-07 (Japanese grant publication; registered 2014-11-21)

Fourteen glass bodies in ten components form six powered units. G1, G3 and G6 remain fixed to source rounding; G2, G4 and G5 change position. The modeled moving compensator is G4. Front plano-convex L1 and rear meniscus forms discriminate Example1 from Examples2/3. Official cyan low-dispersion locations agree with L3/L9; aspheric-element locations agree with L7/L10.

The correlation is probable and discriminated by geometry, not proof of the manufactured prescription. Marketed endpoints are not substituted for native patent values.

## Optical Architecture

The model retains 25 numbered surfaces and 14 material entries. The numbered aperture stop is S13; its adjacent source distances are retained. Source physical count is 14 elements in 10 components. Resin replication layers are distinct media, not extra marketed lens elements.

The continuous zoom is a constrained reconstruction through 201 stations. Only stations 0, 100 and 200 are published. Native radii, glass coordinates and distances are unchanged; intermediate movement is numerical, not a recovered factory cam. The aperture is calibrated to the source f-number at each zoom station; this is not independent evidence for physical stop diameter.

### Explicit reconstruction assumptions

- All physical semi-diameters and iris radii inferred; printed F-number calibrates entrance pupil, not independently measured aperture.
- Continuous zoom is a constrained engineering cam: linear native gap drivers and rear plane; G4 shift compensates first-order focus while retaining native residual. No published intermediate cam.
- No finite focus, decenter, OIS stroke or marketed 0.9 m performance is reconstructed.
