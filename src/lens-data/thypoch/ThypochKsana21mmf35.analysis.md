# THYPOCH KSANA 21mm f/3.5 ASPH.

## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** CN 118534627 A  
**Application Number:** 202410641681.3  
**Filed:** 2024-05-22  
**Published:** 2024-08-23  
**Inventors:** Kuang Jian (邝健); Ouyang Xia (欧阳霞); Ye Bo (叶波); Li Zenghui (李增辉); Liu Xiaojuan (刘晓娟)  
**Applicant:** Shenzhen Dongzheng Optical Technology Co., Ltd. (深圳市东正光学技术股份有限公司)  
**Title:** 一种光学镜头及摄像头模组 (An optical lens and camera module)  
**Embodiment analyzed:** Example 1, Tables 1a–1g, Figures 1–7

The model transcribes the complete A-publication's Example 1 at unit scale.
The association with the Thypoch Ksana is construction-based. Neither the patent
nor the manufacturer confirms that these rounded numerical values are the
unchanged production prescription.

1. Both optical sections contain eight powered elements in six air-separated
   components, with cemented L3/L4 and L6/L7 pairs on opposite sides of the stop.
2. The first doublet has negative L3 followed by positive L4; the second has
   positive L6 followed by negative L7. This sequence distinguishes Example 1
   from Example 2 rather than merely matching focal length and element count.
3. The last lens is a rear-facing meniscus with both surfaces aspherical. The
   official optical image marks this L8 as aspherical, matching source S14/S15.
4. The maker marks L2/L4/L5 as high-index glass. Their native patent indices are
   2.00/1.88/2.00. Product ED marks on L1 and L6 are separate evidence; L6's
   patent 1.73/54.68 is retained rather than replaced by a presumed ED material.
5. Published 21.64 mm, f/3.50, 45.31° half-field and 43.20-mm image diameter are
   close to the marketed 21 mm, f/3.5, 90.6° diagonal field and 43.2-mm image circle.
   These numerical similarities support the construction match but do not prove it.

The official product is a manual-focus Leica M-mount full-frame lens, with nine
iris blades and an f/3.5–f/22 range. Its 0.5 m minimum focus is measured from the
sensor plane. Patent Table 1a defines D0 from the object to the first surface,
so its 0.5 m near state is a different reference. The production coating,
mechanical barrel, rangefinder coupling and exact material identities are not
encoded by the prescription.

## Optical Architecture

The architecture is a compact wide-angle system with a front negative meniscus,
two multi-element groups and a central stop. G1 contains L1–L4; G2 contains L5–L8.
Both groups are positive in isolation, although G1 is weak. The front negative
element alone does not justify calling the complete system retrofocus.

The literal rounded prescription gives EFL **21.726121 mm**.
Its standalone group focal lengths are **G1 +209.300668 mm** and
**G2 +29.517655 mm**. The physical first-vertex-to-source-image track
at infinity is **52.84 mm**. Paraxial BFD from the last lens
vertex, including the source camera plate, is **18.287263 mm**.
The source image is at 18.06 mm behind that vertex, leaving residual defocus.

Track/EFL = 2.432095 and physical BFD/EFL = 0.841718.
Thus the verified literal model satisfies neither the telephoto track criterion
nor BFD > EFL for retrofocus. These ratios refer to the infinity source state.
They are not mechanical barrel-length ratios.

The cemented D1 pair L3/L4 has standalone EFL **+269.650052 mm**;
D2 L6/L7 has standalone EFL **-71.286424 mm**. These are
thick-component powers with air on both sides. They must not be confused with
individual element powers or a sum of powers assigned to the in-situ shared interface.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

### L1 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.50, νd = 81.60. Glass: Unmatched (low-dispersion crown) (class only). f = -33.252411 mm.

The convex-to-object negative meniscus begins the angular transformation of the wide field. Its comparatively high Abbe number is a source fact; no particular low-dispersion catalog melt is established. The manufacturer marks the corresponding position ED. Assignment of a specific aberration contribution would require a verified aberration decomposition. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L2 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 2.00, νd = 25.43. Glass: Unmatched (high-index flint) (class only). f = +36.895130 mm.

This positive meniscus follows the first air gap and precedes the negative/positive cemented pair. Its high index provides positive power with the published moderate front curvature and weak rear surface. This description follows shape and power; the patent does not quantify an isolated coma or spherical-aberration contribution for L2. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L3 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.75, νd = 25.05. Glass: Unmatched (dense flint) (class only). f = -10.587384 mm.

L3 is the biconcave negative front member of D1. The glass beyond its rear interface is L4 rather than air. The quoted standalone focal length imagines air on both sides and therefore is not the refracting power of the actual cemented interface. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L4 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.88, νd = 39.22. Glass: Unmatched (lanthanum flint) (class only). f = +11.236295 mm.

L4 is the biconvex positive rear member of D1. Its higher Abbe number contrasts with L3, making chromatic balancing a plausible qualitative interpretation of the pair. The pair is only weakly positive in isolation; no measured secondary-spectrum performance is claimed. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L5 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 2.00, νd = 25.43. Glass: Unmatched (high-index flint) (class only). f = +24.536190 mm.

The biconvex positive singlet is the first powered element behind the central stop. It shares the same rounded native coordinates as L2, but equal printed coordinates do not prove identical manufactured melts. The official diagram marks both positions HRI. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L6 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.73, νd = 54.68. Glass: Unmatched (high-index crown) (class only). f = +13.970102 mm.

L6 is the biconvex positive front member of D2. The official product image colors this position ED, whereas the selected patent publishes only the native coordinates shown above. The model preserves those coordinates and makes no ED-material substitution. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L7 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.70, νd = 30.05. Glass: Unmatched (dense flint) (class only). f = -11.250976 mm.

L7 is the negative rear member of D2. The positive/negative pair is net negative in isolation. Its lower Abbe number relative to L6 is consistent with a chromatically balancing pairing, but the available source numbers do not establish partial dispersion or an APO result. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

### L8 — Positive Meniscus (2× Asph)

nd = 1.59, νd = 61.25. Glass: Unmatched (crown) (class only). f = +179.280914 mm.

Both surfaces of this rear-facing meniscus are aspherical. The positive standalone power follows the unchanged Table 1a radii and index. Table 1f nevertheless prints a negative L8 power sign; this contradiction is retained rather than repaired by changing a radius, glass or coefficient. Figure 1 and the official product section support the meniscus form. (Table 1a, PDF p8; group membership ¶0084–0085.)

## Glass Identification and Selection

The source labels its coordinates Nd/Vd and supplies no individual nC, nF, ng
or partial-dispersion measurements. All seven distinct powered-lens coordinates
and the separate camera-plate coordinate were screened against authoritative
HOYA, OHARA, SCHOTT, HIKARI, SUMITA and CDGM catalogs.

The following names are compatibility examples under a nearest-two-decimal
rounding assumption, not chosen materials. The patent does not specify its rounding
method; none of these rows is substituted into the native prescription.

| Position | Native nd / νd | Examples compatible with both rounding cells     |
| -------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| L1       | 1.50 / 81.60   | HIKARI Q-FK01AS                                  |
| L2, L5   | 2.00 / 25.43   | CDGM H-ZLaF90                                    |
| L3       | 1.75 / 25.05   | HOYA FF8                                         |
| L4       | 1.88 / 39.22   | CDGM H-ZLaF68N                                   |
| L6       | 1.73 / 54.68   | OHARA LAL18 and S-LAL18; CDGM H-LaK52            |
| L7       | 1.70 / 30.05   | HOYA FD15/E-FD15/E-FD15L; CDGM H-ZF11            |
| L8       | 1.59 / 61.25   | HOYA BACD5 molding variants; CDGM H-ZK3 variants |
| CG       | 1.52 / 64.20   | HOYA BSC7; CDGM H-K9L variants                   |

OHARA LAL18 and S-LAL18 remain distinct catalog names. Broader index/Abbe
compatibility is not equivalent to matching dispersion curves, source precision,
supplier, melt or production materials. Runtime coordinate tolerances are also
different from source rounding cells. All authored element annotations remain
Unmatched classes, deliberately leaving identity unresolved despite the available
candidates. The plate carries its native nd/νd without an invented catalog name.

The model's wavelength behavior uses the runtime's nd/νd fallback. It therefore
cannot independently reproduce the patent's chromatic plots or substantiate
anomalous-dispersion, apochromatic or secondary-spectrum claims.

## Focus Mechanism

Example 1 explicitly links G1, G2 and STOP (¶0086). All eight lenses and the
diaphragm unit translate together toward the object as focus moves closer; the
camera-side protective plate and sensor remain fixed. No floating group is added.

| Source state | Object-to-first-vertex D0 | Physical S15-to-CG gap D15 | First vertex to source image |
| ------------ | ------------------------: | -------------------------: | ---------------------------: |
| Infinity     |                  Infinity |                   16.71 mm |                     52.84 mm |
| Near         |                    500 mm |                   17.67 mm |                     53.80 mm |

The published unit-focus travel is 0.96 mm. Table 1a then adds 0.85 mm of protective
glass and 0.50 mm of air before IMAGE. The model retains these through rearPlates,
not an air-equivalent replacement. Paragraph ¶0091's loose D15-to-IMAGE wording
and stray D11 reference do not override the explicit sequential tables.

The near source state is certified as 500 mm from the first surface. Its
object-to-image distance is 553.80 mm, used for the model's 0.5538 m focus label.
The official product's 500-mm sensor-plane MFD is not silently substituted.
Intermediate slider states linearly interpolate the published gap endpoints;
the patent supplies no mechanical cam law for these intermediate positions.

The coarse literal prescription leaves the source image 0.227263 mm before
paraxial infinity focus and 0.219432 mm before paraxial focus at the source near
distance. The near image plane's exact paraxial conjugate is instead 648.457648 mm
from the first vertex. That computed diagnostic does not replace the source label.
The source-certified finite ray calculation uses 500 mm; generic UI focus rays
use their own approximate launch convention and are not identical to it.

## Aspherical Surfaces

L8 carries source S14/S15, authored as 14A/15A. Paragraph ¶0096 defines

z(h) = c h² / [1 + √(1 − (1 + k)c²h²)] + Σ Aₚ hᵖ, with c = 1/R.

Thus model K equals source k without an offset. Radii and radial coordinates
are in millimetres; Aₚ has units mm^(1−p). The scale factor is one. The full
published even-order schedule is retained, including zero A14 and A16.

| Coefficient |       14A |       15A |
| ----------- | --------: | --------: |
| K           |     −3.99 |     +7.19 |
| A4          |  7.26E−05 |  2.54E−04 |
| A6          | −8.31E−07 | −1.36E−07 |
| A8          |  2.76E−08 |  2.98E−08 |
| A10         | −1.31E−09 | −8.18E−10 |
| A12         |  1.13E−11 |  6.44E−12 |
| A14         |         0 |         0 |
| A16         |         0 |         0 |

At its inferred 7.7-mm semi-diameter, 14A has sag -0.867752 mm, departure -0.006210 mm from the same-radius sphere, and actual rim-slope angle 18.570996°.

At its inferred 7.7-mm semi-diameter, 15A has sag -0.498517 mm, departure +0.632343 mm from the same-radius sphere, and actual rim-slope angle 1.374821°.

These are model-aperture results, not published clear apertures. The higher-order
terms and conic are evaluated together; the sign of A4 alone is insufficient to
describe the entire correction profile. The rear conic's mathematical radial
limit is 9.357689 mm, with the current 98% policy cap at 9.170536 mm. Both stored
SDs are 7.7 mm. No polynomial refit or sign correction is applied. The sources do
not establish the manufacturing method of the aspheric element.

## Conditional Expressions and Verification Limits

| Condition | Literal computation | Printed value | Source range |
| --------- | ------------------: | ------------: | ------------ |
| TL1/TL2   |            1.586026 |         1.586 | 0.95–1.65    |
| TTL/f     |            2.432095 |         2.442 | 2.26–2.68    |
| fG2/f     |            1.358625 |         1.361 | 0.87–1.89    |
| H/BFL     |            1.181150 |         1.196 | 1.05–1.47    |

All four inequality ranges pass. Differences from rounded printed summary values
are preserved; a successful range test is not a claim that the printed last digit
has been reproduced. The computed surface-by-surface Petzval sum is
**0.004273091181 mm⁻¹**, using φ/(n n′) at each refracting interface.
This first-order sum is not a reproduction of the patent's tangential/sagittal
field-curvature plots.

The iris radius is inferred by exact native entrance-pupil calibration to f/3.50:
**3.873496827440 mm**. Clear SDs are inferred from Figure 1 and ray/geometry checks.
They are not a published aperture schedule. Native build, schema and production
render diagnostics pass at five focus samples, with zero hidden trim. Current
default slope, conic and 90% shared-gap intrusion limits are retained.

At infinity, a 44.368761° chief reaches the modeled 21.6-mm image radius. At the
source 500-mm near configuration, the corresponding chief angle is 44.049060°.
The source 45.31° chief also transmits at both endpoints, reaching 22.396276 mm
and 22.674066 mm respectively. The difference from the printed field/height pair
is disclosed and is not corrected by changing source geometry or materials.

The native audit includes 3,075 generic-state rays and a separate 976-ray check
from the exact finite source plane. No failed ray or sampled inside-glass side
violation remains. Ordinary aperture-clipped marginal rays are present; these
checks do not establish an unvignetted full pupil or radiometric performance.
Finite sampling also does not prove clearance over a continuum. The unit-focus
interpolation and generic UI launch assumptions remain separate from the exact
published near configuration.
