The original SongRaw Moonlit AF 50mm f/1.2 artwork reproduced by [Phillip Reeve](https://phillipreeve.net/blog/review-songraw-50mm-1-2-moonlit/) and [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/04/07/new-company-songraw-unveils-first-lens-full-frame-af-50mm-f-1-2-moonlit/) matches the 15 elements, ten groups, five cemented pairs and two aspheric elements, L1 and L15. Ordered shapes support the association; exact production radii, coefficients, back focus and clear apertures remain unknown. Dimensions are in millimetres at scale factor 1; asphere coefficients are also unscaled.

The numerical prescription follows the later U tables, with one correction to the image gap explained below.

## Optical Architecture

The design has 15 elements in ten air-separated groups. Five cemented pairs C1–C5 are L1/L2, L3/L4, L8/L9, L10/L11 and L13/L14, respectively. Functional groups G1 (L1–L6) and G3 (L10–L15) are fixed; G2 (L7–L9) alone moves for focusing (¶0046–0053). The iris remains at the source's S11 position, between G1 and G2. The tabulated prescription gives positive isolated powers for G1 and G3 despite the patent's negative-power description.

## Glass Identification

The published d-line indices and Abbe numbers retain their coarse precision of two and one decimals. Each glass is labeled Unmatched with its printed nd/νd; no factory melt or catalog glass is identified. The lower-dispersion L2, L4, L9 and L11 contrast with their more dispersive cemented partners, consistent with the patent's chromatic-correction explanation. Abbe-based spectral estimates cannot establish anomalous partial dispersion or measured colour correction. Computed EFL is 49.903117 mm, compared with the stated 49 mm and marketed 50 mm.

## Focus Mechanism and Image Plane

Only infinity is modeled: the U supplies no close-focus station. G2 moves internally; G1, the stop and G3 stay fixed. G2 is at its image-side travel boundary at infinity (¶0053), so nearer focus requires objectward motion. The production minimum focus distance, 0.52 m per [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/04/07/new-company-songraw-unveils-first-lens-full-frame-af-50mm-f-1-2-moonlit/), supplies no numerical close-focus spacings.

Table 1 prints S26-to-image spacing as 14.935 mm, repeated as about 14.9 mm in ¶0047. The model corrects this gap to the U prescription's calculated infinity back focus of 17.91466693210846 mm. No other prescription spacing, radius, index or coefficient changes. The illustrated filter FIT has no published thickness or material and is omitted. Mechanical flange distance does not independently establish optical back focus.

## Aspherical Surfaces

The U table supplies even-asphere coefficients through A12 for S1 on L1 and S25/S26 on L15, all with K = 0. The standard conic-plus-even-polynomial equation's square-root term contains 1 − (1 + K)Y²/R². Every U coefficient retains its printed precision; the earlier A coefficients differ. The tables do not establish manufacturing method.

## Aperture and Geometry

Nominal f/1.25 is the patent's MTF condition within its stated f/1.2–1.3 range. The iris radius is derived from that design aperture. The production lens stops down to f/16, as documented in the [Phillip Reeve review](https://phillipreeve.net/blog/review-songraw-50mm-1-2-moonlit/). Clear semi-diameters are inferred from Fig. 1/2 and ray clearance, rather than published dimensions. S3/S4 use 19.05/19.08 mm to clear the axial beam. S13/S14 use equal 19.78 mm rims: their combined sag is 5.37795 mm across a 5.492 mm gap, just below the 98% intrusion limit. The surfaces would meet near 20 mm radius, short of the f/1.25 marginal ray heights of 20.26/20.30 mm. Their remaining clipping follows from the source geometry.

Fig. 1's fixed front and rear groups establish a scale of approximately 15.52 native pixels/mm. Its curved faces end short of flat element edges at S3, S4, S13 and S24. Opaque lands extend those faces to 20.00, 22.00, 20.86 and 20.50 mm respectively, preserving the optical caps and their ray clipping. The S13 land matches L7's front edge, whose radius must clear the axial beam. Fig. 1 draws G2 farther toward the object than the tabulated infinity position shown in Fig. 2; its element outlines supply rim proportions, rather than a different focus station.

Geometric transmission is about 95.2% on-axis, 67.8% at half-field and 32.8% at the edge. The remaining axial loss is about 4.8%, equivalent in pupil area to approximately f/1.28. These are geometric model results, excluding reflection, absorption and diffraction, rather than measured illumination. The 23.790486° half-field is inferred from atan(22/EFL) and the source's upper 44 mm image diameter. Exact production apertures and the patent's published polychromatic MTF are not established by this model.

## Comparison with the Priority Application

CN 119148346 A also prints about 14.9 mm to the image, but uses a different infinity layout: S10-to-stop is 6.0 mm, stop-to-S17 is 31.1 mm, and G2 is nearest the object with S10-to-S12 at 7.35 mm. Its coarser table calculates to about 48.85 mm EFL and 15.6 mm back focus; substituting only the U's L3 index gives about 14.8 mm back focus. The printed image gap is therefore consistent with the earlier layout, whereas the U's 15.594 mm stop-to-G2 gap gives 17.915 mm back focus. The A's stated focus direction is opposite to the physically consistent U arrangement.

The A also gives L3 index 1.78 rather than 1.77, shares front and rear asphere terms that the U separates, and differs in S25 A10 and S26 A4. These differences are not combined into the U model. The corrected image gap is calculated from the later layout; neither publication prints 17.91466693210846 mm.
