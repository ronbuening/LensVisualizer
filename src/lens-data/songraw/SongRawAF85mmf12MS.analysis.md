## Patent Reference and Design Identification

**Patent:** CN 119667909 A\
**Filed:** 2025-01-23\
**Published:** 2025-03-21\
**Inventor:** Yuwen Chang (昌宇文)\
**Assignee:** Shenzhen Songruo Photography Equipment Co., Ltd.\
**Title:** 一种定焦镜头 (*A fixed-focal-length lens*)\
**Example details:** Table 1 / Fig. 2a on PDF page 11; Table 2, the even-asphere equation and Fig. 2b on page 12; Fig. 1 on page 10.

This model transcribes Example 1 (实施例一) and is associated with SongRaw's AF 85mm f/1.2 product family. The association does not establish an exact retail prescription. Numerical agreement with CN 223827884 U has been reported but has not been independently confirmed.

The product association rests on several construction similarities:

1. The maker and patent describe 18 elements in ten air-separated groups.
2. The maker lists one aspherical element, five ED elements and eleven high-index elements. The [exploded construction diagram](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0628/6943/8707/files/SONGRAW-MS-85MM-F1-2-LENS-F1.2-ELEMENTS.jpg?v=1790344124) supports the broad shapes and final aspherical element, but does not establish exact cementing, stop placement or glass identities.
3. The nominal 85 mm focal length and f/1.2 product aperture are consistent with the patent's stated f/1.2–1.3 design range. The prescription gives EFL 84.619374543 mm, rather than exactly 85 mm.

[Dustin Abbott's September 2026 review](https://dustinabbott.net/2026/09/songraw-af-85mm-f1-2-ms-review/) identifies the silver MS Nikon Z version and gives a 0.85 m minimum focus distance. The [maker's product page](https://songrawlens.com/products/songraw-af-851-2-ms-full-frame-autofocus-lens-golden-focal-lengthportraits-with-depth) offers Sony E and Nikon Z variants. These similarities support a family association; an exact match to the production prescription remains unproven.

## Optical Architecture

The design has eighteen glass elements in ten air-separated optical groups. The patent instead describes three functional groups with positive / negative / positive power: G1 = L1–L6, G2 = L7–L8 and G3 = L9–L18 (¶0027–¶0031). G1 is the fixed front group, G2 is the moving internal-focus doublet, and G3 is the fixed rear group. Moving only the cemented G2 pair reduces the moving lens count (¶0033).

The cemented assemblies are L3/L4, L5/L6, L7/L8, L9/L10/L11, L12/L13, L14/L15 and L16/L17. The iris at source surface S14 stays fixed; the data labels it `STO`. The source infinity gaps S10→S11 = 2.138 mm and S13→S14 = 21.473 mm are retained.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

The shapes and power signs follow the patent's element descriptions (¶0035–¶0052). Glass names below are spectral proxies, not supplier identifications. Individual element focal lengths are not tabulated. The patent attributes correction to the cemented assemblies collectively, without separate element aberration budgets (¶0058).

### L1 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.85, νd = 23.8. Glass: unmatched; supplier unknown.

L1 begins fixed positive G1 with a convex object face and concave image face.

### L2 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.59, νd = 68.3. Glass: H-ZPK5 (CDGM spectral proxy).

L2 is the second positive meniscus in G1. Its higher Abbe number distinguishes it from the more dispersive L1.

### L3/L4 — Cemented Meniscus Doublet

L3: nd = 1.59, νd = 68.3. Glass: H-ZPK5 (CDGM spectral proxy). L4: nd = 1.85, νd = 25.2. Glass: unmatched; supplier unknown.

Positive L3 and negative L4 share a positively curved cemented interface. Their contrasting Abbe numbers support the patent's stated use of cemented positive/negative pairs for chromatic correction.

### L5/L6 — Cemented Doublet

L5: nd = 1.59, νd = 68.3. Glass: H-ZPK5 (CDGM spectral proxy). L6: nd = 2.00, νd = 25.5. Glass: TAFD40 (HOYA spectral proxy).

Biconvex positive L5 and biconcave negative L6 complete G1. The rear face of the assembly is weakly concave.

### L7/L8 — Moving Cemented Focus Doublet

L7: nd = 1.92, νd = 20.9. Glass: E-FDS1 (HOYA spectral proxy). L8: nd = 1.70, νd = 55.5. Glass: unmatched; supplier unknown.

Positive meniscus L7 is cemented to biconcave negative L8. Together they form negative G2, the only group that moves during focusing.

### L9/L10/L11 — Cemented Triplet

L9: nd = 1.59, νd = 68.3. Glass: H-ZPK5 (CDGM spectral proxy). L10: nd = 1.77, νd = 29.7. Glass: NBFD29 (HOYA spectral proxy). L11: nd = 1.73, νd = 54.7. Glass: S-LAL18 (OHARA spectral proxy).

The triplet begins G3 behind the iris. Biconcave negative L10 lies between positive L9 and L11, combining contrasting dispersions within a single cemented assembly.

### L12/L13 — Cemented Doublet

L12: nd = 1.73, νd = 54.7. Glass: S-LAL18 (OHARA spectral proxy). L13: nd = 1.77, νd = 29.7. Glass: NBFD29 (HOYA spectral proxy).

Positive L12 and negative L13 continue the rear group's sequence of cemented positive/negative assemblies.

### L14/L15 — Cemented Doublet

L14: nd = 2.00, νd = 29.1. Glass: TAFD55 (HOYA spectral proxy). L15: nd = 1.62, νd = 36.3. Glass: E-F2 (HOYA spectral proxy).

Positive L14 and negative L15 form the next rear doublet. Their Abbe-number difference is smaller than that of L12/L13; no independent quantitative correction contribution is assigned to this pair.

### L16/L17 — Cemented Doublet

L16: nd = 2.00, νd = 25.5. Glass: TAFD40 (HOYA spectral proxy). L17: nd = 1.49, νd = 70.4. Glass: H-QK3L (CDGM spectral proxy).

Positive L16 and negative L17 form the final cemented pair, ahead of the separate aspherical element.

### L18 — Negative Meniscus, Two-Sided Asphere

nd = 1.81, νd = 40.7. Glass: L-LAH84 (OHARA spectral proxy).

L18 ends G3. Its surfaces S28 and S29 are the only aspherical surfaces in the prescription. The patent does not isolate their individual contributions to the final aberration balance.

The patent assigns one material to L2/L3/L5/L9 (¶0007) and attributes axial chromatic, spherical and distortion correction to the seven cemented assemblies (¶0058). The maker's diagram marks L2/L3/L5/L9/L17 as ED, consistent with their low-dispersion values and proxies. Their ED tags remain inferred; neither tags nor proxies establish production partial dispersion or apochromatic performance.

## Focus Mechanism and Image Plane

The patent describes internal focusing by moving G2 toward the image for nearer objects while G1, the iris and G3 remain fixed. A stepping motor is described (¶0028, ¶0033). No complete finite-focus station or numerical travel is supplied, so the model implements infinity only and supplies no invented focus law. The marketed 0.85 m minimum focus distance is product context, not a modeled endpoint.

The modeled final gap is 19.813299312 mm, placing the image at calculated paraxial focus without a filter. The printed S29→IMG distance of 20.300 mm is about 0.487 mm longer. The patent prints nd to only two decimals, allowing rounding of ±0.005; rounding alone can move the back focus of a comparable design by several millimetres. No unique misprint has been established, so the difference remains unresolved.

The calculated gap does not establish a source error or an exact filter conversion. Figure 1 draws a protective filter labeled FIT before the image, but gives no thickness or refractive-index prescription. The model omits that unspecified filter and retains every other numerical prescription value.

## Aspherical Surfaces

Only S28 and S29, on final L18, carry standard even-asphere terms through A12. The equation uses the standard conic constant K, with K = 0 denoting a spherical base (Fig. 2b; ¶0054). Coefficients are retained at the source's millimetre scale; the scale factor is 1 and no units conversion is applied.

| S28 | 0 | -9.74182e-6 | +1.27066e-8 | -9.6825e-12 | -8.09107e-15 | +5.01459e-17 |

## Aperture and Geometry

The source explicitly gives an entrance-pupil radius of 34.309 mm, rather than an iris radius. Combining it with calculated EFL gives f/1.233195; the patent states f/1.25 and permits f/1.2–1.3. The model retains the printed pupil radius and derives a paraxial iris radius of 18.474887 mm.

Clear apertures are inferred from Figure 1, which gives no aperture dimensions. The broad front and narrower rear follow its silhouette. S7 has a 24.17 mm optical cap and a flat land extending to the 31 mm outer rim of L3/L4; the land preserves the square outer edge without opening the aperture. S8 retains its 24.17 mm cap. S27/S28 retain 16.16 mm caps to avoid surface overlap. The figure draws the final pair almost meeting at larger heights, but continuing the prescribed curves to those heights would make them cross. These rims are not established factory clear apertures.

The model's 18.474887 mm iris clears the axial marginal rays. A real-ray calculation using the full 34.309 mm entrance pupil instead reaches a 19.072 mm iris radius and clips at S7 and S15–S20. That larger iris differs from the model's paraxially derived iris.

The model has substantial off-axis geometric vignetting, with about 29.31% of the pupil blocked at half-field and 68.97% at the edge, compared with no axial blockage. These figures describe geometric clipping, not measured illumination or coating transmission. The 14.573558° half-field is derived as atan(22/EFL) from the upper source image diameter of 44 mm; the patent does not publish a field angle.

## Model Scope and Limitations

Table 1 controls the numerical prescription. Its S10 radius is 568.322 mm, while the prose gives 568.332 mm. S2 = 215.365 mm and S28 = -119.605 mm agree between the table and prose.

Calculated transmitted monochromatic geometric RMS radii are 0.005745 mm axial, 0.009893 mm at half-field and 0.018087 mm at the edge. These are not diffraction MTF, measured performance or a reproduction of the source's 430–658 nm polychromatic charts.

Coarse glass precision, inferred apertures, the omitted unspecified filter, the unresolved back-focus difference and uncertain exact product identity limit comparisons with the production lens.
