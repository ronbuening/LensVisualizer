## Focus and Image Plane Interpretation

The modeled final gap remains 19.813299312 mm, placing the image at the literal prescription's lens-only paraxial focus. The patent prints nd to only two decimals; the SongRaw 50 validator showed that ±0.005 nd rounding alone moves a comparable design's back focus by several millimetres. The printed S29→IMG distance of 20.300 mm is 0.487 mm longer than this model's calculated focus. The independent validator's 2,570-case single-value scan found no unique misprint, so it supplies no justified prescription correction. Retaining the calculated final gap follows the YN 40 precedent of solving the last gap to paraxial focus. This is a qualified modeling interpretation, not proof of a source error or an exact no-filter conversion. The printed distance remains in source evidence and errata; no other numerical source value is changed.

FT is drawn but has no thickness/index prescription; no filter is invented. Only infinity is implemented. The source's G2 near-focus motion is described but supplies no complete finite station, so no focus law is fabricated.
