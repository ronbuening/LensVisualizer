## Optical Architecture

The source has 22 powered elements in 17 contiguous air-separated components. The zoom-group element counts are 7+1+4+1+9. Component counts are not the number of mechanically moving zoom groups. Cemented interfaces are source surfaces 10, 21, 29, 34, 37. The source stop is surface 25, renamed STO in the data file. No rear PP occurs in this embodiment. The adjacent Example 5 plate and sister US20180188510 prescription were not imported. The literal final air is 24.783 mm. TELE track differs by 0.001 mm from WIDE/MIDDLE; it is not normalized.

Every published zoom position is contained in this single model:

- WIDE: f=18.592 mm, F/2.750, full field=78.6 degrees; D13=1.726 mm, D15=1.184 mm, D22=17.241 mm, D24=25.173 mm
- MIDDLE: f=37.183 mm, F/2.750, full field=41.2 degrees; D13=10.015 mm, D15=17.312 mm, D22=4.375 mm, D24=13.622 mm
- TELE: f=55.217 mm, F/2.750, full field=28.4 degrees; D13=20.857 mm, D15=18.458 mm, D22=5.041 mm, D24=0.967 mm

The model has 33 authored zoom samples, of which only the source's 3 stations are marked published. Additional rows are an inferred paraxial cam: the source track and residual defocus are interpolated, target EFL follows the calculated source endpoints, and the remaining zoom gaps are solved. The slider interpolates these samples piecewise-linearly. This is not a recovered production zoom cam. Adaptive knot placement limits added sampled longitudinal error to 1.22 λd N², with λd=587.5618 nm and a 2% numerical margin, separately from literal source residuals. This engineering budget matches the corrected Minolta models: the added geometric defocus disk is at most half the d-line Airy-disk diameter. It is not a manufacturing tolerance or factory performance claim. Geometry and exact-ray behavior between samples remain explicit validation gates.

## Glass Identification

The patent supplies d-line indices and Abbe numbers but no catalog glass names, melt data or partial dispersion. All material labels therefore remain unmatched. C/F/g behavior uses the engine's Abbe-based approximation; the high-Abbe positional matches do not prove a particular fluorophosphate formulation, fluorite, APO behavior or factory chromatic performance.

## Focus Mechanism

Internal focus by G1b/L14 (surfaces 7–8), moving imageward toward reconstructed near focus at 0.85 m, and objectward toward infinity. D6 grows and D8 shrinks; D6+D8 and glass thickness stay fixed. Other groups, stop and image plane remain fixed at a given zoom setting. Patent publishes infinity zoom rows only. Numeric focus travel is a reconstruction, not a factory cam; macro is not modeled. The target normal-focus distances are product context; their numerical L14 spacings were solved from the literal prescription, not copied from a patent. The full L14 group translates rigidly and the adjacent-gap sum remains fixed. No inferred row is marked published and no finite conjugate is certified.

## Aperture and Rim Inference

The patent places the stop axially but expressly does not specify its size or shape. The model derives the iris from the source design F-number at each zoom position. The initial unvignetted paraxial field envelope exceeded physical source-glass clearance and was rejected. Lens clear semi-diameters are now inferred from schematic group proportions and native geometric limits, then subject to the native cap, edge, gap, iris, hidden-trim and exact-ray checks. These are not factory rim or barrel measurements. Nine blades are product context, not a detailed blade geometry. No gap-sag override is used.

## Spherical Prescription and Validation Status

No aspheric coefficients are published for this embodiment. Local bounded source, adaptive-slider, focus-direction, geometry, iris, exact-ray classification and native silhouette checks pass; the companion audit gives the tested scope. These checks do not establish converged throughput, MTF agreement, factory performance or release readiness. The file remains hidden pending the corrected package integration gates.
