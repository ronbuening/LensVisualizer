# Audit Log — FUJIFILM TCL-X100 33mm f/2 (Fujifilm X100)

Patent: US 2015/0226942 A1, Example 1 (Figure 1)

## 2026-07-24 — Patent-figure, glass, and metadata audit

The Figure 1 optical section was registered to the complete converter-plus-master prescription. Clear apertures are not tabulated by the patent, so the drawing was treated as a contour reference rather than dimensional evidence. The edits bring the large converter elements and the master L35 blank closer to the illustrated shapes while respecting the prescription's edge and gap limits.

| Surfaces | Before `sd` | After `sd` | Disposition |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| S1-S2 (L11) | 27.8 | 24.1 | Reduced to the measured Figure 1 rim |
| S3-S4 (L21) | 12.6 | 14.0 | Enlarged toward the illustrated converter rear group |
| S5-S6 (L22) | 11.8 | 13.0 | Enlarged to the largest validator-safe contour near the drawing |
| S7-S8 (L23) | 10.7 | 11.8 | Enlarged to the edge-thickness limit; the drawn blank is larger than the spherical prescription permits |
| S16-S17 (L35 shared/rear) | 7.2 | 8.5 / 9.1 | Enlarged to match the master-lens outline |

The S7-S8 drawing measurement cannot be copied literally: `sd = 12.0` produces negative L23 edge thickness. Apparent L31/L32 figure extents are also crossed by stop/leader lines and were not used. The final surface validator reports no errors, and the image-circle audit reports no undersized surfaces.

The display name was corrected from the mechanical phrase “Mounted on X100” to the product-style `FUJIFILM TCL-X100 33mm f/2 (Fujifilm X100)`.

### Glass disposition

The patent supplies `nd` and `νd`, but no vendor names or line-index tables. The existing catalog nevertheless resolves 11 of 12 elements to coefficient-backed coordinate matches. The data and analysis labels now expose those defensible assignments—S-FSL5, S-TIH53, S-LAL14, S-FTM16, S-LAH58, and S-NPH1—while continuing to treat them as catalog equivalents rather than production-supplier proof. L31 retains a cross-vendor S-TIH13/E-FD13 class label, and L36 remains unmatched.

No radius, thickness, index, asphere coefficient, focus state, stop size, element count, or group count was changed.
