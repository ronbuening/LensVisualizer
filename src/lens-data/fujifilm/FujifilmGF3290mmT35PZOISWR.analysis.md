## Optical Architecture

The five motion groups have positive, negative, positive, negative and positive net power (¶0159). They contain 3/5/9/3/5 elements respectively. Six cemented doublets leave 19 air-separated components; the camera-side plate is excluded from that count. During zoom, G2 and G4 move while G1, G3 and G5 remain fixed relative to the source image plane (¶0160). The four printed gap schedules preserve that plane. Between the three published stations, linearly interpolated gaps are an inferred motion hypothesis rather than a certified parfocal zoom cam. Design-plane charts retain the resulting intermediate defocus. The app’s separate bounded best-axial diagnostic does not replace the source plane or calibrate a factory cam; Patent positions mode selects the printed configurations. Source S23 is a real flat glass face and S36 is a real planar cemented interface; neither is an extra stop or dummy surface.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The palette retains every printed nd, νd and absolute θgF, including the rear plate. The material table defines d-line indices and θgF = (ng − nF)/(nF − nC) (¶0162–0163). Structured ΔPgF is θgF − (0.6438 − 0.001682νd), using the engine’s normal line. The carried arithmetic digits preserve reproducibility; θgF itself is printed to three decimal places. No separate measured C/F/g indices or complete manufacturer dispersion curves are fabricated. Nonreference spectral channels therefore remain qualified Abbe/partial-dispersion reconstructions. Reference calculations use the literal d-line indices at 587.5618 nm. The source thermal coefficients at 25°C are archived but no temperature model is claimed.

The very high-Abbe elements at source S21, S38 and S43 correlate with the three ED positions in the manufacturer section. That positional match does not establish an exact catalog glass, melt, supplier, coating or anomalous-dispersion certification. The table’s ED column means maximum effective diameter, not an ED-glass designation.

## Focus Mechanism and Zoom States

Example 1 identifies negative G4 as the focusing group, moving toward the image when focusing from infinity to close objects. The fourth through sixth elements of G3 form the stabilization group (¶0160), corresponding here to L12–L14 / source L34–L36. The model holds those elements centered and includes only the published infinity zoom states. No finite conjugate, continuous focus cam, motor calibration or stabilizer decenter law is certified. Canonical closeFocusM = 1e15 disables finite-focus operation.

## Stop and Clear-Aperture Assumptions

Source S17 explicitly marks the stop at z = 81.7251 mm. Table 34 row (36) prints φSw/φSt = 1.0000, so the axial beam at the stop has the same diameter at wide and tele: the patent describes a constant iris. Real-ray F-numbers at the printed stop radius of 12.3645 mm are 3.193/3.254/3.295, matching the printed 3.19/3.25/3.30, so the printed FNo drift does not need a changing iris. The engine’s native per-station open-FNo rule gives iris radii 12.2685/12.2047/12.1367 mm; these paraxial values sit below the 12.3645 mm real-ray radius. The iris plane stays at least 1.5 mm from the adjacent surfaces: that is the printed S17–S18 spacing, and the printed DD16 never falls below 2.0005 mm. Blade and barrel mechanics remain unproved. The f/22 endpoint is a modeled control range, not a sourced factory transmission-stop limit.

Lens face radii use the literal maximum effective diameter divided by two. Those source ray envelopes are not asserted to be manufactured clear rims, and no clearance inflation is added to improve throughput. The initial 90% gap guard rejects S8→S9 and S14→S15 even though their exact physical shared-band clearances are +0.9191697831 mm and +0.1476380496 mm. The tested gapSagFrac = 0.9134 is the smallest 1E−4-rounded margin fraction admitting those source caps. It changes no SD, optical radius, gap, glass, coefficient, stop or image-plane value. Five zoom geometry states pass with zero hidden trim.

Repo audits of this prescription place all three stations within 3% of the stated f-number, with no rim, trace or iris limit, and they cover the 27.39 mm image height at every station with no undersized image circle. A real-ray clearance check at that height shows no axial clipping and no blocked chief ray. Off-axis vignetting at the front-group rims is retained at its actual surfaces. These results are not measured illumination or factory vignetting.
