Production correspondence is probable; the patent does not establish the exact manufactured prescription.

## Optical Architecture

Eleven powered elements form nine air-separated components: three front singlets, a cemented doublet, the stop, a second doublet, a positive singlet, then three rear singlets. The positive first group contains L1–L8 and the stop and moves objectward for close focus; the negative rear group L9–L11 stays fixed relative to the sensor during focusing. The low-index, high-Abbe L4 and L8 and the two-sided rear asphere match Fujifilm's drawing. The sensor plate is represented by an air-equivalent spacing.

## Glass Identification and Selection

All powered-element nd, νd and θgF values are retained. θgF=(ng−nF)/(nF−nC) is a partial-dispersion ratio, distinct from anomalous ΔPgF. Three-term Cauchy curves reproduce nd, the F−C difference and the g−F ratio, yielding calculated nC/nF/ng values without identifying production glass or melts. L4 and L8 have inferred ED designations from Fujifilm's drawing and their compatible nd/νd values. The patent's ED column denotes effective diameter, not ED glass. Individual power signs do not uniquely establish aberration contributions.

## Focus Mechanism

The first eight lenses and stop move together 23.9818 mm objectward relative to the rear group and sensor. DD15 increases from 2.9000 mm at infinity to 26.8818 mm at the printed −0.5000× near state. The 101 focus positions use calculated inverse object-to-image distance spacing; the intervening motion is not a measured cam. With the physical plate train, the near state implies an object distance of 257.939149977679 mm before the first vertex and 0.426259949977679 m to the sensor. These are derived distances; Fujifilm specifies 0.43 m and 0.5×. Intermediate finite-focus MTF is unavailable.

### Tilt and Shift Movements

Shift spans ±15 mm and tilt ±10°, following Fujifilm's specifications; model controls step by 0.1 mm and 0.1°. The camera frame and 44 × 33 mm sensor stay fixed while the optics move. The tilt pivot is 109.9746 mm objectward of the image plane, using Example 18's infinity EFL and the principal-point guidance in ¶0276, Figure 42. This is a design reference, not a measured hinge position.

The plate at surfaces 22–23 is replaced by an air-equivalent rear spacing: 78.8866 + 3.2/1.51680 + 0.9874 = 81.9837 mm. Reference-wavelength paraxial focus, EFL and focus travel are preserved; the plate's spherochromatism is omitted. Physical infinity vertex-to-image length is 144.339 mm; model track is 143.249 mm.

## Model Scope and Limitations

Combined extreme shift and tilt require about 27.5° traced half-field, beyond the patent's 43.4° full design field (21.7° half-field), and vignette. Shift alone places the sensor corner at √(37² + 16.5²) ≈ 40.5 mm, within the approximately 43.8 mm patent image height; this does not guarantee full-pupil illumination. Off-axis marginal rays vignette at surface 1.

The printed stop diameter is 17.440 mm; the model derives its radius from design f/5.76. Printed effective diameters and gaps are retained. At the surface 19–20 gap, a rim allowance of 0.911 accommodates positive physical separation; 0.90 and 0.91 do not. The 4.419 µm infinity defocus and calculated near magnification −0.499875264 reflect prescription rounding. MTF convergence varies with field, spectrum and spatial frequency across reference, photopic and C/d/F calculations. Numerical convergence describes sampling, not measured production performance.
