## Optical Architecture

The source has 22 powered elements in 17 contiguous air-separated components. The zoom-group element counts are 7+4+3+8. Component counts are not the number of mechanically moving zoom groups. Cemented interfaces are source surfaces 10, 19, 24, 30, 35. The source stop is surface 26, renamed STO in the data file. The source rear PP remains a 2.000 mm plate with nd=1.51633 and νd=64.14, 5.000 mm air before and 30.658 mm after. BF=36.976 mm is the lens-only/air-equivalent datum, not the physical after-plate gap. The 22/17 product count excludes this camera-side plate.

Every published zoom position is contained in this single model:

- WIDE: f=51.501 mm, F/2.750, full field=32.2 degrees; D13=14.039 mm, D20=22.245 mm, D25=13.006 mm
- TELE: f=134.933 mm, F/2.754, full field=12.0 degrees; D13=44.630 mm, D20=1.243 mm, D25=3.417 mm

The model has 35 authored zoom samples, of which only the source's 2 stations are marked published. Additional rows are an inferred paraxial cam: the source track and residual defocus are interpolated, target EFL follows the calculated source endpoints, and the remaining zoom gaps are solved. The slider interpolates these samples piecewise-linearly. This is not a recovered production zoom cam. Adaptive knot placement limits added sampled longitudinal error to 1.22 λd N², with λd=587.5618 nm and a 2% numerical margin, separately from literal source residuals. This engineering budget matches the corrected Minolta models: the added geometric defocus disk is at most half the d-line Airy-disk diameter. It is not a manufacturing tolerance or factory performance claim. Geometry and exact-ray behavior between samples remain explicit validation gates.

## Glass Identification

The patent supplies d-line indices and Abbe numbers but no catalog glass names, melt data or partial dispersion. All material labels therefore remain unmatched. C/F/g behavior uses the engine's Abbe-based approximation; the high-Abbe positional matches do not prove a particular fluorophosphate formulation, fluorite, APO behavior or factory chromatic performance.

## Focus Mechanism

Internal focus by G1b/L14 (surfaces 7–8), moving imageward toward reconstructed near focus at 1.2 m, and objectward toward infinity. D6 grows and D8 shrinks; D6+D8 and glass thickness stay fixed. Other groups, stop and image plane remain fixed at a given zoom setting. Patent publishes infinity zoom rows only. Numeric focus travel is a reconstruction, not a factory cam; macro is not modeled. The target normal-focus distances are product context; their numerical L14 spacings were solved from the literal prescription, not copied from a patent. The full L14 group translates rigidly and the adjacent-gap sum remains fixed. No inferred row is marked published and no finite conjugate is certified.

## Aperture and Rim Inference

The patent places the stop axially but expressly does not specify its size or shape. The model derives the iris from the source design F-number at each zoom position. The initial unvignetted paraxial field envelope exceeded physical source-glass clearance and was rejected. Lens clear semi-diameters are now inferred from schematic group proportions and native geometric limits, then subject to the native cap, edge, gap, iris, hidden-trim and exact-ray checks. These are not factory rim or barrel measurements. Nine blades are product context, not a detailed blade geometry. For the 50–135, exact axial marginal rays require enlarged G2/G3 clear rims; the D15 gap needs 91.2881% sag intrusion at its necessary 11.93 mm shared rim. Default 90% and a 91% stage cannot preserve this nominal pupil. A 91.5% gap-sag allowance is the smallest tested sufficient stage, retaining positive physical clearance and all native checks. This existing allowance is retained unchanged from dense version3; no additional increase is made.

## Spherical Prescription and Validation Status

No aspheric coefficients are published for this embodiment.
