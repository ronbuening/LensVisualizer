Claim 1 and paragraph 0005 state that, in focusing from infinity to a near object, the first and second groups both move toward the object along the axis so that their separation decreases; paragraph 0010 ties condition (2) to the ratio of the two travels. The table on PDF page 4 marks d11 as the only variable gap and prints d12, from the stop to surface 13, as a fixed 4.3100, so the stop travels with the second group. PDF page 5 prints d11 = 6.81 at OBJ = ∞ and 3.08 at OBJ = 55, and the description of Fig. 5 gives the near magnification as 1:2.6. The patent prints OBJ = 55 without defining its reference plane; the text's "55 mm in front of the first surface" is the reading that reproduces 1:2.6 (1:2.58). No image distance is printed for the near state and no back focus for either state.

Glass. The prescription table has only r, d, n and ν columns (PDF pages 4–5), and paragraph 0016 defines n as the d-line index and ν as the Abbe number. The patent names no glass and gives no further line indices or partial dispersion, so L9 and L10 (1.60625 / 63.1) and the resin layers (1.51840 / 52.1) stay unmatched.

Engine values, the same before and after: focal length 27.106 mm, F/1.86, stop radius 13.508 mm, paraxial half-field estimate 40.41° limited by surface 4, traced corner coverage 100 % (21.65 mm), no undersized surface in the image-circle check, aperture check within 3 % of F/1.86 with no rim clip, validator clean. The chief ray reaches 21.63 mm at 38.88° against the patent's 38.6°.
