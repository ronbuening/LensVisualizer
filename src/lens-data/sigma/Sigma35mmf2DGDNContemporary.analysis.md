## Optical Architecture

Ten elements in nine air groups, arranged as a fixed positive front group G1, a negative focusing element G2 and a fixed positive rear group G3. The source gives group focal lengths31.17,−76.31 and95.57mm. The stop is source row12, after the front cemented pair.

The source absolute partial-dispersion ratios PgF0.5951 and0.5440 are distinct from the printed anomalous deviation0.0053. The latter uses PgF+0.0018νd−0.64833; it is converted through absolute PgF before using the engine normal line0.6438−0.001682νd. These values do not identify a supplier.
