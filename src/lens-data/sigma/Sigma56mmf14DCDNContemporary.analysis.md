**Filed:** 2018-08-24

**Applicant:** Sigma Corporation
**Title:** Imaging optical system (結像光学系)
**Example details:** Numerical Example 1, original pp. 15–16; Figure 1, p. 26; asphere conventions, p. 14.

This is an unscaled reconstruction of the specified patent example, a **related
variant**, not the proven factory prescription of the SIGMA 56mm f/1.4 DC DN |
Contemporary C018. The complete 31-page original publication is the numerical
source. Family metadata identifies JP7094550B2, but its original grant PDF was
not inspected and no A/B numerical equivalence is claimed.

The manufacturer's current official C018 page and construction diagram provide
several convergent but non-unique identification criteria:

1. Ten photographic elements in six air-separated components, matching E1.
2. Two front singletons, a front doublet, a negative focus singleton, a rear
   triplet and final doublet; the second element is biconvex and double-aspheric.
3. A final rear asphere, consistent with source S17.
4. Marketed 56 mm f/1.4, 50 cm minimum focus and 28.5° APS-C full field are
   compatible with, but not substituted for, source 53.95 mm f/1.46 and 28.62°.
5. The native 500.0002 mm object-to-image distance agrees closely with the
   rounded 500 mm source caption and manufacturer 500 mm minimum focus.

All seven source examples were compared. E2–E5 have nine elements and are not
construction matches. E6 has ten but a meniscus L12 and much thicker L34.
**E7 remains a close viable alternative** with the same ten/six topology and
biconvex L12. The supplied E1 attribution is retained without claiming that the
maker drawing uniquely selects it. Current mount metadata describes available
C018 products, not verification of identical internal prescriptions or camera
stacks for every mount. The source 28.4 mm image circle is retained independently
of smaller MFT or Canon crop formats.

## Optical Architecture

The source describes positive G1, stop, negative G2 and positive G3 in order.
G1, the stop and G3 are fixed to the image plane in the mechanism description;
only the single negative G2 moves imageward for close focus. The design reduces
the moving optical mass to one element. This is an internal-focusing medium
telephoto architecture, not unit focusing or a reconstructed zoom.

G1 spans S1–S7 with published group power f = +43.50 mm. G2 spans S9–S10,
f = −37.32 mm. G3 spans S11–S17, f = +42.42 mm; its triplet and doublet
subassemblies have published isolated focal lengths +102.19 and +60.65 mm.
The six air-separated lens components should not be confused with these three
motion groups. The seventeen lens surfaces include explicit plane stop S8.

The source also lists camera-side plane filter F at S18–S19: actual thickness
2.0000 mm, nd 1.51680, νd 64.20, preceded by 14.5792 mm air and followed by
1.0000 mm air. It is modeled as a rear plate, excluded from the ten photographic
elements and not replaced by air-equivalent thickness. The patent does not
identify a particular commercial camera stack or a filter clear aperture.

## Element-by-Element Analysis

Element focal lengths below are isolated thick-element d-line paraxial values
in air. They are not additive powers for cemented assemblies. Role descriptions
are positional optical interpretations, not measured allocations of aberration
correction or assertions about manufacturing technique.

### L11 — Positive Meniscus

nd = 1.94595, νd = 17.98. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = +130.49 mm.

The front positive meniscus has the highest index and lowest Abbe number in G1. It collects the large axial bundle and participates in the positive front group. The source publishes its PgF; this is evidence for partial dispersion, not a vendor glass identity.

### L12 — Biconvex Positive (Asph)

nd = 1.55332, νd = 71.68. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = +67.82 mm.

The second, positive biconvex element has two independently specified aspheric faces and a high Abbe number. Its location and shape match the manufacturer drawing, but also occur in E7. The weakly curved negative rear radius is preserved rather than flattened to a plane.

### L13 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.85478, νd = 24.80. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = -28.31 mm.

This negative meniscus is the front member of doublet D1. It shares cemented S6 with L14 and introduces lower-Abbe material opposite a positive, higher-Abbe partner. No separate air space is added at the common interface.

### L14 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.72916, νd = 54.67. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = +26.73 mm.

The positive rear member of D1 completes G1. Its source partial-dispersion deviation is negative relative to the stated normal line. The large thickness and contrasting index at S6 are source data, not inferred glass substitutions.

### L21 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.56384, νd = 60.67. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = -37.32 mm.

The negative singleton is the entire moving focus group G2. It lies after the stop and has a strongly curved rear face. Its imageward travel changes both adjacent air gaps; it is not translated together with the stop or rear group.

### L31 — Negative Meniscus

nd = 1.64769, νd = 33.84. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = -154.98 mm.

The first, negative member of rear triplet T1 shares S12 with L32. It supplies opposing power within an overall positive cemented component. Its glass identity and anomalous dispersion beyond nd and Abbe number are unresolved.

### L32 — Biconvex Positive

nd = 1.90043, νd = 37.37. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = +17.12 mm.

This positive biconvex central member of T1 has high index and is cemented on both faces to negative members. The topology is source-backed; a particular chromatic correction budget is not independently established.

### L33 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.59270, νd = 35.44. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = -21.35 mm.

The final negative member of T1 shares S13 and ends at S14. The three members have distinct published glass constants. The small figure-inferred triplet rim is important to off-axis clipping and is not a factory-vignetting measurement.

### L34 — Biconcave Negative

nd = 1.51742, νd = 52.15. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = -35.45 mm.

This thin negative member begins final doublet D2 at S15 and shares S16 with L35. Its curvature and thickness are important discriminants against the thicker corresponding member of E6, but do not uniquely exclude E7.

### L35 — Biconvex Positive (Asph)

nd = 1.77250, νd = 49.50. Glass: Unmatched (source constants only). f = +22.69 mm.

The positive rear member of D2 terminates in aspheric S17. It supplies positive rear power and additional surface-shape freedom near the image-side field bundles. No resin layer or specific asphere manufacturing method is inferred from the equation alone.

## Glass Identification and Selection

Every glass remains Unmatched. Rounded nd/νd coordinates are not sufficient to
establish an exact catalog glass, supplier or melt. The patent references the
d line at 587.56 nm and publishes PgF for only the three positive G1 elements.
Its normal line gives ΔPgF = PgF − 0.6483 + 0.0018 νd. These three derived
values are authored directly; missing values are not manufactured as zeros.

| L12     | 1.55332 | 71.68 |        0.5402 |        0.020924 |
| L13     | 1.85478 | 24.80 | not published | not established |

The mean positive-G1 ΔPgF is +0.0182313, reproducing the source 0.0182.
Positive/negative cemented combinations provide chromatic-design freedom;
no apochromatic performance is claimed. Mixed-spectrum simulation is permitted
by the pinned engine but uses approximate dispersion where only nd/νd are
available. It is not equivalent to complete measured spectral curves.
The engine's generic mixed-spectrum warning describes all eleven optical media
as nd/Abbe-only; the raw warning is retained, but three lens media do carry the
source-derived partial-dispersion deviations above. No full curve is implied.

## Focus Mechanism

The negative singleton G2 travels 5.5189 mm imageward. Source d10 decreases by
5.5188 mm, leaving a 0.0001 mm rounding difference despite fixed-G3 prose.
Both printed endpoints are preserved; that small discrepancy is not redistributed.

| Quantity                             | Infinity | Native finite |
| ------------------------------------ | -------: | ------------: |
| d8: stop to G2 (mm)                  |   2.7902 |        8.3091 |
| d10: G2 to G3 (mm)                   |  11.3404 |        5.8216 |
| Physical first surface to image (mm) |  74.9278 |       74.9279 |
| Object to first surface (mm)         | infinity |      425.0723 |
| Object to image (mm)                 | infinity |      500.0002 |
| Source EFL (mm)                      |    53.95 |         52.16 |
| Source F-number                      |     1.46 |          1.62 |

The exact finite conjugate uses the first-surface reference and literal d0,
not the rounded 500 mm caption in the wrong reference frame. The fixed source
image planes retain paraxial closure residuals −0.000185793 and −0.000219283 mm.
The model does not translate the image plane to erase them.

No factory intermediate cam was recovered. The two-point linear trial misses
the illustrative slider-0.5 image plane by −0.478832 mm. The candidate instead
uses 65 inferred controls, solving a scalar common path parameter for the two
gaps while preserving their native endpoints and an interpolation of the native
residual. Only the endpoints are published; the near endpoint has an explicit
finite-conjugate declaration. Intermediate states are not certified for MTF.

Across 1,025 samples, the inferred path has maximum absolute paraxial plane
residual 0.000384470 mm. This is not an all-focus object-distance accuracy bound:
the first nonzero sample has 3.4858% label error. Near infinity, the rounded
prescription has a finite algebraic conjugate around 15.665 km while the
illustrative label diverges. Relative error therefore is not uniformly small.
The manufacturer actuator or firmware cam is not recovered by this interpolation.

## Aspherical Surfaces

The original equation uses the standard (1+K) conic convention and even powers.
K = 0 is explicitly printed on all three aspheres; it is not an omitted default.
A4 through A12 are preserved at the original scale, including every A12 term.

source-plane refocus, or fabrication-process assumption was introduced.

## Aperture, Rim and Field Qualifications

The source numerical tables do not supply clear semi-diameters. Inferred rim
seeds were measured from Figure 1 using the actual 57.3486 mm first-to-last
lens-vertex separation. A 400 dpi crop gives 584 pixels for that separation.
The measured clear-curve extents were rounded to 0.1 mm; approximately 0.2 mm
reading uncertainty and unknown actual mechanical/optical boundaries remain.
All elements use square inferred front/rear rims. No gap-sag override is used.

The ordinary-prime contract derives the iris from source infinity F1.46.
The resulting fixed radius is 12.808333614 mm. Real on-axis image-space working
F = 1/(2 NA) is 1.460420 at infinity and 1.614972 at native finite distance.
The latter differs by −0.005028 from printed1.62; it does not establish an
alternative aperture convention or source error. No iris is tuned to force
agreement. The manufacturer's nine-blade count is retained; scalar MTF uses a
circular iris rather than exact mechanical blade geometry.

Independent source-height chief roots at five fields per endpoint all hard-trace
through the inferred rims. Infinity full chief launch angle28.617985° closely
matches printed28.62°. Finite launch-slope full angle25.048644° matches the
printed25.05° approximately, but the first-vertex object-height angle is26.558263°.
Those finite angle definitions differ and must not be presented as one quantity.
The engine reports the latter convention. Source image height14.20 mm is retained.

The independent bounded iris harness requested1,220 rays. Results:1,058 passed,
88 hard-clipped,43 unlaunched aiming failures and31 post-aim noBracket failures.
There were no chief-root failures. On-axis fulliris sample61/61 passed at both
endpoints; all610 stoppedF8 samples passed. This is discrete sampling, not a
continuous full-pupil proof. Hard losses occurred mainly at frontS1 and rear
tripletS11; inferred rims limit how much this says about an actual lens.
Unlaunched and numerical failures are never recast as physical clips.

## Verification Summary

Independent original-pixel source review passed264 comparisons plus7structural
assertions. These comparisons include missing cells, flags and topology, not264
unique numeric source cells. A separate high-precision scalar calculation
reproduced EFL53.948012/52.159801 mm and the five published group powers.
Bounded pinned-runtime geometry passed1,025 sampled states with no hidden trim.

Reference-line diffraction MTF at three fields for each of six endpoint/aperture/
plane cases converged numerically. C/d/F and photopic native-plane MTF at three
fields for each of eight endpoint/aperture/spectrum cases also converged.
These results concern the authored prescription and inferred apertures, not
measured production quality. Sparse independent ray failures remain recorded.

0.0540/0.3146 at half field and0.1901/0.1395 at the edge. Photopic edge40 lp/mm

camera corrections for distortion and peripheral illumination; those corrected
