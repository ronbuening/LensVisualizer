## Optical Architecture

The source is a positive–negative–positive–negative four-group macro design. It contains thirteen spherical glass elements in ten air-separated components. The three cemented pairs span S5–S7, S12–S14 and S18–S20. S22–S23 and S24–S25 are separate rear singlets with a 2.5415 mm air gap. An earlier research description calling the final elements a cemented pair was inconsistent with the literal table and is not adopted.

The primary aperture is source row 15; the additional fixed aperture is row 21. Both axial positions and the intervening air distances are retained. Their radii and all lens clear apertures are inferred, not printed dimensions. No camera-side plate is prescribed or added.

## Glass Identification and Selection

The source lists ND/Vd, but the inspected definition does not establish a numerical reference wavelength. The runtime uses an explicitly assumed helium-d convention. No catalog names, partial dispersions, C/F/g indices or vendor formulas have been invented. Abbe-only dispersion estimates cannot establish factory secondary-spectrum or APO performance. Reference-wavelength MTF is separate from any broadband capability.

## Focus Mechanism

The patent moves groups 2 and 3 in opposite directions while its end groups remain nominally fixed. All three discrete source gap sets are preserved separately. The finite-state independent paraxial image distances differ from the printed BF by +0.418927 and +0.408546 mm; total source tracks also change by +0.0165 and +0.1151 mm. These discrepancies remain unresolved. Neither the source values nor the image plane is retuned to hide them. The maker's 0.46 m minimum and life-size claim are not modeled finite endpoints.

The runtime is deliberately infinity-only: no variable gaps, no interpolated cam law and no finite-conjugate validation. Its required close-focus scalar of 1e9 m is an unvalidated infinity sentinel, not a recovered near endpoint. Pinned-baseline precedents include KinoptikTegea57mmf18 (1e6) and PentaxM28mmf28 (1e15). Generic focus-slider or fallback labels must not be interpreted as validated source states.

## Model Qualification and Verification

Source infinity paraxial EFL is 178.653815287 mm and BF 54.141397648 mm, matching printed 178.6538 and 54.1414. The authored image plane is 220.6623 mm from the first vertex. Source design aperture is f/3.6069, not the marketed f/3.5. Paragraph 0001 describes approximately 14° total field, so a 7° half-field diagnostic is approximate rather than an exact tabulated field.

Clear rims are figure-guided modeling hypotheses, adjusted only to satisfy physical nonintersection and cross-gap geometry. Initial failures remain in the separate build evidence. No source optical prescription was retuned. Bounded static checks pass with no gap-sag override. The bounded native pilot now passes source-row roundtrip, zero render trim, iris clearance, official iris-limited aperture and sampled reference rays. Native EFL is 178.653815287 mm. Full-field pupil throughput in the bounded grid is 33.65%, with no failed or unaimed rays; inferred rims cause appreciable field vignetting. The separate nonlinear EFL/entrance-beam-diameter diagnostic is 3.684781, not the official paraxial aperture census. Reference-only optical-quality MTF is now complete with the limitations below. Integration outcomes are recorded in the accompanying package validation manifest. Local visible registration is not deployment; focused lens/catalog/render coverage is not the entire repository test corpus.

### Reference-wavelength optical quality

The approximate source 7° half-field produces chief height 22.082073 mm, beyond the declared format radius 21.65 mm (fraction 1.019957). The exact-angle source test therefore fails its range assertion before MTF. This failure is preserved; the angle is never silently clamped and no claim of completed 7° MTF is made.

A separate exact-format-corner set uses angles 0°, 3.433225° and 6.866423°, solving the outer chief height against the real declared radius. All twelve combinations of field, geometric/diffraction method and design/best-axial focus converge through 40 lp/mm at grid cap 128. All completed fields have zero failed and unaimed rays.

At the unchanged source plane, reference diffraction MTF at 40 lp/mm is approximately 0.2542 on axis and 0.5360/0.6264 sagittal/tangential at the actual format corner. The separate best-axial diagnostic shift is +0.083314 mm; it gives axial diffraction MTF 0.8491 at 40 lp/mm. This shift is not written into the prescription, and the literal image plane's paraxial residual remains −0.00000235 mm. Reference MTF uses the explicitly assumed d convention. Broadband MTF is blocked by missing partial-dispersion evidence for low-dispersion glasses; no synthetic partial dispersions or factory APO claims are added.
