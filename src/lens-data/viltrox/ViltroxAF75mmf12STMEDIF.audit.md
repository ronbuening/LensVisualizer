### Prescription, labels and source conflicts

- All active radii and spacings agree with Table 1 after the existing 0.9922588071791335 normalization, except the previously disclosed S10 reconstruction and solved image plane. Glass indices and Abbe numbers agree with the table for all sixteen elements.
- L16 is explicitly present in ¶0012 and Figure 1. Its Table 1 surfaces are S27/S28, R = +34.056/+37.660 mm and center thickness 0.51 mm before scaling. GL is a separate auxiliary plate, not L16. Deleting L16 would corrupt the element count and prescription.
- L16's Figure 1 thickness and nearly flat rear face disagree with Table 1. The table-derived element remains a thin meniscus: normalized center thickness 0.506052 mm, edge thickness approximately 0.230334 mm at SD 13.2 mm. Its inspector role now states the discrepancy.
- Additional figure/table conflicts occur at L12's plane front and thin center, L13's rear curvature, L15's rear curvature and GL. Numerical geometry takes precedence; no unsupported sign flips, thickness changes or element reassignments were applied. The analysis identifies each conflict instead of claiming silhouette agreement.
- The display name, 16/11 count, five cemented pairs and L1–L16 labels are retained. Added “RECONSTRUCTED PRESCRIPTION” to the displayed specifications. All elements are spherical; no asphere or APD tags are justified.
- Only L9 moves, monotonically imageward by 10.877152366 mm, with D1+D2 conserved and G1/G2 fixed. The 0.88 m endpoint remains explicitly reconstructed. This prime has no zoom schedule.

### Glass and aperture interpretation

The ten resolved elements retain compatible catalog proxies. L5/L11, L8/L12 and L16 have no compatible catalog candidates under the existing index/Abbe guard. L9 retains its ordinary-crown Abbe fallback: multiple nearby curves do not establish a unique assignment. No supplier, melt or anomalous-partial-dispersion identity was inferred from the rounded table coordinates.

The authored STO SD remains 11.722108147 mm. Corrected its description from a verified physical iris to a paraxial reference: the runtime exact-marginal calibration yields approximately 13.246 mm. Finite-aperture clipping remains part of the numerical reconstruction. Removed the analysis claims that the entire default bundle passes or that these apertures validate the production silhouette/performance.
