## How to use this index

- Reopen only for changed source evidence, a different configuration, or a reproducible defect. A repeated generated queue entry alone does not justify another whole-lens pass.
- Follow the audit for native figure resolution, uncertainty, adopted dimensions, rejected trials, source errors and retained focus/field/shift constraints. Beam clearance, source fidelity and production correlation are separate conclusions.
- Every completed entry includes concave ends and glass, even when no numerical cut, outer shoulder or catalog curve is supported. Actual printed spectral indices and partials remain distinct from catalog proxies.
- Canonical parity establishes unchanged authored source coordinates; it does not imply historical engine or inferred-iris runtime parity. Current runtime checks use the present engine and record changed conventions separately. Runtime focus-control sweeps follow the engine’s declared launch model. Where source finite conjugates are absent, collimated state probes and Gaussian or positive-object supplements are separate checks; a sampled control does not certify a physical finite-focus cam. Finite object-field fractions may be paraxial scalings rather than inverse-solved image-height targets; the linked audit states that convention and any actual chief heights. Linked audits record the launch scope and any unseeded or virtual-object states.
- Laowa 180mm manual-focus and AF configurations share the explicitly identified source audit and analysis; their separate focus trajectories were reviewed.
- “Prior reviewed” identifies completed Z/F coverage outside the current Canon cohort. “Reused for Canon mounts” extends unchanged prior coverage to the listed Canon mounts. Prior reviews outside the earlier Z/F cohort may also be reused with their actual dates when unchanged optical content is verified. Pending and blocked entries have not completed the requested review; an existing older audit link alone does not establish completion.
